# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Monday, 9 November 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Microsoft is splitting the Copilot meeting policy into two separate controls — **MeetingAI** and **TranscriptionWithMeetingAI** — giving admins distinct "who can manage AI," "who can use AI," and "start transcription with AI" settings, with existing configurations mapped across automatically.
- With Exchange Web Services (EWS) being switched off in Exchange Online from October 2026, Teams Rooms on Android, Teams phones, and Teams panels running app versions older than February 2026's minimum release may lose calendar features — IT teams should check device versions now.
- Two simultaneous screen shares (on by default) are rolling out late November to mid-December, with Teams Rooms on Windows (Pro) showing both shares side-by-side in a dedicated layout.
- Teams Rooms on Android (Pro-licensed) is getting private presenter/organiser chat in green-room meetings and events, rolling out mid-to-late November, alongside fixes for consoles stuck "connecting."

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- Starting today, November 9, 2026, Microsoft is introducing fair use limits for additional Copilot Chat features and capabilities for larger organizations (over 2,000 M365 seats), following the same rollout already applied to smaller tenants on November 2.
- In the Microsoft 365 admin center, Copilot > Search > Authoritative content now lets admins add and manage up to 100 SharePoint sites as a centralized governance layer for Copilot Search results.
- From early November 2026, usage-based billing (UBB) models are rolling out in Copilot Chat and Copilot in Word, Excel and PowerPoint, requiring a Copilot licence, UBB enabled, and allocated Copilot Credits.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- A new SharePoint list agent lets Copilot users create Microsoft Lists through natural-language prompts, transforming structured information and Copilot responses directly into Lists.
- If any part of your organisation is in Canada, note that permanent daylight saving from 1 November means Teams Android devices need a firmware update or a temporary time-zone setting in the portal.
- Power Platform's Release Planner is winding down — content is migrating to the AI at Work Roadmap, with the Learn-based Release Planner fully retiring by 15 November 2026.

## Meetingroom Equipment
- AVer has entered the room endpoint market with its CORE500 Room Kit, certified for Microsoft Teams Rooms on Windows, combining the UC500 Mini PC and TC500 Touch Console into a complete meeting room system; sample units are available now, with Americas GA expected Q1 2027.
- Separately, AVer's MT300N has achieved Microsoft Teams certification, meeting performance and compatibility requirements for qualified Teams Rooms deployments.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- Cisco used WebexOne 2026 to announce RoomOS 27, which will let Cisco room devices run Webex, Microsoft Teams, Zoom and Google Meet when it ships this November, deepening the multi-platform-in-one-room trend.
- Zoom has expanded its Zoom for Government platform, bringing more of the Zoom Customer Experience suite plus newly authorized capabilities to U.S. federal agencies.
- Slack continues rolling out governance-focused AI controls, including regional AI inference options for Enterprise+ customers starting with Japan.
