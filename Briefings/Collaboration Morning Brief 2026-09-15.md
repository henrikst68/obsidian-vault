# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Tuesday, 15 September 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Microsoft confirmed it has been recognized as a Leader in the 2026 Gartner Magic Quadrant for Unified Communications as a Service — its eighth consecutive year with this recognition, reinforcing Teams' UCaaS leadership position.
- A dedicated **Meeting Recap App** is rolling out this month Microsoft Teams Is Getting a Dedicated Meeting Recap App This Month, with guidance on what IT teams need to do before it arrives; the app lets users review past meetings in one place, choose from different recap styles like audio and video, and quickly filter recaps to find the ones that matter most.
- Security teams should note ongoing abuse of Teams as an attack vector: threat actors are impersonating IT support staff in Microsoft Teams calls to gain remote access to corporate devices and deploy Chaos ransomware in attacks targeting North American organizations, alongside a new malware family dubbed SynkLoader being distributed in Microsoft Teams phishing campaigns to steal credentials via a fake lock screen.
- Microsoft is also rolling out a new **Efficiency Mode** and meeting protection controls: a new Efficiency Mode for Teams for systems with limited CPU and memory resources to improve app responsiveness, plus a Teams meeting protection policy that allows admins to automatically block all identified external bots from joining meetings.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- Copilot Chat gained shareable outputs and richer answer cards this month: you can now share an individual response or a whole chat session — grab the share dialog, copy a link, and paste it into a Teams chat or email, and new model-driven answer cards dynamically surface things like weather, sports, finance, images, video, places, and news right in the response.
- **Copilot Cowork** got a UX refresh: the left navigation now says Automations instead of Scheduled, the model picker moved into the prompting box with GPT and Claude sections, and a new reasoning-effort slider lets users dial between a faster/cheaper response or a slower, smarter pass.
- Governance is expanding for enterprise agents: multi-tenant agent management in the Microsoft 365 admin center is now in public preview, enabling admins to view and manage agents across the tenants they govern from a single experience.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- LinkedIn Hiring Assistant is now integrated in Teams, helping streamline candidate review and hiring manager collaboration, and users can find chats, files, and other content by asking questions in natural language instead of searching exact keywords.
- Partners can now publish vetted agents at scale: customers can submit agents built in Agent Builder to the Agent Store under a "Built by your org" section after admin review, enabling organizations to share validated agents while maintaining governance.
- On the Teams Rooms/Phone side, Queues app for Microsoft Teams turns an existing call queue into a collaborative workspace where reps and supervisors work the same customer conversations together in Teams.

## Meetingroom Equipment
- Poly and Yealink both made major 2026 hardware pushes: Poly added Mission and Focus 6 headsets plus new room compute, while Yealink expanded its Teams room architecture, MP E2 phone roadmap, and YMCS integration with Microsoft Places.
- HP/Poly's roadmap centers on Studio Room Compute, VideoOS 5.0 and the forthcoming 5.1 release, Poly Studio V12, Poly Lens, WXP, Focus 6, Mission headsets, and its existing CCX Teams phones, while Yealink's breadth spans MeetingBar, MVC S80, MVC S98, SmartVision 80, MeetingBoard Pro, MTower, Teams-certified phones, Teams-certified headsets, and ProAV-style room builds.
- Teams Rooms Pro licensing (~$40/room/month) adds Pro Portal management, intelligent speaker, Microsoft Whiteboard, front-row layout, and Copilot in Teams Rooms — relevant for any room refresh budgeting.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- Salesforce is bundling Slack deeper into its core offering: new Agentforce editions bundle Slack, Tableau Next, the Premier Success Plan, enterprise-grade security, and out-of-the-box AI into every edition, and these editions include Slack, bringing humans and agents into one conversational interface to drive work forward.
- Google is closing cross-platform meeting friction: Google Calendar updates make it easier to join third-party video meetings, including Microsoft Teams, Zoom, and Cisco Webex, when collaborating across different calendar and email clients, with outgoing invites now automatically including the conferencing URL in the event's Location field for external recipients.
- Analysts continue to frame a five-way enterprise messaging split: Slack leads for engineering and ChatOps, Teams dominates M365-standardized enterprises, Webex is the default for Cisco-stack and government organizations, Zoom Team Chat extends from the video-first Zoom Workplace bundle, and Google Chat is the default for Google Workspace organizations.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- **Security:** Brief IT/security teams on the Teams-vector attacks (Chaos ransomware via fake IT-support calls, SynkLoader phishing) — consider reinforcing user awareness training this week.
- **Rollout prep:** Coordinate with IT admins ahead of the Meeting Recap App and Efficiency Mode rollouts landing this month; review Copilot Cowork's new reasoning-effort slider for potential cost/performance tuning.
- **Vendor watch:** If a Teams Rooms refresh is on the roadmap, track Poly's VideoOS 5.1 release and Yealink's Microsoft Places integration before committing to hardware.
