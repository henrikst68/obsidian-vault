# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Wednesday, 11 November 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- A new rAVe [PUBS] column (10 Nov) argues that Microsoft's Device Ecosystem Platform (MDEP) remains important for Microsoft Teams Rooms, but its broader influence on the video collaboration market is much more limited than many expected, and is not a shared foundation under Zoom, Google Meet and Webex as well.
- Ahead of Ignite, UC Today notes Microsoft has stopped disclosing paid Copilot seat counts, making the event the value test... Buyers want pricing, Teams and agent control, not a feature parade.
- Certified AOSP devices are emerging as a wildcard — the same analysis notes certified AOSP devices can run multiple video-conferencing solutions, so rooms can be repurposed without replacing equipment, a potential long-term threat to Teams Rooms hardware lock-in.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- Fair-use limits for Copilot Chat without a full Copilot licence are now live for larger tenants: beginning November 9, 2026, for larger organizations, Microsoft is introducing fair use limits for additional Copilot Chat features and capabilities (smaller orgs started Nov 2).
- Copilot is rebranding its workspace tooling — Copilot Notebooks is now Copilot Workspaces, a shared place for teams to work with Copilot.
- Usage-based billing (UBB) is rolling into core apps: from early November 2026, usage-based billing (UBB) models are rolling out in Copilot Chat and Copilot in Word, Excel and PowerPoint... users need a Microsoft 365 Copilot licence, UBB enabled and Copilot Credits allocated by an admin.
- Microsoft's November Copilot update brings "Autopilot" (a computer-use agent) and a redesigned Home experience; if you have used OpenAI's or Anthropic's computer-use agents, this is Microsoft's version, built into the 365 world, with Skills in Copilot Chat roll[ing] out to everyone in November 2026.
