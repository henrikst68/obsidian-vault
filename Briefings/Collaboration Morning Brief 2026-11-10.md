# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Tuesday, 10 November 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Teams Rooms now support **Cross-Platform Meeting Connect with Zoom**, enabling HDMI share, roster, read-only chat, synced mute/end-call and 1080p video when joining Zoom meetings from Teams Rooms hardware.
- Teams is rolling out **two simultaneous screen shares** (on by default) between late November and mid-December, alongside a redesigned meeting control bar with Leave isolated on the right and separate recording/transcription buttons.
- Admin management of SIP phones, Teams Rooms on Android and panels is moving out of the Teams Admin Centre between mid-to-late November, with phones following by 15 December, and GCC High/DoD environments transitioning by the same date.
- Organisations with Canada-based users need a firmware update or temporary time-zone setting, since permanent daylight saving from 1 November means Teams Android devices need attention and Shifts needs a closest-city check. Separately, Access4 has added Microsoft Teams calling into SASBOSS, its vendor-agnostic platform, via its Microsoft-certified Operator Connect service called UCTeams.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- Beginning November 9, 2026, for larger organizations (over 2,000 seats), Microsoft is introducing fair use limits for additional Copilot Chat features and capabilities, though Copilot Chat remains included in Microsoft 365 subscriptions with no admin action required.
- Copilot's November refresh brings a new Home screen with Chat and Cowork side by side, reusable Skills written in plain English, Automations that run tasks on a schedule, and early access to Autopilot — a cloud agent with its own mailbox, files and computer.
- From early November 2026, usage-based billing models are rolling out in Copilot Chat and Copilot in Word, Excel and PowerPoint, requiring a Microsoft 365 Copilot licence, UBB enabled and allocated Copilot Credits.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- Zendesk's employee service AI agents started rolling out on 3 November, bringing AI-powered support to the help centre, Slack and Microsoft Teams, while Box launched Box Architect on 5 November to build and optimise agentic workflows across enterprise content.
- Google Workspace pushed a weekly update wave on November 9, including a gradual rollout of skills across Workspace Studio starting November 9, 2026 and quick confirmation of system-applied classification labels in Drive, Docs, Sheets and Slides.
- Barco shipped new Teams Rooms on Android firmware for its ClickShare Hub Core (C-100RS and C-300RS) devices, released November 4, 2026, bundling updated Teams client, Intune and Authenticator components.

## Meetingroom Equipment
- The Barco ClickShare Hub Core firmware update (Nov 4) refreshes the embedded Teams client and Microsoft Intune/Authenticator stack for Android-based Teams Rooms.
- IT teams running legacy room hardware should note the broader context: Microsoft ended certification for Teams Rooms devices running Android 10 and 11 on September 3, 2026, making the upcoming Admin Centre device-management transition (by 15 December) a good trigger to audit room estates.
- Nothing significant beyond Barco's firmware drop in the last 48 hours specifically from other MTR hardware vendors (Yealink, Poly, Logitech, Neat).

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- Cisco's WebexOne 2026 wrap-up continues to reverberate: Cisco put AI agents into Webex meetings, its contact centre and room devices, and opened those rooms to Google Meet, with Cisco RoomOS 27 scheduled for availability in November 2026, introducing enhancements across AI, interoperability and workplace management.
- Webex Translator Agent, enabling real-time speech translation in ten languages, moves to general availability in November 2026, and Cisco collaboration devices will support native Google Meet experiences alongside Webex, Microsoft Teams and Zoom through a new Google partnership.
- Zoom's Workplace app continues iterative UX changes, moving share permissions like "Who can share?" to the top level and consolidating "Allow all participants to" controls out of the nested Host tools menu.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- **Licence audit:** With Copilot Chat fair-use limits now active for large tenants (Nov 9) and usage-based billing rolling out in Word/Excel/PowerPoint, confirm Copilot Credit allocation before users hit caps.
- **Device management deadline:** Flag the 15 December cutover of SIP phones, Rooms-on-Android and panel management out of the classic Teams Admin Centre — especially relevant for GCC High/DoD estates.
- **Competitive watch:** Cisco's Google Meet interoperability push on Webex room hardware could reshape multi-platform room strategy — worth monitoring if Henrik's estate mixes Teams and Webex rooms.
