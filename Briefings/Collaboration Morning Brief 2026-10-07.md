# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Wednesday, 7 October 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Microsoft's "What's New in Teams" blog is shifting from a monthly to a quarterly rhythm, with the next edition due in December; this edition highlighted a new **Video Recap** feature that turns a meeting you missed into a short, narrated highlight reel.
- A new **Security Detection Report** is rolling out to the Teams admin center, bringing detections such as impersonation and malicious URLs into one view so admins see the whole picture in one place.
- From early October, images containing QR codes from external users will be obscured in Teams messages by default, requiring users to deliberately reveal them before viewing or scanning — a direct response to rising QR-based phishing.
- Some users reported a Teams sign-in/connectivity disruption on 6 October, with DownDetector report volume first surging around 5:45AM Eastern Time; no official Microsoft post-incident report yet.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- Microsoft 365 Copilot's latest release (6 Oct) adds a **Regenerate response** option, letting users quickly get an alternative response to their latest prompt using options like Try Again or Switch Model.
- A broader Copilot overhaul (message center update, last updated 5 Oct) introduces a unified **Home** screen merging Chat and Cowork, plus the ability for Chat to complete long-running, multi-step knowledge work including drafting email, managing meetings, working in Teams, and managing files, with sensitive actions requiring approval.
- Inside Teams specifically, admins can now discover and enable third-party Teams agents from the admin center, with recommendations and required-config visibility, and GitHub Copilot is joining as an in-chat agent, both reaching GA this month.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- **Teams Phone Agent** — the AI calling receptionist — is now generally available to all customers as of 30 September, per Microsoft Learn's latest update.
- **Copilot in SharePoint** has reached general availability, letting every user create and organize content, ask questions grounded in their SharePoint data, enrich libraries with AI-generated metadata, and build reusable skills, plus new plugin support for connecting to systems such as GitHub, CRM systems, publishing platforms, and internal tools.
- Teams Live Chat (the website chat widget) is being retired, with Microsoft directing customers to Dynamics 365 Contact Center or Copilot Studio instead.

## Meetingroom Equipment
- Nothing significant new in the last 48 hours — the certified-vendor landscape (Logitech, Poly/HP, Yealink, Neat, Crestron, Cisco) remains stable with no fresh product announcements this week.
- Background context for Henrik: comparative guides continue to note Logitech, Poly, and Yealink together account for over 80% of certified Microsoft Teams Rooms and Zoom Rooms hardware sold, useful if any MTR procurement decisions are pending.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- **Slack/Salesforce** rolled out a major platform update around 1–2 October, unveiling an "agentic work operating system" push with native MCP support and Claude Tag for Slack, enabling teams to add an AI assistant directly into Slack channels via an @Claude tag.
- **Zoom** continues to broaden ZoomMate, with analyst GlobalData noting Zoom is expanding the ZoomMate AI assistant with new models and languages alongside a redesigned Contact Centre workspace as part of its "system of action" strategy.
- **Webex** has pushed its October (46.10) app release from an earlier date to 12 October, per Cisco's release notes.
- **Google Workspace** is overhauling its Gemini assistant, replacing "Gems" with a new "Skills" framework, with rollout to Workspace beginning 5 October and completing by mid-November.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- Review the new **QR code obscuring** default and the **Agents in Teams admin center** rollout before GitHub Copilot and third-party agent recommendations start surfacing for end users — decide enablement policy now.
- If any websites still use the Teams Live Chat widget, plan migration to Dynamics 365 Contact Center or Copilot Studio ahead of the mid-October retirement.
- Monitor Slack's aggressive "agentic OS" positioning (Claude Tag, MCP) as a competitive signal — worth a quick comparison note against Copilot's own agent strategy in Teams.
