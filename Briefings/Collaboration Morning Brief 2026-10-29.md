# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Thursday, 29 October 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- **Phishing crackdown incoming:** Microsoft confirmed Teams will soon use AI to watch for phishing and other scams, with AI-powered anti-phishing tools arriving in November, targeting "first contact chats" with outsiders following a wave of attacks.
- **Cross-Platform Meeting Connect unveiled:** Microsoft's Teams blog introduced Cross-Platform Meeting Connect, building on Direct Guest Join, the feature that lets Teams Rooms join Zoom meetings and Zoom Rooms join Teams meetings, with no ship date, rollout schedule, or list of supported devices given — this is a roadmap announcement, not a release.
- **US service blip resolved:** Microsoft restored Microsoft Teams service after investigating an issue affecting some users in the United States on October 26, 2026; root cause/affected features were not disclosed.
- **Minimised meeting view rolling out:** A new compact meeting experience lets users raise their hand and send reactions without reopening the full meeting window, choosing between expanded and compact layouts, available to everyone by the end of October 2026.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- **"Today" feature entering preview:** Microsoft's new proactive assistant doesn't just surface work, it gets work done — drafts are written and ready to edit, schedule changes are proposed, and forgotten follow-ups wait to be sent — entering private preview in October, coming to Outlook and Teams as well.
- **Shared context for @Copilot in Teams:** Invoking @Copilot in Teams now gives the entire team the shared context and permissions of the channel, group chat or meeting.
- **Teams Phone Agent hits GA:** Microsoft's AI receptionist is now generally available, acting as an AI receptionist that sits in front of call queues, answers questions from knowledge bases and files, books appointments, transfers with AI notes, and works in 60+ languages and after hours, billed via Copilot Credits.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- **Azure Communication Services winding down:** Azure Communication Services, Microsoft's CPaaS play competing with Twilio, is retiring as a standalone product, with no new sign-ups from 23 October 2026.
- **Deepfake detection coming to meetings:** Microsoft flagged that deepfake detection is coming to Teams meetings as part of its broader October enterprise update.
- **Partner Center AI expansion:** New Dynamics 365-powered "Sales and Service Skills" are now reusable skills enabling sales and service expertise to be incorporated into Copilot, Cowork, and custom agents, generally available from October 8, 2026.

## Meetingroom Equipment
- **Logitech refresh:** New touch controllers Tap IP 2 can wake meeting-room displays, while Tap Scheduler 2 can turn its own display off when no one is nearby to reduce power consumption.
- **Shure named IDC leader:** Shure was recognised as a leader in AI-enabled conference room hardware, with its portfolio bringing audio, video, compute and ShureCloud device management together, supporting Microsoft Teams, Zoom, Google Meet and Cisco Webex.
- **Teams Rooms Android gets Town Hall control:** A new launch gives presenters full control from the console, including green room and off-stage management, with the ability to switch the front-of-room display to presenter view, now available in Teams Rooms Pro.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- **Zoom-Teams interop timeline diverges:** Zoom's side of cross-platform room interop is expected in the first half of 2027, with HDMI sharing, dual displays, 1080p video, rosters and read-only chat, while Microsoft has given no corresponding date.
- **Cisco goes all-in on multi-platform rooms:** At WebexOne 2026, Cisco confirmed an all-new Webex App built around AI agents, Anthropic's Claude Managed Agents joining Webex spaces, and devices running fully featured Google Meet alongside Webex, Microsoft Teams and Zoom, with Zoom Rooms interop reaching GA this month.
- **Google Workspace AI push:** Google is expanding Gmail's AI Inbox, which reaches paid Workspace subscriptions and promises to surface action items and key topics right inside the inbox, alongside new client-side encrypted file previews in Drive.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- Monitor Microsoft's November rollout of AI anti-phishing tools in Teams — review admin guidance before go-live given the recent spike in Teams-based phishing/malware campaigns.
- Track Microsoft Ignite 2026 (November) for firm dates on Cross-Platform Meeting Connect and any response to Zoom/Cisco's multi-platform room interop push.
- Watch for GA timelines on Teams Phone Agent billing impacts (Copilot Credits) if your org uses or plans to use AI call queue automation.
