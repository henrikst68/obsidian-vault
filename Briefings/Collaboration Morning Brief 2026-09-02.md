# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Wednesday, 2 September 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- A major Microsoft 365 incident that began August 31 knocked out Exchange Online and spread to OneDrive for Business and SharePoint Online, Microsoft Teams, Microsoft Purview, and Microsoft Defender XDR; as of September 1, Microsoft said telemetry data shows "positive recovery trends" while remediation continues.
- Teams Rooms on Windows is rolling out **IntelliFrame people labels** worldwide this month — identifying in-room participants by name and showing contact cards for remote attendees, requiring Teams Rooms Pro licenses and supporting voice/face recognition enrollment.
- Ongoing hardening of admin controls continues, including tighter external-access and federation policies for group chats introduced in the recent release cycle.
