# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Wednesday, 14 October 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Microsoft is rolling out **deepfake detection** for Teams meetings: announced on October 2, 2026 in the Microsoft 365 Roadmap under ID 573451, this feature is currently marked as "In development" and general availability is scheduled for November 2026, integrating third-party detection vendors to verify identity on calls.
- **Copilot is leaving the Teams chat list** this month — from mid-October the Copilot app in the app bar is the only entry point; conversations and deep links are preserved.
- Compliance teams take note: legacy Teams retention policies that implicitly covered Copilot interactions become Teams-only; organizations must create a Copilot-workload retention policy or lose retain/delete coverage, effective this month.
- **Azure Communication Services** (Microsoft's Twilio-competing CPaaS) is being wound down — it is retiring as a standalone product, with no new sign-ups from 23 October 2026 and full retirement by 30 September 2028.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- **Teams Phone Agent** (AI receptionist) is now GA: it's an AI-powered calling receptionist that answers incoming calls and scales to serve customer requests during call-volume spikes and outside regular business hours, rolling out to general availability today, supporting routing, appointments and 60+ languages.
- **Copilot in SharePoint and OneDrive** has reached general availability as part of a broader October refresh that also introduced a new Copilot Chat experience in Teams and Outlook, more PowerPoint skills, new ways to control spending policies, and a stack of new models.
- Billing shift: usage-based billing for Copilot Business licenses was pushed back — previously communicated as November 2, 2026, usage-based billing will now be enabled by default for new Microsoft 365 Copilot Business subscriptions purchased through CSP, beginning December 1, 2026.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- **Logitech joined Microsoft's Device Ecosystem Platform (MDEP)** on Oct 8 with a new hardware lineup — Logitech Rally Bar 2, Tap IP 2, and Tap Scheduler 2 are rolling out globally now, with the larger Rally System 2 Pro modular rig slated for Summer 2027 per vendor reporting.
- Teams Rooms on Android got a notable feature drop: build 1449/1.0.96.2026249711 brings a modernised Gallery view, the interpreter agent on Rooms on Android (Pro), two-way Direct Guest Join with Google Meet, town hall event-group chat, and SIP/H.323 calling via partner, initially with Pexip.
- An AI interpreter feature for Teams Phone devices was quietly scrapped: the built-in AI interpreter for calls on Teams Phone devices was marked Cancelled on the roadmap on 22 September; the interpreter agent on desktop, in meetings, and on Rooms on Android is unaffected.

## Meetingroom Equipment
- Logitech's Oct 8–9 "Logi Launch" was framed as its biggest-ever enterprise push — refreshing its meeting room portfolio with the new Rally Bar 2, Rally System 2 Pro and a new large-room audio approach called Sound Shape, certified for Microsoft Teams, Zoom Rooms and Google Meet.
- Rally Bar 2 runs on MDEP but isn't a full platform switch: this is more than a camera refresh, although it is not a wholesale switch to Windows — Microsoft documents MDEP as an Android-based platform built on the Android Open Source Project.
- Cisco is pushing multi-platform room hardware hard: at WebexOne 2026 it confirmed through a new partnership with Google, Cisco hardware will run fully featured Google Meet alongside Webex, Microsoft Teams and Zoom as part of RoomOS 27, due in November.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- Cisco says its Zoom Rooms integration is now launching: Cisco already counts itself the fastest-growing Microsoft Teams Rooms ecosystem partner, and the Zoom Rooms integration is going live for general availability this month — intensifying hardware-layer competition with Microsoft's MDEP push.
- Google consolidated its enterprise AI story: on Oct 8, Google Cloud CEO Thomas Kurian introduced the Gemini agent, described as "a single, universal agent for work" that handles knowledge work, creates media, and writes code, and it explicitly runs on the web, iOS, Android, Windows, Mac, and the command line, inside Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, and Slack — a direct cross-platform play against Copilot.
- Slack continues incremental AI agent tooling: it now lets users create shareable Slackbot skills via external links so they can travel across orgs, alongside a growing AI agent skill repository with ready-to-add workflows for sales, support, marketing, research and productivity.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- **Compliance/IT:** Audit retention policies before Copilot-workload coverage splits from Teams-only retention this month — legacy policies will silently stop covering Copilot interactions.
- **Admin center:** Review the mid-October Copilot chat-list removal in Teams and communicate the change to end users to avoid support tickets.
- **Procurement/AV:** If room refresh budgets are being planned, compare Logitech's new MDEP-based Rally Bar 2/Tap lineup against Cisco's multi-platform RoomOS 27 devices (Google Meet/Zoom/Teams native) landing in November — both are repositioning for a multi-platform room strategy.
