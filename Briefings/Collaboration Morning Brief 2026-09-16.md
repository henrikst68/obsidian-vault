# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Wednesday, 16 September 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Facilitator now decides on its own when to speak instead of waiting for a prompt, and Teams' Rooms, desks, panels and phones are being rebranded collectively as "intelligent spaces."
- The phased deprecation of device management in the Teams Admin Center began the week of 1 September, and by 30 September admins will no longer manage Teams Rooms on Android, panels or phones from TAC — everything moves to the Teams Rooms Pro Management Portal.
- Teams admins can now access utilization analytics for bookable desks on the Teams Pro Management portal, similar to existing meeting-room analytics, provided a desk has a peripheral device and the right Shared Space license.
- A related feature letting Copilot Studio agents join meetings directly was quietly shelved in August after being on the roadmap for over a year.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- Facilitator's new proactive search requires Microsoft 365 Copilot Premium, must be switched on manually per meeting, and is designed to fire less than once per meeting to avoid distraction.
- Multi-tenant agent management is now in public preview in the Microsoft 365 Admin Center, letting administrators view and govern agents, install or block them, and review risk/activity insights across multiple tenants from one place.
- As of 18 August, customers have access to a simplified Microsoft Copilot app with clearer work/personal indicators and a web app move to copilot.cloud.microsoft.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- GitHub Copilot can now take a task mid-standup and investigate in the background while a meeting continues, and Atlassian's Rovo agent pulls Jira/Confluence ticket context into Teams chat — both part of a wider shift toward outside agents picking up work directly from Teams conversations.
- Agent 365 now ships with pre-integrated partner agents including Miro (visual collaboration boards), Lucid (diagrams/flowcharts), and Manus (autonomous workflow automation), all manageable directly inside Microsoft Teams.
- Viva Connections Home Experience 3.0 has reached general availability, with support for a compact card variant and a multi-source news feed.

## Meetingroom Equipment
- Microsoft's Teams certification for first-gen HP Poly Studio X30/X50/X70, G7500, TC8 and the original Neat Bar/Board ended 3 September 2026 — rooms keep working but no longer carry new certification guarantees.
- Poly and Yealink both made major 2026 hardware pushes in the Teams Rooms space, with HP's Poly line advancing Studio Room Compute and VideoOS 5.0/5.1 while Yealink broadens its MeetingBar/MVC/SmartVision portfolio.
- Passwordless Entra resource accounts for Rooms (Windows and Android), panels and phones are rolling out with a migration wizard and progress dashboard in the Pro Management Portal.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- Cisco's Webex Suite added Day 0 support for the new Apple operating system in September 2026, plus whiteboard "follow" controls so participants track a presenter's viewport during collaborative sessions.
- Zoom's AI Companion can now take meeting notes across third-party platforms including Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and Webex, not just Zoom calls.
- Despite annual predictions of enterprise messaging consolidation, Cisco Webex continues to hold roughly 7% enterprise market share among organizations with 1,000+ employees in 2026.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- **Audit Teams Rooms hardware now:** if you run first-gen Poly Studio X-series or original Neat Bar/Board devices, certification lapsed 3 September — plan refresh or trade-in before broader support questions arise.
- **Prep for TAC device management cutover:** pull device inventories before 30 September and confirm ops teams have access to the Teams Rooms Pro Management Portal ahead of the full cutover.
- **Review Facilitator governance:** since Facilitator's answers appear in meeting chat for all attendees (including external/cross-tenant guests without a Copilot licence), decide organizational policy before broader rollout.
