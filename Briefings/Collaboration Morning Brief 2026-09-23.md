# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Wednesday, 23 September 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Microsoft is rolling out a refreshed meeting experience with simpler controls and a smarter share panel, addressing the fact that meeting controls and share panel have grown crowded, contributing to mis-clicks between high‑impact actions like Share, Leave, and Raise hand, and to accidental shares of unintended content, with rollout completing by end of September 2026.
- A new in-meeting toggle now lets licensed organizers turn off Meeting AI (Copilot, Facilitator, recap) mid-call — Microsoft Teams will introduce a new in-meeting toggle that allows licensed meeting organizers and presenters to turn Meeting AI on or off during a live meeting, providing real-time flexibility to control when AI is active.
- Teams device branding has shifted: the device side of Teams gets a rebrand this month too — Microsoft is now referring to Rooms, desks, panels and phones collectively as "intelligent spaces," alongside a rollout of passwordless Entra resource accounts for Windows and Android Rooms devices.
- LinkedIn Hiring Assistant is now natively integrated in Teams, helping streamline candidate review and hiring manager collaboration.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- Facilitator, Microsoft's in-meeting agent, has moved from reactive to proactive: every other mainstream Copilot surface in Teams today reacts to a prompt, but Facilitator now decides on its own when to speak. It requires Copilot Premium and is designed to fire less than once per meeting to avoid becoming a distraction.
- Third-party coding/work agents are landing inside Teams conversations — GitHub's changelog describes handing it a task mid-standup while the meeting continues, and Atlassian's Rovo agent works similarly for Jira and Confluence, pulling ticket context into a Teams chat.
- Copilot Cowork got a UI refresh this month, including renaming the scheduled-prompts area to "Automations," moving the model picker into the prompting box with separate GPT and Claude sections, and adding a new reasoning-effort slider.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- Agent 365 has pre-integrated several partner agents into Teams at launch, including Miro — the Agent 365 integration brings Miro boards and collaboration tools into Microsoft Teams, enabling teams to create, update, and collaborate on visual content without leaving Microsoft 365 — alongside Lucid diagramming and Manus workflow automation, all tracked via the admin center's observability dashboard.
- Copilot in SharePoint gained new tenant-wide guidance this month: every Microsoft 365 customer now has tenant-specific HTML guidance, so tools like Cowork, Scout, GitHub Copilot, or Copilot Studio can be pointed to it for up-to-date instructions on building HTML inside SharePoint's sandbox.
- Teams Phone infrastructure is tightening: Microsoft began enforcing modern authentication for Teams Phones on June 1, 2026, and devices that relied on retired methods can no longer sign in, accept, or make phone calls, forcing hardware replacement on older units.

## Meetingroom Equipment
- Certified device lifecycle is shrinking: Microsoft ended certification for Teams Rooms devices running Android 10 and 11 on September 3, 2026, and many first-generation devices aren't able to be upgraded to a compatible version.
- Poly and Yealink are intensifying their hardware race for 2026: Poly and Yealink both used 2026 to make their biggest hardware moves yet in the Microsoft Teams Rooms market, and the two certified-device strategies could not look more different — Poly leaning on HP's Studio Room Compute/VideoOS ecosystem, Yealink on breadth across MeetingBar, MVC, and SmartVision lines.
- The new passwordless Entra resource account rollout (noted above) also extends to Rooms, desks, panels, and phones, tightening device identity/security management across the MTR estate.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- Salesforce is repositioning Slack as an AI front door: Slack has become a hub for communicating with AI agents over the past three months, said Alexa Vignone, the company's president and chief revenue officer, with Slackbot users growing by more than 150% from the prior quarter following Dreamforce.
- Slack shipped "Slackforce Surfaces," letting teams transform Salesforce data into interactive visualizations inside Slack, plus "Slack Code," a dedicated space for working with coding agents on a single project from planning through shipping — a direct volley at Teams/Copilot's collaborative-agent push.
- Zoom is refining meeting UX with a redesigned in-meeting share picker and pinned-chat-message banners, though the pinned-message feature will not be immediately available, as it is dependent on a backend server update currently scheduled for September 28, 2026.
