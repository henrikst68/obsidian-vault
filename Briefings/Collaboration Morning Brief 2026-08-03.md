# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Monday, 3 August 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Microsoft's Teams blog wrapped up July noting continued innovation "to help people collaborate with people and AI to get w[ork done]" as the team heads into H2 2026.
- A new ad-hoc room reservation feature is rolling out, letting users quickly book a meeting room for immediate use via a "Reserve" button on the calendar, controllable by IT admins via the Pro management portal.
- Together Mode is now officially deprecated across all Teams clients.
- Worth flagging: a broad Microsoft 365 outage on 23 July degraded Teams chat functionality (images not loading) alongside SharePoint and OneDrive issues, primarily affecting North America, before being resolved the same day.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- AI-powered meeting notes are expanding from regular meetings into Teams Rooms on Windows, rolling out in August and reaching general availability in October 2026.
- The new Facilitator agentic experience will let users capture real-time notes and generate action items in physical, in-person meetings just by tapping "Take notes" — notes appear live on screen and sync to SharePoint.
- Managers can now access Agent 365 data through the Agent Dashboard in Copilot Analytics, giving visibility into activity across all registered agents org-wide.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- Microsoft recently finalized an agreement with the European Commission expanding interoperability and data portability commitments, alongside licensing changes.
- Microsoft also launched the Microsoft Marketplace, a unified destination to find, try, and buy its full catalog of cloud solutions, AI apps, and agents.
- Purview DLP and auto-labeling now extend to non-Microsoft apps including Google Workspace, Box, Dropbox, Salesforce, ServiceNow, AWS, and Cisco Webex — with conditions varying by app.

## Meetingroom Equipment
- Teams Rooms touch boards on Windows now support Miracast for wireless screen sharing alongside Teams Cast and HDMI ingest.
- The main certified hardware vendors for Teams Rooms remain Logitech, Poly/HP, Yealink, Neat, Crestron, Cisco, Lenovo, Jabra, Sennheiser and Shure, each with a different area of strength.
- A new Room Optimization Mode for BYOD spaces, first spotted on the roadmap in June, is set for general availability rollout in August 2026, bringing AI-driven audio/video intelligence to unmanaged meeting rooms.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- From mid-June, Salesforce made Slack the automatic default for every new Enterprise/Unlimited org — a pre-built workspace with no separate purchase or setup required.
- Salesforce frames this as the final step in retiring Chatter (its 2010-era collaboration layer) in favour of Slack, though Chatter isn't being removed for existing customers.
- Independent reviewers continue to rank Zoom as best overall for reliability, Teams as best for Microsoft-centric shops, Google Meet for Workspace users, and Webex for FedRAMP/security-focused enterprises.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- Monitor rollout of **Room Optimization Mode** and **AI Facilitator in physical meetings** this month — both hit GA in August and may require admin configuration ahead of broader availability in October.
- Review the **EU Commission interoperability commitments** for potential licensing/compliance impacts on your tenant.
- Track competitive pressure from **Salesforce's Slack-by-default** move if your org uses Salesforce alongside Teams — evaluate governance implications of a second collaboration layer appearing automatically.
