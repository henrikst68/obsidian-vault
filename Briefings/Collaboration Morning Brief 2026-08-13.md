# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Thursday, 13 August 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Microsoft is **retiring Teams Live Chat** on October 5 — new setups were blocked from August 6, and organisations must remove website widgets before then, though existing customer data and Teams channels will remain accessible. Microsoft is killing Live chat in Teams about 18 months after launch as it seeks to "focus investments on newer customer engagement and communication experiences," with the service retiring October 5. Microsoft blocked new setups on August 6 and told users to remove the widget from their websites before retirement.
- A **redesigned meeting experience** is rolling out this month/September: a refreshed in-meeting experience with a redesigned sharing panel featuring live previews of screens/windows and a two-step confirmation before sharing content, alongside AI meeting archives that help Copilot give more accurate, context-aware answers using AI-generated insights rather than just transcripts.
- A new **"Report" button for deepfake/security threats** in meetings is rolling out this month, letting IT/security teams intervene during suspicious calls. Microsoft has confirmed a new feature in Teams that allows reporting a security issue during a meeting, expected to be fully available in August 2026, driven by rising AI-enabled impersonation risks.
- Teams Rooms on Windows will gain **IntelliFrame people labels** to identify in-room participants by name with contact cards for remote attendees, rolling out worldwide in September 2026, requiring Teams Rooms Pro licenses and supporting voice/face recognition enrollment.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- Copilot's August wave adds **GPT-5.6 and Claude models** that dynamically switch depending on task, plus wider rollout of **Copilot Notebooks** and the new **Cowork** workflow-automation integration. Microsoft 365 Copilot continues to evolve with eight new features introduced in August 2026, including integration of advanced AI models like GPT 5.6 and Claude that dynamically adapt to tasks.
- **Facilitator** is evolving from note-taker to active meeting participant: if a factual question goes unanswered, Facilitator (with permission) runs a web search and posts an answer to the meeting chat, requiring Copilot Premium and manual setup, designed to trigger less than once per meeting, rolling out in September.
- Governance note: Microsoft closed out FY26 with 30 million Copilot seats and Azure up 43% - and, more quietly, OpenAI became a subprocessor in Microsoft 365, with the toggle auto-enabled unless you'd already said no — worth reviewing for EU Data Boundary compliance.
