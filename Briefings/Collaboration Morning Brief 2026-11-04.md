# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Wednesday, 4 November 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Microsoft confirmed in a Message Center update that Teams is introducing AI-powered, context-aware phishing detection to help identify phishing, social engineering, impersonation attempts, and deceptive intent by analyzing conversation context rather than relying on traditional indicators like malicious links; targeted release is underway now with worldwide GA expected December.
- Rollout timeline revised (updated Nov 3): Microsoft has revised rollout dates for the Teams personal message reminders feature, moving them from early November to mid-November 2026, letting users turn chat/channel messages into private follow-up reminders.
- Also updated yesterday: Microsoft updated the rollout timeline for breakout rooms supporting up to 1,000 attendees, with general availability expected by the end of November 2026, with organisers able to create up to 100 rooms without shrinking overall meeting size.
- Roadmap roundup (2 Nov) flags two new November items: Teams Pro Management portal gaining **desk utilization analytics** alongside existing room analytics, and a **redesigned meeting sharing panel** for easier content discovery.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- The Microsoft Copilot app is getting a navigation overhaul: a new Home integrating Chat, Cowork, Projects, and Workspaces — Projects organize related tasks and files without storing content, while Workspaces replace Notebooks for team collaboration, standard rollout beginning late October with deferred release in late November.
- Licensing shift ahead: usage-based billing will now be enabled by default for new Microsoft 365 Copilot Business subscriptions purchased through CSP, beginning December 1, 2026 (pushed back from the originally planned November 2 date).
- Per this week's roadmap scan, Microsoft is introducing enhanced Copilot and AI controls for Teams meetings, with Work IQ positioned as an orchestration layer connecting Copilot, organisational knowledge, agents and M365 services; Copilot Search is also heading to SharePoint/OneDrive (preview Nov, GA January).

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- SharePoint continues absorbing Viva Amplify functionality: Microsoft introduced Site campaigns in SharePoint, a new capability that brings campaign management closer to where SharePoint news is created and published, with Viva Engage cross-posting following.
- Third-party app news: Commission Factory rolled out native **Slack and Microsoft Teams notification integrations**, letting affiliate marketers action program updates directly from chat — a small but telling sign of continued Teams platform extensibility by SaaS vendors.
- Dynamics 365 dominated this week's roadmap volume (45 new entries, over half from Dynamics, plus seven Copilot additions), underscoring Microsoft's push to tie CRM/ERP workflows tighter into the Copilot/Teams fabric.

## Meetingroom Equipment
- Logitech's biggest-ever enterprise launch (Rally Bar 2, Rally System 2 Pro, Tap IP 2/Scheduler 2) continues rolling out on MDEP; a fresh analysis published yesterday notes Facilitator for in-person meetings is now on the roadmap as generally available in Teams Rooms on Windows, and it's coming to Teams Rooms on Android too, targeted November 2026.
- Cisco is finishing its four-platform room strategy: four months after announcing native Zoom Rooms on its hardware, Cisco added Google Meet — when RoomOS 27 ships in November, Cisco devices will run Webex, Microsoft Teams, Zoom and Google Meet, with Cisco also touting itself as Microsoft's fastest-growing Teams Rooms ecosystem partner.
- Neat and Shure's deepened Android-room audio partnership remains notable background context, though no new device news in the last 48 hours specifically.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- Zoom is pushing an AI-productivity narrative: its newsroom highlights a new Unified Context Layer that helps turn meeting conversations into completed work, alongside enhanced My Notes and ZoomMate capabilities, plus new partner-program updates to advance a channel-first strategy.
- Cisco's WebexOne 2026 wrap continues reverberating: Cisco used WebexOne 2026 to put AI agents into Webex meetings, its contact centre and room devices, backed by AI Agent 360, Pindrop deepfake detection, and the Webex Compliance Hub with Theta Lake.
- Analyst framing (UC Today) describes the week's Teams/Zoom cross-platform room work as part of a broader "operating model" shift for meeting rooms, with vendors racing to be interoperable across Teams, Zoom, Webex and Google Meet rather than locked to one ecosystem.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- Review external-chat allow-lists before the new AI phishing detection reaches GA in December — UC Today's analysts argue admins shouldn't wait, given UC Today's weekly roundup argued last week that Teams admins shouldn't wait for November before moving to an allow-list.
- Flag the December 1 shift to default usage-based billing for new Copilot Business CSP licenses to Finance/Procurement ahead of renewal cycles.
- Track breakout room (1,000 attendees) and message reminder feature rollouts for change-management comms, both now expected mid-to-late November.
