# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Thursday, 3 September 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- **Major multi-day M365 outage just resolved:** A core authentication misconfiguration knocked out Teams, Exchange Online, SharePoint, OneDrive, Copilot, Purview and Defender XDR starting Aug 31; "The disruption began at 3:08 pm UTC on August 31 and stems from an issue in a core authentication configuration used across multiple Microsoft 365 services." By Sept 2, "48 hours after the outage began, all Microsoft 365 services have been recovered except Exchange Online, Universal Print, OneDrive for Business, and SharePoint Online."
- Residual impact was still being felt into this week, with reported problems including "search issues, stale presence information, problems with location updates, calendar functionality and difficulties involving Teams Rooms and desktop phones."
- Microsoft was "recognized as a Leader in the 2026 Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ for Unified Communications as a Service—our eighth consecutive year with this recognition."

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- Planner gets native Copilot support: Copilot will now support creating Planner tasks and querying Planner task information, rolling out in September.
- Cowork usage controls matured: users can now choose an effort level to control how Cowork balances quality, speed, and credit usage, with model and effort controls appearing directly in the compose box.
- Admin visibility improved: admins can now create custom reports with new data export capabilities from the Copilot and Agent 365 Dashboards, supporting exports of de-identified, row-level metrics.
- Copilot Search/Chat convergence: Copilot Chat is now available for Microsoft 365 Copilot users from the side pane when using Copilot Search, letting users engage with Copilot while browsing without switching pages.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- Agent 365 is pulling third-party tools directly into Teams: the Agent 365 integration brings Miro boards and collaboration tools into Microsoft Teams, enabling teams to create, update, and collaborate on visual content without leaving Microsoft 365.
- Lucid and Manus are also pre-integrated: the Agent 365 integration brings Lucid's visual intelligence into Microsoft Teams, enabling teams to create and collaborate on diagrams and flowcharts in the flow of work, while the Agent 365 integration brings enterprise-grade observability and governance to Manus, so every action Manus takes on behalf of users is visible, auditable, and subject to organizational policies.

## 🖥️ Meeting Room Equipment
- **Action needed today:** Microsoft's Teams certification for first-gen HP Poly Studio X30, X50, X70, G7500, TC8 and the original Neat Bar and Neat Board ends 3 September 2026, along with several Logitech, DTEN, Yealink and EPOS devices. Existing rooms keep working — "Teams keeps working, backed by two more years of Microsoft support - but new certification guarantees stop."
- Vendor consolidation battle intensifying: HP Poly is pushing its "2026 Poly push around Studio Room Compute, VideoOS 5.0 and the forthcoming 5.1 release, Poly Studio V12, Poly Lens, WXP, Focus 6, Mission headsets" against Yealink's broader room-format portfolio.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- **Meta ditches Google Chat for Slack:** Meta is migrating internal communications from Google Chat to Slack because Slack is the strongest platform for AI agents, according to a memo from AI chief Alexandr Wang. Meanwhile, European governments have spent two years moving the other way, onto sovereign suites chosen for control rather than integrations, with France having ordered its ministries to replace Teams and Zoom for 2.5 million civil servants by 2027.
- Slack is layering in deeper AI: Slackbot now has "Big Mode," a full-screen workspace for research, writing, analysis, and file generation, plus a "Deep Research" mode where Slackbot digs across org data and the web.
- Zoom continues its shift into people-analytics content, publishing new research on how workers dread returning from vacation, and how that dread is changing whether they take time off.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- Confirm with IT whether your tenant is fully recovered from the Aug 31–Sep 2 M365 authentication outage (MO1465074/EX1464935), especially SharePoint/OneDrive sync and Teams Rooms/desktop phone check-ins.
- Audit any Poly Studio X30/X50/X70, Neat Bar/Board (gen1), or listed Logitech/Yealink/DTEN room devices — Teams certification lapses today (3 Sept); plan hardware refresh cycles accordingly.
- Watch the Meta→Slack and France→sovereign-stack moves as early signals of a widening "AI-agent ecosystem vs. sovereignty/control" split shaping enterprise collaboration procurement.
