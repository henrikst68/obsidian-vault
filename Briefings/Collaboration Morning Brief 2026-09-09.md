# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Wednesday, 9 September 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Microsoft has begun phasing out device management inside the Teams Admin Center, with the phased deprecation of device management in the Teams Admin Center beginning the week of 1 September, with Microsoft expecting to complete it "by end of September in all clouds". Admins managing Rooms on Android, panels, or phones need to shift to the Pro Management Portal before 30 September.
- A new **room optimization mode** is rolling out in Teams desktop: users can more easily use their laptop for meetings and collaboration in spaces like focus or huddle rooms that don't yet have a Teams Rooms system, replacing the old shared display mode.
- Microsoft is adding an **org-wide Meetings & Calls view** in the Teams admin center for live monitoring, rolling out 30 September–15 October, which brings live monitoring and troubleshooting with Copilot for Admins support, rolling out worldwide and in GCC with no action needed.
- Teams is preparing to let admins govern third-party agents directly: the Teams Admin Center will recommend third-party agents already in use, with configuration visibility, targeting GA in October 2026 — worth reviewing enablement policy now.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- Microsoft is consolidating its roadmap communications: starting in September 2026, roadmap content for Dynamics 365, Power Platform, and Dataverse moves to the AI at Work roadmap, and Microsoft is shifting from twice-yearly release waves to continuous updates, meaning there will be no September Release Wave 2 announcement this cycle.
- Copilot's integration with Planner is going live: Copilot will now support creating Planner tasks and querying Planner task information, rolling out in September, deepening task management inside chat.
- Governance is getting a boost with Purview adding retention controls for AI memory: Purview introduces retention for Copilot Memory, enabling versioning and historical visibility into memory items stored in Exchange.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- SharePoint's Copilot integration now pushes proactive notifications into Teams: teams can be notified when new files are added or key metadata updates occur via a simple message in a group chat or channel with the file title and link.
- Copilot Notebooks is expanding its grounding sources, with reports that it expands with Power BI, CSV/TSV, and JPG/PNG references — a major boost for grounded AI outputs, giving admins and makers clearer reasoning traces and easier collaboration.
- Google is deepening cross-platform meeting interoperability: Google Calendar updates make it easier to join third-party video meetings, including Microsoft Teams, Zoom, and Cisco Webex, when collaborating across different calendar and email clients, rolling out from 10 September.

## Meetingroom Equipment
- Teams Rooms on Windows is getting **IntelliFrame people labels** for hybrid meetings: the feature identifies in-room participants by name and shows contact cards for remote attendees, rolling out worldwide in September 2026 and requiring Teams Rooms Pro licenses.
- Yealink continues to press its cost/management advantage over Poly: its expanded YMCS integration with Microsoft Places can send occupancy and headcount data into Microsoft Places, giving Teams estates a clearer link between room devices and workplace planning.
- Health monitoring for Android-based Teams Rooms is expanding, with more health signals for Rooms on Android in the Pro portal — storage, meeting app, calendar sync, secondary display and network health, with automatic alerts and ticket workflows — reaching GA in October 2026.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- Slack shipped one of its biggest monthly updates of the year, described as a release that centers on AI collaboration, bringing code channels, new Agent and Slackbot workspaces, deeper research, and tighter connections to Salesforce, Seismic, Lists, and Workflow Builder.
- Cisco is extending its agentic meeting-room stack beyond Webex: general availability is expected this month for the public beta of Cisco Devices for Zoom Rooms, which started in June 2026, with general availability expected September 2026.
- Analysts note the enterprise messaging landscape remains fragmented by ecosystem, with Slack leading for engineering and ChatOps, Teams dominating M365-standardized enterprises, Webex the default for Cisco-stack and government organizations, and Google Chat for Google Workspace organizations, driving demand for cross-platform bridging tools.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- **Urgent:** Confirm your Teams Rooms/panels/phones estate is inventoried and admins have Pro Management Portal access before Teams Admin Center device management is switched off (target: 30 September).
- Review Teams' upcoming "Agents in Teams" recommendation feature and decide an enablement policy for third-party agents ahead of its October GA.
- Watch the Kim Matlock/Matt Slomka **Teams Fireside Chat on 10 September** for more detail on the device management migration and Windows-vs-Android Rooms strategy.
