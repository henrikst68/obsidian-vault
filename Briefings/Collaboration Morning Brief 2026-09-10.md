# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Thursday, 10 September 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- On Teams Rooms on Android, the chat panel will open by default in gallery view, allowing participants to quickly see ongoing meeting chat when they join, with admins able to configure the default behavior and users able to close it in one tap.
- Microsoft is rolling out an enhanced bookable desk experience via Teams panel-based desk dock devices such as the Yealink Linkhub, which indicate at-a-glance desk availability and let visitors book directly on the device.
- The redesigned meeting controls and share panel are continuing their worldwide general availability rollout from early to late September 2026, with GCC, GCC High and DoD environments following through December.
- Recording and transcription are now enabled by default in the global calling policy for new and unmodified existing tenants, broadening access to AI features like Calls Recap and Calling Copilot (not auto-activated per call).

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- Microsoft's September partner update confirms multi-tenant agent management in the Microsoft 365 Admin Center is now in public preview, letting admins view a consolidated agent inventory, install/block agents, and review tenant risk from a single console.
- As of August 18, 2026, customers can access a simplified Microsoft Copilot app with clearer work/personal indicators and a move to copilot.cloud.microsoft; a 15% CSP discount on 3-year Copilot commitments (300+ seats) runs through September 30.
- Copilot's Planner integration—supporting creation and querying of Planner tasks directly from Copilot—rolls out this month, alongside de-identified usage export from Copilot/Agent 365 dashboards.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- Microsoft is consolidating roadmaps: Dynamics 365, Power Platform and Dataverse roadmap content is moving to a single "AI at Work" roadmap as Microsoft shifts from twice-yearly release waves to continuous updates, with Release Planner being retired by November 15, 2026.
- Copilot in SharePoint's September update lets teams notify colleagues in a Teams group chat or channel when new files are added or key metadata changes, keeping the source file in SharePoint while conversation happens in Teams.

## 🖥️ Meeting Room Equipment
- Yealink's new Linkhub desk-dock devices are being integrated with the Teams panel app to power the enhanced bookable-desk experience noted above—an early sign of MTR peripheral vendors expanding beyond room devices into desk-booking hardware.
- Android-based Teams Rooms are getting UI defaults (gallery-view chat panel) rather than new hardware SKUs this week — a software-side refresh for existing certified room devices rather than a new device launch.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- Google is rolling out Calendar interoperability improvements starting today (10 September 2026): improvements to Google Calendar make it easier to join third-party video meetings, including Microsoft Teams, Zoom, and Cisco Webex, when collaborating across different calendar and email clients, with outgoing invites containing third-party conferencing links now automatically formatted for external recipients on Outlook or Apple Calendar.
- Teams remains a growing social-engineering target: a new SynkLoader campaign impersonates IT support staff through Microsoft Teams messages to trick users into downloading malicious files leading to credential theft, while separate Microsoft Security research flagged a human-operated intrusion campaign weaponizing Teams to bypass phishing defenses, escalating from a single compromised workstation to network-wide access.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- **Security posture:** Brief IT/security teams on the SynkLoader and helpdesk-impersonation campaigns; reinforce out-of-band verification for any Teams message claiming to be from internal IT.
- **Governance:** If Henrik manages multiple tenants, trial the new multi-tenant agent management preview in the M365 Admin Center for centralized Copilot/agent oversight.
- **Rollout planning:** Confirm the redesigned Teams meeting controls/share panel (GA through late September) won't disrupt end-user training materials or support documentation.
- **Cross-platform meetings:** Watch how Google's new Calendar interop features affect external meeting invites if Henrik's org regularly schedules mixed Teams/Zoom/Webex meetings with outside guests.
