# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Friday, 21 August 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Some users reported degraded Teams performance on 20 August, with monitoring services logging degraded performance with 108 reports in the past 24 hours, though the service was not entirely down but may experience slowness or instability; DownDetector also showed a report surge starting around 9:06am ET that day.
- Microsoft is preparing a new **Efficiency Mode** for Teams, aimed at improving app responsiveness on systems with limited CPU and memory resources.
- A dedicated **Meeting Recap app** is landing this month, with UC Today flagging what IT teams need to do before it arrives.
- Rolling out this month, Copilot in Teams can now analyze anything shared during a meeting in real-time, including documents, slides, and spreadsheets being screen shared.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- Microsoft's official Copilot release notes (covering 28 July–11 Aug) confirm parallel content and identity crawl for Copilot connectors, making ingested content available to users faster than before.
- August's Copilot wave adds new model choice — GPT-5 in Copilot Chat plus support for Anthropic models, with a phased release reaching Microsoft 365 Copilot license holders first — alongside inline Word/Excel/PowerPoint agents and wider availability of Copilot Notebooks synced with OneNote.
- On the consumer side, Microsoft confirmed Deep Research is being retired in the Copilot app for consumers starting August 18, 2026, part of a broader consumer-app redesign — worth noting for any BYOD/consumer Copilot users in the org.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- **LinkedIn Hiring Assistant** is now integrated directly in Teams, helping streamline candidate review and hiring manager collaboration.
- Teams' natural-language search continues to expand — users can now find chats, files, and other content by asking questions in natural language instead of searching exact keywords.
- Viva Engage remains under active integration into Teams' navigation, continuing Microsoft's multi-year push noted by industry watchers to fold Yammer/Viva Engage functionality directly into the Teams shell.

## Meetingroom Equipment
- Poly and Yealink are locked in an intensifying MTR hardware race: Poly added Mission and Focus 6 headsets plus new room compute, while Yealink expanded its Teams room architecture, MP E2 phone roadmap, and YMCS integration with Microsoft Places.
- HP's Poly division is pushing Studio Room Compute, VideoOS 5.0 (with 5.1 forthcoming), Poly Studio V12, Poly Lens, and Focus 6/Mission headsets as an integrated managed estate story.
- Cisco is extending its agentic room features across platforms: the Agentic Director and Workspace Advisor agents in RoomOS 26 are already supported for Webex and Microsoft Teams Rooms customers, signalling continued cross-platform hardware compatibility.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- Slack launched **Slack Code** on 20 August — an AI coding environment where teams can use coding agents like Claude Code and GitHub Copilot directly in Slack, with Claude, ChatGPT, Devin and Copilot all founding partners, free on every plan.
- The feature ships with dedicated channels where AI agents write, review and ship software in the open, positioning Slack more aggressively as an agentic workplace competitor to Teams/Copilot.
- Slack's broader August admin update also adds agents in DMs, Slackbot-generated slides and dashboards, Google Docs as a Workflow Builder knowledge source, and adjustable AI content safety filters, alongside a FedRAMP boundary deprecation admins needed to act on by 19 August.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- **Monitor Teams service health** — keep an eye on the Microsoft 365 admin center for any follow-up on the 20 August performance degradation reports before assuming full resolution.
- **Benchmark Slack Code** against Copilot's inline Word/Excel/PowerPoint agents — Slack's move into agentic coding workflows is a direct swipe at Microsoft's "Frontier Firm" positioning and worth a competitive read-out.
- **Review MTR refresh cycle** — with Poly and Yealink both pushing significant hardware/software updates (VideoOS 5.1, Focus 6 headsets, YMCS/Places integration), this is a good moment to reassess room hardware standardization if a refresh is due.
