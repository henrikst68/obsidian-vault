# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Friday, 31 July 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Cisco has published guidance enabling **native Microsoft Teams Rooms support on Cisco hardware**, published July 28 — a notable expansion of the certified device ecosystem beyond the traditional Poly/Logitech/Yealink/Neat lineup.
- Microsoft continues to move device management out of the Teams admin center: administrators can now use the Pro Management portal to monitor device health, manage inventory, and access analytics for both Windows and Android devices, with management of Android-based Teams Rooms, panels, and phones moving from the Teams admin center to the Pro Management portal.
- A new admin-facing security feature lets users flag risk directly in the product: users can now report suspicious external users directly within Teams, alongside existing block actions, with reports surfaced in the Teams admin center for investigation.
- Compliance teams should note: Teams meeting recordings can now automatically inherit the meeting's sensitivity label when admins enable label inheritance in the sensitivity label policy, so a "Confidential" meeting's recording carries the same label.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- Copilot Cowork, an agentic system that plans, executes, and delivers real work, is now generally available worldwide — users define the task and Cowork executes it start to finish, returning a completed deliverable rather than a draft.
- Model diversification is accelerating: two new frontier models — OpenAI's GPT-5.6 and Anthropic's Claude Sonnet 5 — arrived in Copilot this month, while Bloomberg reports Microsoft is using more of its own MAI models in M365 Copilot scenarios for efficiency on non-consumption workloads, adding model resilience alongside Anthropic and OpenAI.
- Agent adoption is being pushed directly into Teams: Copilot Studio agents can now be shared directly to a Teams team with a notification to the main channel and an install link, closing the adoption gap between an agent existing and a team knowing about it.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- July is a high-density roadmap month with major updates across Purview, Teams, OneDrive/SharePoint, and Copilot, shifting attention toward data security automation, Teams meeting intelligence, and OneDrive/SharePoint UX refreshes.
- Teams meeting Notes, powered by Loop, is being added to instant calls and meetings, including those from Meet now and calls started from chat — allowing users to collaboratively build agendas, capture notes, and create action items.
- Billing flexibility is coming to AI infrastructure: Microsoft is introducing pay-as-you-go usage for Work IQ APIs, giving organisations more flexibility in how they consume and pay for productivity intelligence capabilities within Microsoft 365.

## Meetingroom Equipment
- Cisco's move into native MTR support (published July 28) gives customers another certified path for boardroom hardware alongside the established Poly, Logitech, Yealink, Jabra, Crestron and Neat lineup.
- HP/Poly recently launched the **Studio Room Compute**, an AI-powered dedicated processing hub for Teams Rooms, and Barco's ClickShare Hub is gaining traction for wireless BYOD in Teams Rooms.
- Vendor ecosystem note: feature parity across Logitech, Poly, Neat, Yealink, and DTEN is high in 2026, with five OEMs dominating the certified Zoom Rooms list — the same appliance hardware increasingly runs both MTR and Zoom Rooms modes interchangeably.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- Salesforce has pushed **Slackbot to general availability**, positioning it as "the front door to the Agentic Enterprise, powered by Salesforce", deepening Slack's role as an agent orchestration layer that rivals Copilot's Teams integration.
- Zoom is playing a cross-platform card: AI Companion can now take notes across third-party meeting platforms like Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and WebEx, enriching notes with transcripts even when Zoom isn't the meeting host.
- Competitive positioning continues to sharpen around security: Webex holds a US Defense Information Systems Agency (DISA) Provisional Authority at Level 5 while Zoom has DISA Level 4 clearance, a differentiator enterprises are weighing for regulated workloads.
