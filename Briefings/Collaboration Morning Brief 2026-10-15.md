# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Thursday, 15 October 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- An upcoming Teams update will let organizers share video directly from OneDrive during Teams events instead of screen-sharing or embedding clips in PowerPoint, improving quality and reducing friction for producers.
- Microsoft's weekly roadmap roundup confirms meeting organizers can now delete meeting-generated content — recording, transcript, AI summary, and notes — directly from the recap page, while shared files remain untouched.
- Teams Events now supports up to 20,000 registrations for organizers with valid Attendee packs, up from previous limits.
- A GSA-related outage on 13 October briefly disrupted Teams alongside Outlook, OneDrive and SharePoint for users in Germany and the UK reaching Microsoft 365 through Global Secure Access, attributed to GSA infrastructure performing below acceptable thresholds; service was restored same day.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- Admins have until **25 October** to review a key control: a new "advanced package uploads" setting in the Copilot control system will, by default, let users upload advanced packages such as declarative agents with actions or MCP servers.
- Copilot in Teams is getting more context-aware — when @Copilot is invoked in Teams, the entire team now gets the shared context and permissions of the channel, group chat, or meeting.
- Copilot Notebooks (rolling out to Frontier) will let users delegate simple tasks to Copilot — managing references, synthesizing project context, and creating or editing Word, Excel, or PowerPoint files directly from chat.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- **Teams Phone Agent** (AI receptionist) is now generally available, billed via usage-based Copilot Credits, and requires a Teams Phone resource account plus a spending policy configured in advance per Teams Phone Agent generally available, an AI receptionist billed by the call, charged with usage-based billing through Copilot Credits including call duration and capabilities invoked, needing a resource account and a spending policy.
- **Azure Communication Services** is being wound down as a standalone product — Microsoft's CPaaS/Twilio-competing play is retiring, with no new sign-ups from 23 October 2026 and standalone retirement by 30 September 2028; Teams-related interop (Teams Phone, Unify, video interop) is unaffected.
- Copilot in SharePoint/OneDrive has reached general availability, and SharePoint News posts in the Teams app now get an AI-generated audio overview via the SharePoint app for Teams (Viva Connections).

## Meetingroom Equipment
- **Logitech** unveiled its next-gen Teams Rooms lineup built on Microsoft's Device Ecosystem Platform (MDEP) on 8 October, including Rally Bar 2 with built-in occupancy sensors, Rally System 2 Pro for large modular rooms, Rally Speaker 2 Pro/Max using Sound Shape audio technology, and refreshed Tap IP 2/Tap Scheduler 2 controllers; Rally Bar 2 and the Tap devices are rolling out now, with the rest slated for summer 2027.
- Microsoft has fully moved **Android meeting-room device management** off the Teams Admin Center: inventory, updates, health monitoring and settings for Rooms on Android, Teams phones, panels and SIP phones now sit in the Teams Rooms Pro Management Portal instead.
- Cisco used WebexOne (Austin, Oct 5–8) to push multi-platform room hardware: Cisco's room devices started with Webex and Microsoft Teams Rooms, and are now adding Zoom and Google Meet, with native Zoom Rooms support on Cisco hardware reaching GA in October.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- **Google** unified its enterprise AI strategy at Gemini at Work 2026 (Oct 8–9), launching a single, universal Gemini agent for work that answers questions, handles knowledge work, creates images/media, and gives coworker agents their own Workspace identity and spend controls.
- **Slack** rolled out shareable Slackbot skills on 8 October, letting users create external links so skills can travel across orgs, alongside a growing AI agent skill repository with ready-to-add workflows for sales, support, marketing and research.
- **Zoom's** AI Companion now works across competitor platforms — My Notes takes meeting notes not just in Zoom but across third-party platforms like Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and Webex, intensifying cross-platform AI notetaking competition.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- **Flag for admins:** Review and configure the Copilot "advanced package uploads" setting before it defaults to ON for users on 25 October.
- **Budget/licensing:** Assess exposure to the ACS standalone retirement (no new sign-ups from 23 Oct) and the new usage-based Copilot Credit billing for Teams Phone Agent.
- **Room equipment refresh cycle:** If evaluating MTR hardware, note Logitech's MDEP-based Rally Bar 2/Tap IP 2 availability now vs. Rally System 2 Pro in summer 2027 — factor into procurement timing.
