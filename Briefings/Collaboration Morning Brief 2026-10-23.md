# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Friday, 23 October 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Microsoft published plans for **Cross-Platform Meeting Connect**, aimed at giving Teams Rooms hardware a "more complete room experience" when joining Zoom meetings, including planned HDMI sharing so people can connect a laptop and share content directly, plus synced mute, unmute, and end-call controls on room hardware.
- Teams' **Meet app has been renamed "Events app"** in GCC tenants, letting users discover organisational events and plan their own, per the latest roadmap roundup.
- A new rollout lets Teams generate **intelligent meeting recaps without retaining the underlying transcript** — a privacy-friendly option for sensitive meetings.
- Community complaint volumes for Teams/M365 spiked on Oct 20 with a monitoring snapshot showing 83 Teams reports against a baseline of 13, and 73 Microsoft 365 reports against a baseline of 22, though this reflects a spike in community reports, not a confirmed outage; it follows earlier October hiccups with Azure Rights Management and meeting sign-ins.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- Microsoft's Partner Center rolled out a new limited-time offer (dated Oct 21) to **"Grow Microsoft 365 E7 deals with matched Copilot credits,"** aimed at CSP partners.
- **Viva Insights' Copilot Analytics** now surfaces an Agent 365 Dashboard for Microsoft platform agents without an Agent 365 license, giving admins one consolidated view of agent reporting, including who built each agent and why.
- Power Apps is consolidating AI controls: Maker Studio now adds a top-level "Copilot and AI" tab so makers can see and adjust all AI options for model-driven apps in one place, instead of scattered settings.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- **SharePoint News can now cross-post into Viva Engage**, with SharePoint's native commenting replaced by Engage's richer conversation module
