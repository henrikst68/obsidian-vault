# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Tuesday, 20 October 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- **Copilot exits the Teams chat list:** From mid-October, Copilot leaves the Teams chat list, with the Copilot app in the app bar becoming the only entry point for conversations.
- **External file sharing defaults are changing:** External chat file sharing turns on by default from late October — the paperclip and drag-and-drop arrive in federated 1:1, group and meeting chats, with files going to the sender's OneDrive and Teams assigning permissions. This sparked debate at a recent London user group.
- **Deepfake detection coming to meetings:** Microsoft has formally put on the roadmap that Teams will get plug-in connectivity for third parties to detect synthetic audio and video and alert users natively, with GA targeted for November.
- **Azure Communication Services winding down as standalone:** Azure Communication Services, Microsoft's CPaaS play, is retiring as a standalone product, with no new sign-ups for retiring services from 23 October 2026. Teams interop pieces (Teams Phone, Extensibility, Unify, video interop, Click-2-Call) are unaffected; ACS Direct Routing, PSTN Direct Offer, Chat and Rooms are retiring.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- **Teams Phone Agent GA expands:** Teams Phone Agent now supports 42 voices, including 33 new ones, building on its late-September general availability as an AI-powered calling receptionist that answers incoming calls and scales to serve customer requests during call-volume spikes and outside regular business hours.
- **New unified Copilot "Home" rolling out:** The Copilot app is introducing a Home experience that brings Chat and Cowork together on one screen, letting users switch between modes in the prompt box, rolling out through October.
- **Usage-based billing becomes the default:** Usage-based billing will be enabled by default for new Microsoft 365 Copilot Business subscriptions purchased through CSP, beginning December 1, 2026 — worth flagging to procurement/finance ahead of time.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- **GitHub Copilot expands cross-platform reach:** Multi-repo support for GitHub Copilot in Slack and Microsoft Teams now adds support for working across multiple repositories in a single conversation.
- **SharePoint Copilot gets workflow and chat upgrades:** Copilot can now help build quick workflows to collaborate in Teams chats or channels when key file changes and metadata updates occur, without manually configuring Power Automate, plus new contextual suggestion chips in chat.
- **Interpreter and Rooms updates in the pipeline:** Recent Message Center items flag a bilingual consecutive interpretation mode for Copilot in Teams and IntelliFrame people-labelling for in-room participants in Teams Rooms, per this week's roadmap tracking.

## 🖥️ Meeting Room Equipment
- **Logitech's biggest-ever enterprise launch:** Logitech introduced a new range of enterprise collaboration products on October 14 — its largest enterprise launch in company history — led by the Rally Bar 2, Rally System 2 Pro and a new audio technology called Sound Shape, built on Microsoft's new Device Ecosystem Platform (MDEP).
- **Analyst caution despite the launch:** JPMorgan cut Logitech's price target to $94 from $102 while keeping a Neutral rating, leaving the October 27 earnings report as the next test of demand.
- **Yealink pushes mobility for frontline Teams Phone users:** Yealink's MP55 E2 and MP66W deliver natively certified, wireless-ready handsets built for shared spaces for reception, healthcare, retail and manufacturing workers. Separately, Neat Pad Pro and Pad Gen 2 devices are showing as "Unmonitored" in Microsoft's Pro Management Portal — a reporting-only issue, with devices confirmed healthy.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- **Google doubles down on Gemini-powered Workspace AI:** Google is rolling out AI Inbox in Gmail to paid Workspace customers, initially limited to US-English accounts, alongside new "next steps" in Google Meet, powered by Gemini and Workspace Studio, to automate repetitive post-meeting tasks.
- **Zoom refreshes meeting controls and AI features:** Zoom Meetings added translated speech projection, voice simulation, and countdown alerts for regulated participants nearing meeting limits, alongside a reorganized host-tools UI.
- **Cisco pitches integration-first strategy at WebexOne:** Cisco emphasised that "every subsequent technology you buy should cost you less to integrate than the previous one," pointing to native Google Meet, Teams, and Zoom support on Cisco devices as a differentiator against platform lock-in.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- **Flag the ACS retirement deadline (23 Oct)** to any teams using Azure Communication Services Direct Routing/PSTN — confirm migration plans before new sign-ups close.
- **Review default external file-sharing change** rolling out late October in Teams chats; update DLP/governance policies if needed before it goes live.
- **Watch Logitech's Oct 27 earnings** for signal on enterprise MTR demand following the Rally Bar 2/MDEP launch — relevant if room refreshes are on the roadmap.
