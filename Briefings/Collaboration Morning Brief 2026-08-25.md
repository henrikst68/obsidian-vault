# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Tuesday, 25 August 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Management of Android-based Teams Rooms, Teams panels, and Teams phones is moving from the Teams admin center to the Pro Management portal, providing a consistent and secure management experience across devices and manufacturers.
- Teams adds multi-line calling on mobile, letting users make and receive calls using multiple assigned phone numbers directly from a mobile device, with unified call history and seamless line switching.
- Microsoft has confirmed a new in-meeting "Report" feature that lets participants flag suspicious activity so IT/security teams can intervene, driven by rising impersonation and deepfake risks in online meetings.
- A pre-join mic/speaker test tool (record and play back a clip) has gone live worldwide, with GCC/GCC High/DoD completing rollout in early August.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- Microsoft closed out FY26 with 30 million Copilot seats and Azure revenue up 43%, while OpenAI quietly became a subprocessor in Microsoft 365, with the toggle auto-enabled unless previously opted out — worth a governance/compliance check for Henrik's tenant.
- Facilitator is gaining a web-search extension: if a factual question goes unanswered in a meeting, it can (with permission) run a web search and post the answer to chat, requiring Copilot Premium and now rolling out in September, pushed from August.
- Deep Research is being retired in the Copilot app for consumers starting August 18, 2026, alongside podcasts, Group chat, and other features retiring as part of a broader Copilot app redesign.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- This month's update batch also includes eight Copilot updates, five Teams updates, and a new certification programme for Teams Phone voice agents.
- Poly and Yealink both made major 2026 hardware moves in the Microsoft Teams Rooms market — Poly added Mission and Focus 6 headsets plus new room compute, while Yealink expanded its Teams room architecture, MP E2 phone roadmap, and YMCS integration with Microsoft Places.
- Join-by-code for private Teams now requires owner approval before external users can join, while public teams remain unaffected.

## Meetingroom Equipment
- Poly's Studio Room Compute, the forthcoming VideoOS 5.1 release, Poly Lens, and HP's wider Workforce Experience Platform aim to reduce IT management overhead across standardized device estates.
- Yealink's room-design breadth spans MeetingBar, MVC S80/S98, SmartVision 80, MeetingBoard Pro, and MTower, covering more room shapes than a single standardized product line.
- Teams Rooms Pro licensing at roughly $40 per room/month adds advanced meeting intelligence alongside stronger admin controls, a relevant cost lever if Henrik is planning room refreshes.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- Slack launched "Slack Code," giving teams dedicated channels where AI agents write, review and ship software together, with Claude, ChatGPT, Devin and Copilot as founding partners, free on every plan.
- Starting August 26, 2026, Google Chat becomes the home for "Ask Gemini," a unified command line for work powered by Workspace Intelligence — a direct competitive move against Teams/Copilot integration.
- Cisco's Webex Contact Center expanded AI Assistant language support to 15 languages in General Availability and 13 in Controlled Availability as of August 18, 2026.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- Check tenant admin settings for the OpenAI subprocessor toggle in Microsoft 365 Copilot — confirm this aligns with data governance policy given it was auto-enabled by default.
- Track Google's "Ask Gemini" in Chat (live from Thursday, 26 Aug) as a competitive benchmark against Copilot's Teams/Viva Engage agents.
- If planning meeting room refreshes, compare Poly's VideoOS 5.1/room-compute roadmap against Yealink's broader room-shape lineup and Places integration before committing budget.
