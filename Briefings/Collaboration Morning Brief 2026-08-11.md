# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Tuesday, 11 August 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Microsoft is retiring **Teams Live Chat** on 5 October 2026, saying it wants to "focus investments on newer customer engagement and communication experiences." New setups were already blocked from 6 August, and admins are being told to remove the widget from their websites before shutdown.
- A new **bot-detection and reporting feature** is rolling out through August: Teams will flag unauthorized external meeting bots and give organizers a way to report security issues mid-meeting, part of a push against AI deepfake and impersonation risks in meetings.
- **Room Optimization Mode** is set for worldwide GA this month, bringing AI-driven noise suppression, auto speaker-framing, and echo cancellation to BYOD/laptop-led meeting rooms without dedicated hardware — scheduled to replace shared display mode in Teams desktop rather than sit beside it as a separate legacy option.
- Admins should note several August rollouts landing in parallel: new meeting-app pinning limits, cross-tenant audit-record sharing for meeting participant details, and stricter PowerShell controls for federated external access.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- Microsoft is rolling out **GPT-5 in Copilot Chat plus Anthropic model support**, giving license holders more model choice with "faster, more capable responses with a phased release that reaches Microsoft 365 Copilot license holders first."
- **Inline app agents** for Word, Excel and PowerPoint can now be invoked directly inside Copilot Chat, and **Copilot Notebooks** (syncing with OneNote) are now widely available to commercial and education customers.
- Governance is becoming a bigger talking point: commentators note the update wave "raises important tradeoffs around governance, cost, and the need for human verification, so organizations should pilot thoughtfully and set policies early."
- Separately, Microsoft closed out FY26 with 30 million Copilot seats and Azure up 43%, and OpenAI has quietly become a subprocessor in Microsoft 365 with the data-sharing toggle auto-enabled unless orgs had already opted out.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- **Copilot in SharePoint** can now generate an interactive HTML dashboard directly from a list that "stays connected to the underlying list" instead of requiring manual report rebuilds.
- Microsoft is piloting a scaled-down **"Copilot in 30" trial motion** for small businesses (under 300 employees), pairing a 25-user, 30-day Copilot Business trial with a new industry-tailored evaluation tool launching mid-August via partners.
- Teams Phone ecosystem is expanding with a new **certification programme for Teams Phone voice agents**, and meeting-room hardware partners (e.g., Lenovo/Huddly) continue deepening Teams Rooms and Zoom Rooms cross-compatible device integrations.

## Meetingroom Equipment
- **Teams Rooms on Windows** is getting IntelliFrame people-labelling (name ID + contact cards for remote attendees) via certified intelligent cameras, rolling out worldwide in September and requiring Teams Rooms Pro licensing.
- Hardware vendors are aligning around AI-ready rooms: Lenovo's ThinkSmart Core Gen 2 paired with Huddly's intelligent audio-video devices brings deeper platform integration and unified device management, with a consistent Microsoft Teams Rooms and Zoom Room experience across room sizes.
- Industry commentary continues to stress that as Teams Rooms adds more AI intelligence, "quality AV hardware becomes more important" since good microphones, DSP, loudspeakers and camera systems directly affect room performance.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- **Slack/Salesforce**: August release notes show Slack shipping productivity features including scheduled custom statuses, a merged Omniswitcher search, and Canvas auto-generated tables of contents, alongside deeper Slackbot-Agentforce integration (auto-creating Salesforce-linked channels, unified Agentforce DM view).
- **Zoom** continues pushing its "system of action" strategy with AI Companion's agentic retrieval spanning Zoom, Google Drive, OneDrive and Slack content — Zoom reports monthly active users of AI Companion have more than tripled year-over-year in the fourth quarter of FY26.
- Analysts (Gartner) now group Slack alongside Zoom, Webex, Teams and Google Workspace in the Enterprise AI Assistants (EAIAs) category, signalling the competitive field is converging on agentic AI as the key battleground rather than core video/chat features.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- **Audit Teams Live Chat usage** in your org before the 5 October retirement — flag any customer-facing website widgets still active for removal.
- **Review governance policies** ahead of broader Copilot model rollout (GPT-5/Anthropic) and confirm your tenant's stance on the OpenAI subprocessor toggle in Microsoft 365.
- **Watch Room Optimization Mode's August GA** — worth testing in BYOD/huddle spaces before wider rollout to gauge impact on informal meeting rooms.
