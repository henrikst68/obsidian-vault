# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Friday, 6 November 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Microsoft has given admins new control over meeting transcripts: Teams administrators will be able to use a meeting policy to control whether profane words are masked in live transcription and saved meeting transcripts, with filtering remaining on by default, rolled out this week.
- Teams is getting AI-powered phishing detection for external chats — Message Center notice MC1519866 confirmed Teams will use context-aware detection to identify phishing, social engineering, impersonation and deceptive intent, starting with chats from external contacts reaching out for the first time, with Targeted Release beginning in early November and worldwide general availability following in early to mid-December.
- 8x8 announced Microsoft certification this week to simplify Direct Routing/Operator Connect calling, noting channel partners get Direct Routing and Operator Connect under one contract, bill and management experience, with onboarding expanding through Q4 2026.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- Microsoft is rolling out Copilot Chat "fair use" limits: starting November 2, 2026 for organizations with 2,000 or fewer Microsoft 365 seats, and beginning November 9, 2026 for larger organizations, additional Copilot Chat features and capabilities will be subject to fair use limits, though Copilot Chat remains included in Microsoft 365 subscriptions, and no admin action is required.
- The Microsoft Copilot app is getting a navigation overhaul: a new Home integrates Chat, Cowork, Projects, and Workspaces, with Projects organizing tasks/files and Workspaces replacing Notebooks for team collaboration, rolling out from late October 2026.
- Microsoft's October Copilot roundup flagged Agent 365 expansion: organizations can use Agent 365 to understand who is using Autopilot, manage and govern its access, assess risks, and measure business value, rolling out in November to Frontier.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- Agent 365 is onboarding ecosystem partners directly into Teams — e.g. Box's Agent 365 integration surfaces file management and collaboration capabilities directly in Teams and Outlook, letting users access, share, and collaborate on Box content without leaving Microsoft 365.
- Partner-ecosystem commentary notes Microsoft's fast release cadence is forcing resellers to adapt: in the Lync and Skype for Business years, upgrades arrived in phases and partners had time to learn how to sell and deploy each one before the next came along — no longer the case with Teams.
- Microsoft confirmed logistics for its flagship conference: Microsoft Ignite 2026 takes place November 17–20 in San Francisco and online, where attendees can build connections and get early product access to shape 2027 plans.

## Meetingroom Equipment
- Teams Rooms hardware lifecycle is tightening: Microsoft ended certification for Teams Rooms devices running Android 10 and 11 on September 3, 2026, and first-generation devices including the HP Poly Studio X Series Gen1, Neat Bar/Board Gen1, Logitech Rally Bar Huddle, and Yealink RoomPanel Plus can't be upgraded to a compatible version — Henrik's customers on older fleets should check models now.
- Vendor competition is intensifying: a comparison piece notes Poly added Mission and Focus 6 headsets plus new room compute, while Yealink expanded its Teams room architecture, MP E2 phone roadmap, and YMCS integration with Microsoft Places as 2026's biggest moves.
- Copilot is now reaching physical meeting rooms: Copilot in Teams Rooms requires a Teams Rooms Pro license, a Microsoft 365 Copilot license for users interacting with it from the room console, and an intelligent speaker device (Jabra Speak2 75, EPOS Expand 80T, or Yealink SmartVision 60) for speaker attribution.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- Cisco used WebexOne 2026 to go "all-platform": it confirmed Anthropic's Claude is coming into Webex, OpenAI's dots will connect through MCP, and Cisco room devices will run Google Meet natively alongside Microsoft Teams, Zoom and Webex, with RoomOS 27, due in November, adding native Google Meet support so one device fleet can boot into Webex, Teams, Zoom or Google Meet.
- Native Zoom Rooms support on Cisco hardware is also landing: native Zoom Rooms on Cisco hardware reaches general availability this month, deepening cross-platform device interoperability.
- Zoom's developer platform shipped incremental AI/infra updates this week, including RTMS changes coming in the November release — V2 protocol, application-layer media encryption, Zoom Phone support, transcript rewind, multiple individual video streams, and a TLS 1.2/1.3 requirement starting November 15, 2026.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- **Audit MTR fleet age**: if any Teams Rooms hardware is 4+ years old (especially Poly Studio X Gen1, Neat Gen1, Logitech Rally Bar Huddle, Yealink RoomPanel Plus), check certification status given the Android 10/11 cutoff.
- **Flag Copilot Chat fair-use limits** to stakeholders ahead of the November 9 rollout to larger (2,000+ seat) organizations — no admin action required, but end-user expectations may need managing.
- **Block calendar time for Microsoft Ignite 2026** (Nov 17–20, San Francisco/online) — expect major Teams, Copilot/Agent 365, and admin-agent announcements shaping 2027 roadmaps.
