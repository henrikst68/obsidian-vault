# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Tuesday, 8 September 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- **Android device certification cliff hits admin deadline:** Android 10 and 11 Teams devices lost certification on 3 September, leaving one year of Microsoft support — first-generation HP Poly, Logitech and Yealink models with no way to upgrade the Android version, and Teams Admin Center management for these device types stops by 30 September.
- **Live meeting monitoring for admins:** An org-wide Meetings & Calls view in the Teams admin center for live monitoring and troubleshooting, with Copilot for Admins support, is rolling out 30 September to 15 October worldwide and GCC, showing in-progress meetings when at least one participant holds a Teams Premium or Teams Rooms Pro licence.
- **Third-party agents get admin visibility:** Admins can discover and enable Teams agents for third-party apps already in use from the Teams admin center, with recommendations and required-config visibility, reaching GA in October 2026.
- **Fallout from late-August outage still fresh:** A widespread Microsoft 365 outage that initially disrupted Exchange Online expanded to affect Teams, SharePoint Online, OneDrive for Business, and Defender XDR, with isolated email delays metastasizing into cross-platform disruptions.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- **Copilot Notebooks gets richer grounding:** Copilot Notebooks expands with Power BI, CSV/TSV, and JPG/PNG references — a major boost for grounded AI outputs, and these additions allow users to bring organizational data, structured tables, and visuals directly into notebooks, so Copilot can generate more accurate summaries, briefs, and presentations grounded in richer context.
- **SharePoint skills now portable:** Reusable Copilot skills can now follow users across SharePoint and OneDrive, with Copilot able to measure and improve those skills via evaluations, alongside new guidance for AI agents building SharePoint-safe experiences.
- **Governance win for prompt libraries:** Admins can now delegate Copilot prompt publishing to users or groups — a major governance improvement for org-wide prompt libraries.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- **One admin experience for agent installs:** A Teams update unifies app and agent installation management across Microsoft 365 and Teams admin centers — previously, changes in one console didn't apply consistently to the other, and now installation is unified across all supported surfaces for a more streamlined admin experience.
- **Microsoft Marketplace live:** Microsoft is launching the Microsoft Marketplace, a unified web destination to find, try, and buy its full catalog of cloud solutions, AI apps, and agents.
- **Google closes the meeting-interop gap:** Starting 10 September, Google is rolling out Calendar improvements that make it easier to join third-party video meetings, including Microsoft Teams, Zoom, and Cisco Webex, when collaborating across different calendar and email clients.

## Meetingroom Equipment
- **Certification expiry hits multiple vendors:** Microsoft's Teams certification for first-gen HP Poly Studio X30, X50, X70, G7500, TC8 and the original Neat Bar/Board ended 3 September 2026, along with select Logitech, DTEN, Yealink and EPOS devices, though rooms don't switch off — Teams keeps working, backed by two more years of Microsoft support, but new certification guarantees stop.
- **Poly vs. Yealink hardware race intensifies:** Poly added Mission and Focus 6 headsets plus new room compute, while Yealink expanded its Teams room architecture, MP E2 phone roadmap, and YMCS integration with Microsoft Places.
- **Desk hubs get smarter:** Desk hub devices such as the Logi Dock Flex now support auto-reserve on connect, with guests outside the tenant able to book from the touch screen, part of the broader Android-device consolidation into the Pro Management Portal.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- **Slack becomes Salesforce's default:** From June 15, new Salesforce orgs in Enterprise or Unlimited edition come with a Slack workspace already built and configured — no separate purchase or setup, with the collaboration layer arriving bundled with the CRM. This continues to reshape competitive dynamics against Teams in CRM-heavy accounts.
- **Cisco opens Zoom Rooms hardware market:** The public beta of Cisco Devices for Zoom Rooms started in June 2026, with general availability expected September 2026, positioning Zoom as a certified Cisco Device for Zoom Rooms running on Cisco hardware.
- **Cross-platform agentic AI is the industry theme:** Cisco's push is explicit that it wants one agentic workplace experience where the Director Agent doesn't care which meeting platform the call is on.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- **Audit Teams Rooms/panel inventory now:** With Android 10/11 certified-device management leaving the Teams Admin Center by 30 September, confirm which rooms need migration to the Pro Management Portal before functionality gaps appear.
- **Pilot Copilot Notebooks' new reference types** (Power BI, CSV/TSV, images) for exec reporting use cases — could reduce manual deck-building for Henrik's team.
- **Track competitive pressure:** Salesforce's Slack-by-default shift and Cisco's Zoom Rooms hardware entry both signal accelerating platform-agnostic collaboration — worth monitoring for any multi-vendor procurement conversations.
