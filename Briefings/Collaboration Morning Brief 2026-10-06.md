# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Tuesday, 6 October 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Microsoft's "What's New in Teams" cadence is shifting from monthly to quarterly reporting, with the latest roundup (published Oct 1) confirming What's New in Teams is shifting from a monthly to a quarterly rhythm, with the next edition due in December, March, and June.
- Microsoft is rolling out simplified meeting controls and a redesigned share panel to reduce mis-clicks between actions like Share, Leave, and Raise hand, with rollout completing by end of October 2026.
- Teams Live Chat is now retired — the live chat widget must be removed from websites by 5 October, with Microsoft pointing users to Dynamics 365 Contact Center or Copilot Studio instead.
- Security hardening continues: images containing QR codes from external users will be obscured by default in Teams messages, requiring users to deliberately reveal them, with GA in October 2026 across Android, desktop, iOS and Mac.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- Microsoft has split Copilot into two billing tracks as of Oct 1: a flat per-user licence for "everyday AI" and usage-based billing via Copilot Credits for frontier models, with admins needing to create spending policies before either activates.
- A new Copilot Chat experience is landing in Teams and Outlook, alongside Copilot in SharePoint and OneDrive going generally available — admins have until October 25 to review one of the most important related settings.
- Agent governance is maturing: Teams Admin Center now recommends agents for third-party apps already in use, with GitHub Copilot joining the chat and GA expected October 2026.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- Microsoft's Agent 365 framework is expanding partner agents directly into Teams — Figma's design workflows now integrate into Teams, enabling design review and feedback without leaving Microsoft 365, alongside new Miro, Box, Coursera, Genspark and Zensai integrations.
- Granular Conditional Access for Teams meetings is rolling out, giving admins the ability to apply security policies to selected sensitive meetings.
- Microsoft's partner program is realigning around AI: a new Agentic Business Solutions specialization consolidates Low Code Application Development and Intelligent Automation, reflecting how partners combine low-code, automation and Copilot Studio agent-building.

## Meetingroom Equipment
- Device management is consolidating: Teams Rooms Android devices, panels and phones are moving from the classic Teams Admin Center to the **Pro Management Portal**, per this week's UC Today coverage.
- Hardware lifecycle alert: Android 10/11 Teams devices lost certification on 3 September, affecting first-generation HP Poly, Logitech and Yealink models with no path to upgrade the Android version.
- New capability on the way: local Pan-tilt-zoom (PTZ) controls for compatible cameras from the in-room console, with feature enablement planned for October 2026; Android breakout-room support is also reaching GA this month.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- **WebexOne 2026** is underway in Austin (Oct 5–8) — Cisco is showcasing RoomOS supporting Webex, Microsoft Teams Rooms, Zoom Rooms and Google Meet on the same hardware, and announced a LeapXpert integration letting Webex users message clients on WhatsApp without leaving the platform.
- Zoom is pushing further into Cisco's hardware turf: Zoom brought native Zoom Rooms support to Cisco devices just a week ahead of WebexOne, intensifying room-hardware competition.
- Google Workspace shipped a new Confluence integration for Google Chat, bringing Atlassian project context directly into Chat conversations, while also rolling out reusable "skills" in Gemini to eventually replace Gems.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- **Review before Oct 25**: Confirm Copilot Credits/spending policies and the new admin-center authoritative content settings before the forced deadline.
- **Hardware audit**: Check your MTR estate for any first-gen Android 10/11 devices (HP Poly/Logitech/Yealink) that lost certification and plan refresh cycles.
- **Website check**: Verify the Teams Live Chat widget has been removed from any customer-facing sites (deadline already passed Oct 5) to avoid broken integrations.
