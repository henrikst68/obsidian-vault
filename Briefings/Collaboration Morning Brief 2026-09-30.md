# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Wednesday, 30 September 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Microsoft has rolled out a new admin policy that lets meeting organizers Microsoft has introduced a new Teams admin policy that allows organizers to prevent third-party bots from joining meetings without approval, alongside a related capability where Microsoft is rolling out a new Teams meeting protection policy that allows administrators to automatically block all identified external bots from joining Teams meetings.
- Admins should note a reporting delay: the following usage report types are delayed since September 19, 2026: Microsoft Teams usage reports, Group Activity reports, Mailbox usage reports, with Copilot Readiness and other reports delayed since September 23.
- Microsoft continues to hold its UCaaS leadership position, having been recognized as a Leader in the 2026 Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ for Unified Communications as a Service—our eighth consecutive year with this recognition.
- On the security side, Bleeping Computer flags that a previously unknown malware family dubbed SynkLoader is being distributed in Microsoft Teams phishing campaigns to steal credentials via a fake lock screen, and separately threat actors are impersonating IT support staff in Microsoft Teams calls to gain remote access to corporate devices and deploy Chaos ransomware.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- Dynamics 365 Sales is getting a major grounding upgrade: Dynamics 365 Sales adds Work IQ to ground Copilot and agents in business data, reusable skills, and governed actions across Dynamics 365 and Power Platform, available in preview starting September 30, 2026, with rollout continuing throughout October 2026.
- Copilot Cowork has moved to general availability with new cost controls — Copilot Cowork worldwide in general availability, bringing a full agentic experience for long-running, multi-tool work with new plugins, Edge web browsing, and stronger security, compliance, and cost management controls — and billing for it has now started, so admins should check spending caps.
- The Copilot connector ecosystem is expanding fast, with nine new partner plugins available now, including Enosix, Harvey, LSEG, Miro, monday.com, Moody's, Morningstar, S&P Global Energy, and TeamsMaestro, plus eight more coming soon.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- Agent 365 has landed several ecosystem partner agents directly inside Teams, including one where the Agent 365 integration brings Lucid's visual intelligence into Microsoft Teams, enabling teams to create and collaborate on diagrams and flowcharts in the flow of work, and collaborate on process maps and visual plans without leaving Microsoft 365.
- Miro is also now natively woven into Teams via Agent 365, letting users create and collaborate on Miro boards directly from Teams conversations and use Work IQ tool access to connect Miro workflows with Microsoft 365 data.
- SharePoint's Copilot capabilities got a September refresh, with reusable skills that follow you across SharePoint and OneDrive, Copilot able to measure and improve those skills with evaluations, and new guidance to help AI agents build live, SharePoint-safe experiences.

## Meetingroom Equipment
- IT teams should audit device fleets: Microsoft's Teams certification for first-gen HP Poly Studio X30, X50, X70, G7500, TC8 and the original Neat Bar and Neat Board ends 3 September 2026 - along with a handful of devices from Logitech, DTEN, Yealink and EPOS — rooms keep working but won't get new certification guarantees.
- Vendor rivalry is heating up ahead of Q4 refresh cycles: Yealink's newer AV ONE architecture and MVC line are being pitched for complex spaces, while Poly by HP wins the argument when the buyer wants room hardware, headsets, phones, lifecycle planning, and analytics pulled into one managed estate, backed by 2026 moves around Studio Room Compute, VideoOS 5.0/5.1, Poly Studio V12, and Poly Lens.
- For standardisation, Copilot is now embedded at the room level: Copilot for Teams Rooms adds meeting recap, speaker identification, real-time Q&A, insights, and follow-up automation — worth evaluating for exec boardrooms.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- Meta is pushing further into enterprise collaboration: it unveiled Muse for Small Business that connects its agent to popular software from companies like Asana, Zoom, Intuit, Box, Canva and Salesforce's Slack, and can also link to Meta ad accounts and professional Instagram and Facebook profiles — a notable AI-agent play into the SMB collaboration space.
- Slack rolled out one of its biggest AI/CRM upgrades at Dreamforce, giving a major AI and Salesforce upgrade with Slackforce Surfaces, Slackbot in Lightning Experience, Big Mode, and Slack Code — bringing interactive CRM data, team-based AI in channels, and live code collaboration.
- Webex has quietly improved its whiteboarding UX for hybrid meetings, adding follow controls so participants can stay with anyone's cursor and viewport as they move around the canvas, while presenters can bring everyone's attention to their current view.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- Review the Teams admin center bot/meeting-protection policies and consider enabling the new external-bot blocking feature given active phishing/impersonation campaigns (SynkLoader, IT-support impersonation ransomware).
- Audit meeting room fleets against the 3 September 2026 certification cutoff list (Poly X30/X50/X70/G7500/TC8, original Neat Bar/Board) to plan refresh budget for FY27.
- Track Copilot Cowork billing now that GA/billing has started — confirm spending limits are set before usage ramps across the org.
