# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Friday, 7 August 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Microsoft's August 2026 update cycle includes retirement dates for SMS and voice MFA, eight Copilot updates, five Teams updates, and a new certification programme for Teams Phone voice agents.
- Microsoft is preparing to roll out a new Efficiency Mode for Microsoft Teams for systems with limited CPU and memory resources to improve app responsiveness.
- Microsoft Teams is getting a dedicated Meeting Recap App this month, and IT teams need to prepare before it arrives.
- Security teams should note: initial access broker KongTuke has moved to Microsoft Teams for social engineering attacks, taking as little as five minutes to gain persistent access to corporate networks. Separately, a threat group tracked as UNC6692 uses social engineering to deploy a new custom malware suite named 'Snow.'

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- Microsoft closed out FY26 with 30 million Copilot seats and Azure up 43% — and OpenAI quietly became a subprocessor in Microsoft 365, with the toggle auto-enabled unless previously opted out.
- Notebooks in the Copilot app are rolling out early to mid-August with OneNote sync, while the existing OneNote experience remains unchanged.
- Copilot now surfaces rich images inline from files and meetings directly within responses to improve comprehension, rather than text-only output as before.

- Partner-facing: "Copilot in 30" combines a 25-user, 30-day Microsoft 365 Copilot Business trial for organizations under 300 employees, with a new Copilot Success Microskilling series available from August 3, 2026 to help partners drive adoption.
