# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Wednesday, 12 August 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Microsoft is preparing a new **Security Detection Report** in the Teams admin center that consolidates previously scattered threat signals into a single dashboard, covering impersonation attempts, malicious URLs, and weaponizable file types, with general availability now expected to start in late August 2026, with a global rollout completing by early September.
- Microsoft's August Partner Center update introduces a way to manage agents across customer and subsidiary tenants from a new, single experience in the Microsoft 365 admin center.
- Pricing housekeeping: as of July 1, Microsoft updated global pricing and packaging for M365 commercial suites, applying to new customers immediately and existing customers at renewal, and effective August 1, Microsoft updated its Partner Code of Conduct to clarify partner compliance expectations.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- Copilot's August wave adds new model choice: eight new features introduced in August 2026 include integration of advanced AI models like GPT-5.6 and Claude, which dynamically adapt to tasks such as generating detailed reports or concise summaries.
- **Copilot Notebooks** have graduated from limited preview: notebooks, previously limited to select users, are now accessible across all Microsoft 365 licenses, including commercial and educational plans, and Copilot now supports additional file types, including markdown (.md), text (.txt), and RTF files.
- Community roundups note Microsoft closed out FY26 with 30 million Copilot seats and Azure up 43%, and flag that OpenAI became a subprocessor in Microsoft 365, with the toggle auto-enabled unless you'd already said no — worth a governance check.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- New AI meeting artefact rolling out: AI meeting archives (.meeting) generate an AI summary file for every eligible meeting, stored in tenant-owned SharePoint Embedded, not user-accessible, with a default five-year retention — Henrik may want to review the retention default before it lands in September.
- Copilot mobile can now capture in-person meetings by default: Copilot mobile records in-person meetings on by default, sending audio to the user's OneDrive with transcript and insights, inheriting OneDrive permissions and Purview governance.
- Anthropic deepened its M365 footprint: Anthropic consolidated to a single Claude for Microsoft 365 add-in, and their M365 connector gained write tools — email, calendar, SharePoint files.

## Meetingroom Equipment
- Certified MTR hardware remains concentrated among Poly, Yealink, Logitech, Jabra, Crestron, and Neat, with buyers advised that only Microsoft-certified hardware is supported for Teams Rooms.
- Fresh vendor comparison: Poly added Mission and Focus 6 headsets plus new room compute, while Yealink expanded its Teams room architecture, MP E2 phone roadmap, and YMCS integration with Microsoft Places.
- On room design breadth, Yealink's portfolio stretches across MeetingBar, MVC S80, MVC S98, SmartVision 80, MeetingBoard Pro, MTower, Teams-certified phones and headsets, and ProAV-style room builds, while Poly leans on its HP-integrated device/analytics stack.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- Salesforce is pushing deeper into collaboration: Salesforce Summer '26 makes Slack the default for all new Enterprise and Unlimited orgs — from June 15, a new org comes with a Slack workspace already built and configured, no separate purchase or setup. This directly challenges Teams' position as the default workplace hub for CRM-heavy shops.
- Salesforce frames this as replacing Chatter: Salesforce describes the change as the final piece needed to replace Chatter, the collaboration layer it launched in 2010, with Slack as the default — Chatter isn't being removed, but new organisations will work from Slack first.
- Security researchers continue flagging Teams as an attack surface across the industry: threat actors increasingly abused Microsoft Teams during Q2 to deliver social engineering, phishing, and malware payloads, since Teams traffic typically bypasses secure email gateways.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- Track the new **Teams Security Detection Report** GA (late August) and plan admin-center review for impersonation/malicious URL/file detections.
- Assess governance impact of OpenAI's new subprocessor status in M365 and the GPT-5.6/Claude model rollout in Copilot before enabling broadly.
- If any business units run Salesforce alongside Teams, flag the Slack-by-default change for new orgs to avoid tool sprawl or duplicate collaboration surfaces.
