# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Tuesday, 28 July 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- A fresh outage hit Teams and Microsoft 365 on 27 July, with reports first surging around 9:28AM Eastern Time on Downdetector; status trackers logged the service as operational again by 7:41 PM UTC, though 11 user-submitted outage reports were logged in the preceding 24 hours.
- This follows last week's confirmed root cause for the 23 July disruption: Microsoft's preliminary post-incident review attributed connectivity failures in the West US region to a bug in a maintenance-request conversion system that removed more network routes than intended.
- Security guidance this week continues to flag Teams-based social engineering: Microsoft guidance continues to call out phishing and impersonation risks in Teams, especially from external chats and first-contact scenarios, as attackers no longer depend only on email.
- The redesigned meeting controls and share-panel overhaul are actively rolling out now, with rollout starting July 2026 and completion by end of August 2026.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- Momentum continues around Copilot Cowork, Microsoft's agentic AI layer for Microsoft 365 — now positioned as an agent that turns broad business goals into multi-step execution plans, continuing work in the background and stopping at checkpoints for human approval.
- New frontier models have landed inside Copilot this month: two new frontier models — OpenAI's GPT-5.6 and Anthropic's Claude Sonnet 5 — arrived in Copilot in July 2026.
- Governance is expanding alongside capability: Microsoft Copilot added governed agent publishing, tenant-wide prompt galleries, and broader MCP agent access across Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook and Catalyst, while improving connector governance and nested permissions.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- Copilot Studio now lets teams push adoption directly into Teams: agents can be shared straight to a Teams team with a notification to the main channel and an install link, closing the adoption gap between "an agent exists" and "my team knows about it".
- Planner is being folded into the agentic layer — Planner capabilities are coming to Copilot Cowork, letting users view and update plans, buckets, goals and tasks and carry out work across Microsoft 365 with approval, targeted for GA in September 2026.
- Admins get tighter oversight of AI risk in Teams: Teams has added an Organization Trust Score for apps and agents, easier access to unsent drafts, and simplified external collaboration controls in the Teams Admin Center.

## Meetingroom Equipment
- Nothing significant published in the last two business days; the most recent notable item remains Microsoft's Express Install standardized MTR deployment program, which continues to be promoted for its speed and cost savings but predates this reporting window.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- Slack shipped an MCP-based upgrade: Slack added the Slackbot MCP client (now GA) to connect apps and shared channels in one conversational workspace, with a partner ecosystem of 20+ apps including Salesforce, Canva, Linear and Zoom.
- Zoom pushed routine hardening updates: Zoom Workplace shipped a security enhancement for its desktop app across Windows, macOS and Linux.
- The bigger structural story remains Salesforce's push to make Slack the default collaboration layer for new customers, reshaping competitive dynamics against Teams in CRM-adjacent workflows.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- Review external/guest access and MFA policies given ongoing Teams-based phishing and impersonation warnings — this is a live, recurring risk theme flagged again this week.
- Track the July outage pattern (23 July and 27 July) for any SLA/incident review implications if Henrik's org was affected.
- Watch for the Planner-in-Cowork GA (targeted September 2026) and the Teams-channel agent-sharing rollout, both relevant if Henrik is planning Copilot governance or adoption campaigns.
