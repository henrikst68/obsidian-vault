# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Monday, 10 August 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Microsoft is rolling out a new **in-meeting "Report" button** in August 2026 that lets participants flag security issues (e.g., AI deepfake impersonation) so IT/security teams can intervene immediately, alongside a redesigned meeting UI to reduce accidental screen shares and meeting exits.
- Microsoft is retiring Teams Live Chat on October 5, about 18 months after launch, as it looks to focus on newer customer engagement experiences; new setups were already blocked on August 6 and admins have been told to pull the widget from their sites.
- Microsoft was again named a Leader in the 2026 Gartner Magic Quadrant for Unified Communications as a Service — its eighth consecutive year with this recognition, reinforcing Teams' UCaaS leadership position.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- Microsoft's FY26 close showed 30 million Copilot seats and Azure revenue up 43%, while OpenAI quietly became a subprocessor in Microsoft 365 with the toggle auto-enabled unless previously opted out — worth a governance/data-residency check given EU Data Boundary wording changes.
- Copilot in SharePoint added the ability to generate an interactive HTML dashboard directly from a list that stays connected to the underlying data, cutting manual report rebuilding.
- Copilot continues its shift from chatbot to orchestration layer, with new GPT-5.6 models and app-native actions across Word, PowerPoint, Excel, Teams, SharePoint, Notebooks and Cowork turning it into a system of agents and skills rather than a single chat box.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- Microsoft's partner motion **"Copilot in 30"** — a 25-user, 30-day Copilot Business trial bundled with adoption guidance for orgs under 300 employees — gets a new mid-August customer evaluation tool with industry-tailored prompts.
- The August partner update also flags retirement dates for SMS and voice MFA, plus a new certification programme for Teams Phone voice agents, relevant for telephony/contact-centre partners.
- MTR-certified hardware ecosystem remains active, with vendors like Yealink offering standardized MTR deployment across focus rooms, training rooms and boardrooms with centralized management and enterprise-grade security.

## Meetingroom Equipment
- Yealink continues expanding its Teams Rooms portfolio, supporting bulk device management via Microsoft Teams Admin Center, MTR Portal Management Platform, Zoom Device Platform, and Yealink Management Cloud Service.
- AudioCodes' certified Teams devices emphasize noise-canceling microphones, high-fidelity speakers, and plug-and-play setup for meeting rooms and desk phones.
- Broader Teams device catalog (Lenovo, Logitech, HP) continues to ship AI-enabled cameras and docking monitors certified for Teams, per Microsoft's devices hub.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- Slack rolled out several UX updates including a merged Omniswitcher that puts navigation and search together via a single CMD+K search-first experience, plus scheduled status, canvas tables of contents, and temporary channel joins.
- Slack also introduced admin controls to manage access to future AI features for Workspace Primary Owners on Pro, Business+, and Enterprise Select plans.
- Google Workspace is expanding **Gemini in Classroom** to all-age K-12/higher-ed students starting August 10, and continues layering Gemini into Docs, Sheets, and Meet (e.g., automatically capturing screenshots of presented content directly into Meet notes).
- Zoom's AI Companion continues aggressive expansion — AI Companion 3.0 is now integrated across Zoom Workplace, Zoom Business Services and Workvivo, with monthly active users more than tripling year-over-year in Q4 FY26.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- Review the **OpenAI-as-subprocessor** default toggle in the M365 admin center — confirm whether opt-out is still in place for Henrik's tenant given the auto-enable change.
- Audit any embedded **Teams Live Chat widgets** on company websites before the October 5 retirement to avoid service disruption.
- Watch for the **Copilot in 30 evaluation tool** (mid-August) if considering a Copilot Business trial rollout for smaller teams/departments.
