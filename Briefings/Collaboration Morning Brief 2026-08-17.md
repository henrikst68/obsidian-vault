# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Monday, 17 August 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Microsoft was recognized as a Leader in the 2026 Gartner Magic Quadrant for Unified Communications as a Service for the eighth consecutive year, positioned highest on Ability to Execute and furthest on Completeness of Vision.
- Teams has rolled out a new **room builder tool** in the Pro Management portal: a visually engaging tool to help IT managers design and configure Teams Rooms traditional, signature, and flex meeting spaces, enabling faster design, standards creation, and rollout with simplified device and license selection.
- The **default profanity filter for live captions** is being switched off tenant-wide: Microsoft is updating the default profanity filter setting for live captions from On to Off across Teams meetings, calls, events, and Microsoft Teams Rooms devices to improve accessibility and align with EU requirements.
- Teams mobile now supports **multi-line calling**: users can make and receive calls using multiple assigned phone numbers directly from their mobile device, with unified call history and seamless switching across lines.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- Microsoft closed FY26 with strong Copilot momentum — 30 million Copilot seats and Azure up 43% — while quietly making OpenAI a subprocessor in Microsoft 365, with the toggle enabled by default.
- **Copilot mobile** now records in-person meetings by default: audio goes to the user's OneDrive with transcript and insights, inheriting OneDrive permissions and Purview governance; admins can disable org-wide via Intune. A related AI meeting archive feature stores an AI-generated summary file for every eligible meeting in tenant-owned SharePoint Embedded, with a default five-year retention that admins should review before it lands in September.
- Copilot's August feature wave includes GPT-5.6 and Claude model integration that dynamically adapts to tasks like generating reports or summaries, plus expanded Copilot notebooks now available across all Microsoft 365 licenses, integrating with OneNote for brainstorming and collaboration.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- Teams Rooms management is consolidating: administrators can now use the Pro Management portal to monitor device health, manage inventory, and access analytics for both Windows and Android devices, with management moving from the Teams admin center to the Pro Management portal.
- A new **Teams Phone voice-agent certification programme** was flagged this month alongside five Teams updates and eight Copilot updates, signalling deeper AI-agent integration into telephony workflows.
- Anthropic has consolidated its Microsoft 365 presence: Anthropic consolidated to a single Claude for Microsoft 365 add-in, and their M365 connector gained write tools — email, calendar, SharePoint files.

## Meetingroom Equipment
- Teams Rooms on Windows is preparing **IntelliFrame people labels**, which will identify in-room participants by name and show contact cards for remote attendees, rolling out worldwide in September 2026 and requiring Teams Rooms Pro licenses with voice and face recognition enrollment.
- BYOD meeting spaces get improved oversight: administrators gain new reporting and device health insights in the Teams Pro Management portal, with options to opt out of data collection, and users can disable automatic meeting room mode.
- Nothing significant today from major hardware OEMs (Poly, Yealink, Logitech, HP) beyond routine certification updates.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- Gartner's newly published 2026 UCaaS Magic Quadrant shows a settled field: Cisco, Microsoft, RingCentral and Zoom hold onto their Leader positions, exactly as in 2025, while 8×8, Dialpad and GoTo remain Visionaries. Notably, Google and Sangoma, both rated Niche Players a year ago, have dropped out entirely.
- Gartner has tempered its telephony-consolidation forecast: the prediction that 90% of organisations would run enterprise calling through cloud office/collaboration tools by 2028 has been revised down to 70%, suggesting slower UC platform convergence than expected.
- Zoom is leaning into its cross-platform AI assistant: ZoomMate now captures and connects conversations across Zoom Meetings, Phone, Chat, Microsoft Teams, Google Meet and Slack, helping users prepare for meetings and move to execution with deliverables grounded in actual decisions.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- **Review the live captions profanity-filter default change** before it reaches your tenants — it flips from On to Off unless already configured, per EU accessibility alignment.
- **Audit the AI meeting archive retention default** (5-year, stored in tenant-owned SharePoint Embedded) ahead of its September rollout — decide if this meets your data-governance policy.
- **Track the Teams Rooms IntelliFrame rollout (Sept 2026)** — plan licensing (Teams Rooms Pro) and enrolment for voice/face recognition if hybrid meeting equity is a priority for your rooms.
