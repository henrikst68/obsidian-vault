# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Thursday, 24 September 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Microsoft is finishing the rollout of a new admin policy that lets IT automatically block identified external meeting bots instead of relying on organizer approval; targeted release began mid-September 2026 with worldwide general availability now expected between early and late October 2026.
- Microsoft has been recognized as a Leader in the 2026 Gartner Magic Quadrant for Unified Communications as a Service — its eighth consecutive year with this recognition.
- Teams experienced another brief availability blip this week: Microsoft Teams was reportedly down for hundreds of users on September 21, 2026, with DownDetector reports surging around 9:10 a.m. Eastern.
- The **Queues app** continues rolling out, turning call queues into a shared workspace where calling representatives and supervisors work the same set of customer conversations together in Teams.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- **Facilitator** is getting more proactive: when a factual question goes unanswered during a meeting, Facilitator can now run a Copilot web search and post the answer directly into the meeting chat, without anyone asking it to. It requires Microsoft 365 Copilot Premium, must be switched on manually per meeting, and is designed to fire less than once per meeting.
- **Copilot Cowork** reached worldwide general availability with expanded model choice — OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra joined the frontier models available in Copilot Cowork and Copilot Studio — plus nine new partner plugins including Miro, monday.com, and Morningstar, with eight more coming soon.
- Copilot in SharePoint's September update opened a new **co-build program**, inviting customers to nominate high-value SharePoint scenarios that could benefit from Copilot to help shape the solution with Microsoft's product team.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- **Workflows for Teams** now supports multi-step automation, letting users design automated processes that perform several actions in sequence after a single trigger — covering scenarios such as collecting information, sending notifications, updating systems and routing requests.
- **LinkedIn Hiring Assistant** is now natively integrated in Teams, helping streamline candidate review and hiring manager collaboration.
- Viva/SharePoint tie-in: a new AI-generated audio overview lets users listen to a summary of organisational news rather than reading the full post via the SharePoint app in Teams (Viva Connections).

## Meetingroom Equipment
- **Yealink** continues expanding its fourth-generation MVC lineup; the MCore Kit serves as the core component of Yealink's fourth-generation MVC system, featuring a high-performance mini-PC and touch panel designed for video conference rooms.
- **MAXHUB** launched an entry-level option for hybrid spaces: the MAXHUB XCore Kit is a certified entry-level Microsoft Teams solution that includes a console and a Windows-based computer, aimed at focus and small BYOD rooms.
- A known device issue is still being tracked: some users of HP DirectConnect mode on Poly Microsoft Teams Rooms on Android (MTRA) devices may experience call drops — worth a check if your estate uses Poly/HP room kits.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- Salesforce's Summer '26 release continues reshaping the competitive picture: from June 15, a new Salesforce org in Enterprise or Unlimited edition comes with a Slack workspace already built and configured — no separate purchase or setup, with the collaboration layer arriving with the CRM, positioning Slack as a default challenger inside Teams-standardized enterprises.
- **Zoom** is deepening AI Companion's reach into rival ecosystems: Zoom's "My notes" now works across third-party meeting platforms like Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and WebEx, and users can connect Gmail and Outlook accounts to AI Companion 3.0 on the web to use emails as context for questions and summaries.
- Market analysts note fragmentation rather than consolidation: the five dominant enterprise messaging platforms in 2026 are Slack, Microsoft Teams, Zoom Team Chat, Cisco Webex, and Google Chat, and most enterprises aren't choosing one platform — they're running two or more simultaneously.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- Pilot and communicate the **bot-blocking meeting policy** before it defaults into wider GA in October — review current "Manage external bots" settings now.
- Decide on governance for **Facilitator's proactive web-search** feature before enabling org-wide; note it posts to chat for all attendees, including unlicensed external guests.
- Monitor Poly/HP MTRA "DirectConnect" call-drop issue if your estate uses those room devices, and keep an eye on Salesforce/Slack bundling as a competitive signal for any dual-platform strategy.
