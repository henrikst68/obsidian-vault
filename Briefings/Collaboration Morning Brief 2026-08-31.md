# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Monday, 31 August 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- A new standalone **Communicator app** for org-wide announcements is entering Public Preview and GA simultaneously worldwide from mid-August 2026, based on Message Center update MC1459143 (published 21 Aug). It's built specifically for organisation-wide announcements like safety alerts, compliance notifications, and training updates, with Public Preview and General Availability both beginning worldwide in mid-August 2026.
- Teams suffered a multi-feature outage on 26 August, with Microsoft's last status update at 7:26 a.m. IST noting it would continue monitoring for 15–30 minutes; the incident was later marked "largely mitigated." A separate advisory notes some shared-channel "Copy Link" URLs remain malformed after a recent update.
- Teams has added admin and calling updates, bringing user-reported security signals into the Teams admin center, enabling Teams Phone user multi-line with up to 10 numbers per user, and a simpler 8-digit numeric-only meeting passcode option.
- Heads-up for admins: Microsoft's Teams certification for first-gen Poly Studio X30/X50/X70, G7500, TC8 and original Neat Bar/Board ends 3 September 2026, alongside select Logitech, DTEN and Yealink devices.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- The latest Copilot release wave (11–25 August) adds **Python-powered editing in Excel**, letting Copilot execute Python code for advanced analysis directly in workbooks. Updates released between August 11 and August 25, 2026 include the ability to use Python when Editing with Copilot in Excel, executing Python code for advanced analysis, automation, and data transformation with results output directly in the workbook.
- Copilot's grounding sources are expanding into Viva Engage: Viva Engage content from private communities and events will now be used as a grounding source for Microsoft 365 Copilot, with users only able to see content they are privileged to access.
- Model choice is broadening — Microsoft is rolling out GPT-5 in Copilot Chat and adding support for Anthropic models, with a phased release reaching Microsoft 365 Copilot license holders first.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- Google has extended calendar interoperability across ecosystems: Google Calendar improvements make it easier to join third-party video meetings, including Microsoft Teams, Zoom, and Cisco Webex, when collaborating across different calendar and email clients, rolling out from 27 August.
- Salesforce launched **Slack Code** on 24 August — a new surface environment turning AI software development into a collaborative, multiplayer activity, letting cross-functional teams work with AI coding agents including Claude Code, Devin, GitHub Copilot, and ChatGPT in a shared space rather than isolated browser tabs.
- Salesforce and Anthropic announced **Claudeforce** (26 August), an expanded strategic partnership bringing Claude's intelligence and reasoning together with Salesforce's enterprise harness across data, workflows, and governance.

## 🖥️ Meeting Room Equipment
- Certification cliff approaching: Teams certification for first-gen Poly Studio X30/X50/X70, G7500, TC8, and the original Neat Bar/Board ends 3 September 2026, though rooms won't switch off — Teams keeps working, backed by two more years of Microsoft support, but new certification guarantees stop.
- Vendor rivalry intensifying: HP's 2026 Poly push centers on Studio Room Compute, VideoOS 5.0 (with 5.1 forthcoming), Poly Studio V12, Poly Lens, and new Focus 6/Mission headsets, while Yealink expanded its Teams room architecture, MP E2 phone roadmap, and YMCS integration with Microsoft Places.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- Zoom's AI branding continues to evolve post-rename: ZoomMate, launched 1 June 2026, is Zoom's new agentic AI product tier building on the $12 Custom AI Companion add-on, starting at $20 per user per month in North America with 2,200 AI credits per user per month.
- Salesforce flagged continued momentum from AI/Slack/Agentforce: Salesforce's Deputy CFO said Q2 performance and updated outlook reflect execution against its strategy, including returning to faster organic growth in H2 of the fiscal year.
- Competitive landscape remains fragmented: the five dominant enterprise messaging platforms in 2026 are Slack, Microsoft Teams, Zoom Team Chat, Cisco Webex, and Google Chat, each with a distinct primary use case — Slack for engineering/ChatOps, Teams dominating M365-standardized enterprises, Webex for Cisco-stack/government, and Google Chat for Workspace orgs.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- **Audit MTR hardware estate** ahead of the 3 September certification expiry for first-gen Poly/Neat/Logitech devices — no immediate outage risk, but plan refresh budget.
- **Monitor Teams service health** — two outages in the past week (26 Aug feature disruption, screensharing degradation) suggest a rockier-than-usual patch cycle; keep fallback conferencing options ready.
- **Watch Slack Code and Claudeforce** as competitive signals — Salesforce is aggressively pushing agentic coding and CRM-AI integration into Slack, a space Teams/Copilot will likely need to respond to.
