# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Thursday, 17 September 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- **Fresh service degradation:** Microsoft confirmed a fresh service degradation affecting its Microsoft 365 suite, with users worldwide reporting an inability to access core applications since the evening of September 16, 2026, tracked as MO1472904. This follows a turbulent year with a multi-day Exchange Online authentication failure in late August and a networking misconfiguration in July that disrupted Teams and SharePoint across North America — worth flagging to IT if Henrik's org has seen intermittent access issues this morning.
- **Device rebrand:** Microsoft is now rebranding Rooms, desks, panels and phones collectively as "intelligent spaces," alongside a rollout of passwordless Entra resource accounts for Windows and Android Rooms devices, panels and phones.
- **Admin deadline approaching:** If you manage Teams Rooms on Android, Teams panels or Teams phones from the Teams Admin Center, that stops by 30 September — device management is moving fully to the Pro Management Portal.
- **Teams Live Chat sunset:** Microsoft is retiring Teams Live Chat in October 2026, just months after expanding it, pointing customers to Copilot Studio instead.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- **Facilitator goes proactive:** For the first time, Copilot in Teams has waited to be asked — open the panel, type a question, get an answer — but this month that starts to change: Facilitator can now act without being prompted, and outside agents are getting an official route into Teams through the admin centre. Note: once Facilitator is enabled, its answers appear in the meeting chat for everyone present, including external and cross-tenant attendees who may not hold a Copilot licence — a governance point worth flagging.
- **Third-party agents in meetings:** GitHub's Copilot and Atlassian's Rovo agent can now investigate a problem in the background while a meeting continues, with everyone able to see its progress and steer it, or pull ticket context into a Teams chat, signalling Teams becoming a hub for external AI agents.
- **Sharing controls added:** New Session and Response Sharing in M365 Copilot let users share Copilot chat content through a link — Session Sharing shares a full chat session, while Response Sharing shares a single Copilot response.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- **UCaaS recognition:** Microsoft has been recognized as a Leader in the 2026 Gartner Magic Quadrant for Unified Communications as a Service — its eighth consecutive year with this recognition.
- **SharePoint/Copilot skills portability:** Reusable skills can now follow users across SharePoint and OneDrive, Copilot can measure and improve those skills with evaluations, and new guidance helps AI agents build live, SharePoint-safe experiences.
- **Entra tightens Teams device security:** Microsoft Entra Resource Accounts for Teams Devices deliver a secure, passwordless identity model for Teams Rooms, Panels, Common Area Phones and other shared devices, replacing legacy password-based authentication with hardware-protected, device-bound credentials.

## Meetingroom Equipment
- **Poly vs Yealink race continues:** Poly added Mission and Focus 6 headsets plus new room compute, while Yealink expanded its Teams room architecture, MP E2 phone roadmap, and YMCS integration with Microsoft Places.
- **Passwordless rollout hits hardware partners:** The shift to Entra resource accounts affects all certified MTR vendors (Logitech, Poly, Yealink, Crestron, Neat), with a migration wizard and progress dashboard in the Pro Management Portal available now across worldwide, GCC and GCC High tenants to help admins move existing accounts across.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- **Cisco's best quarter in years:** Cisco's Collaboration business had its best quarter in seven years in Q4 FY26, with Webex, devices and contact center all growing.
- **Slack doubles down on agentic AI:** Slack's September release centers on AI collaboration, bringing code channels, new Agent and Slackbot workspaces, deeper research, and tighter connections to Salesforce, Seismic, Lists, and Workflow Builder so teams and agents can work in one shared context.
- **Zoom Workplace refresh:** Zoom rolled out a refreshed, simplified Workplace app look, an enhanced in-meeting experience with a simplified toolbar, and a cleaner Zoom Chat design consistent across desktop, mobile, and web.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- **Monitor MO1472904** in the Microsoft 365 admin center for the ongoing service degradation reported since last night; confirm no impact on Henrik's tenant.
- **Prepare for the 30 September Teams Admin Center cutover** — ensure Android Rooms/panels/phones are migrated to the Pro Management Portal ahead of the deadline.
- **Set a governance policy on Facilitator's proactive web search** before enabling it broadly, given its answers are visible to external/cross-tenant attendees without a Copilot licence.
