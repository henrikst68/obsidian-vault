# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Thursday, 5 November 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Microsoft is rolling out AI-powered, context-aware phishing detection for Teams chats from new external contacts, targeting fake IT help-desk scams; Targeted Release begins in early November and runs until the middle of the month, with worldwide general availability following in early to mid-December, and admins won't need to change any settings to switch it on.
- Most of the protection Teams has offered against phishing so far has focused on what a message carries, scanning links and attachments for known threats; the new capability looks instead at what the conversation is trying to get the user to do.
- Hyland launched a new Teams integration that lets users access, share, and capture content stored in OnBase or Nuxeo repositories without leaving the Teams interface, aimed at regulated industries needing strict document governance.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- Microsoft confirmed updates to the Copilot app's Home and navigation: a new Home integrating Chat, Cowork, Projects, and Workspaces, where Projects organize related tasks and files without storing content, while Workspaces replace Notebooks for team collaboration, with rollout starting late October 2026.
- Copilot will now proactively nudge users into the right mode — Microsoft 365 Copilot will proactively suggest switching to Cowork if it determines that mode is better suited for the request, announced in Message Center on November 3, 2026 — with no administrator settings required and rollout scheduled for mid-to-late November 2026.
- This week's roadmap roundup flagged that Federated Copilot Connectors (MCP type) currently let Microsoft 365 Copilot read data from third-party services in real time, and the update unblocks connector tools that create, update, and delete content in those services within Copilot Chat, plus a new Copilot-powered SharePoint Site Builder for easy site creation.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- 8x8 secured Microsoft certification to simplify global Teams calling, giving channel partners Direct Routing and Operator Connect under one contract, bill and management experience, with onboarding expanding through Q4 2026.
- Yealink marked its 25th anniversary by hosting a Munich summit where industry leaders from Microsoft, Qualcomm, AVIXA, Frost & Sullivan and CyberIntelligence explored the "New System of Work," with Qualcomm highlighting on-device AI evolving cameras from capture to understanding.
- Admins should note a critical vulnerability disclosed this week: CVE-2026-122120 is a critical-severity vulnerability rated 9/10 on the CVSS scale, where Yealink Plug-in Softwares before version 2.35.219.0 has an unauthorized file write flaw exploitable for remote code execution.

## Meetingroom Equipment
- Cisco confirmed that when RoomOS 27 ships in November, Cisco devices will run Webex, Microsoft Teams, Zoom and Google Meet — continuing its multi-platform room strategy unveiled at WebexOne 2026.
- Australian distributor Bluechip began rolling out Spacera's Apollo meeting-room operations platform, which supports Microsoft Teams Rooms, Zoom Rooms, Cisco Webex and Google Meet environments, with integrations spanning leading AV vendors including Neat, Poly, Logitech, Yealink, Cisco and Crestron.
- No major new MTR-certified hardware launches from Logitech or Poly in the last 48 hours — Logitech's "biggest enterprise launch ever" (Rally Bar 2/Rally System 2 Pro) remains the most recent flagship news but is slightly outside the window.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- Slack highlighted a notable AI-adoption milestone, reporting that agents are increasingly active in its platform: through open standards like MCP, agents can pull context from Slack across platforms like GitHub, Salesforce, and Jira to take direct action in real time.
- Google Workspace pushed a major feature wave this week, adding Gmail Live and Docs Live for voice-based interaction, live speech translation in 63 languages on Google Meet, and Markdown support in Docs and Drive, alongside faster Microsoft 365 migrations.
- Google also clarified its education AI stance after admin backlash, confirming Gemini, Gemini Notebook, Gemini in Classroom and Google Search will be "entirely off for K-12 student accounts until school administrators explicitly turn them on."

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- Prepare comms/training ahead of the Teams AI phishing-detection rollout (early Nov targeted release, GA by mid-December) — no admin config needed, but good to flag to security teams.
- If running Yealink endpoints, check firmware against CVE-2026-122120 (critical RCE flaw) and patch to 2.35.219.0+ promptly.
- Track the Copilot "Home/Cowork/Projects/Workspaces" consolidation rolling out through November — licensing and usage-based billing implications for Copilot users should be reviewed before deferred release in late November.
