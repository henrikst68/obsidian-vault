# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Wednesday, 29 July 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Microsoft confirmed Teams, SharePoint and other Microsoft 365 users reported disruption on 23–24 July 2026 during an Azure West US network incident, with connectivity failures and latency between 14:44 and 19:41 UTC on 23 July, caused by a bug in a maintenance-request conversion system that removed more network routes than intended.
- A new admin policy shipped via PowerShell: Teams released a new version of their PowerShell module on July 19, containing WAM support and cmdlets to limit federated chat for specific users and tenants.
- Rolling out this month: a private chat for organizers, co-organizers and presenters in meetings, webinars and town halls, letting them communicate before/during/after an event without attendees seeing it. Also, Planner is now available as a tab in Teams Shared and Private channels, letting users create or add a plan directly within a channel.
- Device/app governance tightened: admins can now manage Teams Core agents like Facilitator from a dedicated experience in the Teams admin center, controlling availability by org, user or group.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- Big model refresh: two new frontier models — OpenAI's GPT-5.6 and Anthropic's Claude Sonnet 5 — arrived in Copilot this month, alongside Copilot Notebooks now able to turn notes into a Word document, Excel spreadsheet or PowerPoint deck.
- Governance flag for Henrik's tenant admins: OpenAI-operated models are tenant-controlled — eligible commercial tenants become enabled for all users from 24 July 2026 unless admins opt out under Copilot → Settings → AI providers operating as Microsoft subprocessors.
- Agent lifecycle governance expanded: customers can now submit agents built in Agent Builder to the Agent Store under "Built by your org," with admin review/approval in the M365 Admin Center enabling orgs to share validated agents at scale while maintaining quality and governance.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- Device management consolidation: management of Android-based Teams Rooms, Teams panels, and Teams phones is moving from the Teams admin center to the Pro Management portal for a consistent, secure experience across devices and manufacturers.
- Salesforce deepened Slack's CRM angle: Slack now supports email domain verification for Slack CRM so customer support emails come from a familiar address rather than a system-generated one, alongside new AI Guardrails to protect native AI features with content safety filters for search and Slackbot.
- Microsoft's specialization/partner track is shifting: Microsoft is updating the Copilot specialization to better align with market demand, renaming it the Microsoft 365 Copilot specialization with refreshed skilling and validation requirements.

## Meetingroom Equipment
- Nothing significant in the last 48 hours specific to MTR hardware vendors (Yealink, Logitech, Jabra, MAXHUB, AudioCodes) — most vendor news is from earlier in the year. The main room-equipment item this week is Microsoft's own admin-side consolidation: room/device management shifting into the Pro Management portal (see Ecosystem section above).

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- New competitive entrant: Jack Dorsey launched Buzz, a new open-source group chat platform positioned as a direct competitor to Salesforce's Slack, aiming to combine human conversations with AI agents in a single workflow-focused interface, reported just two days ago.
- Zoom pushed further into AI-driven sales tooling: Zoom announced general availability of Sales Assist, Ask ZRA, and Sales Roleplay for Zoom Revenue Accelerator, delivering AI-powered guidance before, during and after every customer engagement.
- Zoom also made an APAC leadership move, appointing Carlos Quaderi as Head of Asia Pacific (APAC) to drive its next phase of AI-powered growth, signaling continued regional investment against Teams/Webex.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- **Review the Azure West US post-incident report** once finalized — the 23–24 July routing bug affected Teams/SharePoint availability and it's worth confirming no lingering impact on your tenant's region.
- **Check the OpenAI-subprocessor toggle** in Copilot admin settings before the 24 July default-enablement date fully rolls out across your tenant, if not already reviewed.
- **Watch Buzz vs. Slack** — a new open-source entrant from Jack Dorsey could reshape competitive dynamics in chat tooling; worth monitoring for enterprise traction signals over the coming weeks.
