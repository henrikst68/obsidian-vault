# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Tuesday, 29 September 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Microsoft's external meeting-bot blocking policy is in its final push: targeted release begins mid-September 2026 and is expected to complete by late September 2026, with general availability worldwide beginning in early October 2026. Admins should pilot this before flipping it on org-wide.
- The last officially acknowledged Teams outage was on September 22, 2026, with no user-submitted reports of outages in the past 24 hours as of the latest check — service currently stable.
- Microsoft continues rolling out passwordless resource accounts for Teams Rooms and phones: Entra passwordless resource accounts for Rooms (Windows and Android), panels and phones are rolling out from August, with a migration wizard and progress dashboard in the Pro Management Portal, across worldwide, GCC and GCC High.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- Copilot Cowork has moved from preview to paid: billing for Copilot Cowork begins today, and before usage ramps, admins can set spending limits and allocate budgets using cost management controls. Nine partner plugins (including Miro, monday.com, and Moody's) are now live.
- A new "Work IQ" layer is about to hit preview: Dynamics 365 Sales adds Work IQ to ground Copilot and agents in business data, reusable skills, and governed actions, available in preview starting September 30, 2026, with rollout continuing throughout October 2026 — worth watching for Teams/Copilot chat implications.
- SharePoint's Copilot update is also emphasizing reusable skills: reusable skills can now follow users across SharePoint and OneDrive, with Copilot able to measure and improve those skills with evaluations.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- Salesforce and Slack deepened their AI tie-up at Dreamforce: Slack released a major AI and Salesforce upgrade with Slackforce Surfaces, Slackbot in Lightning Experience, Big Mode, and Slack Code, bringing interactive CRM data, team-based AI in channels, and live code collaboration.
- Cisco is opening its hardware to rival platforms: the public beta of Cisco Devices for Zoom Rooms started in June 2026, with general availability expected in September 2026, alongside enhanced Google Meet support on the same hardware.
- Zoom's AI Companion is widening its data reach: users can now pull data from up to 10 third-party apps—including Slack, Salesforce, Workday, and Seismic—directly into AI Companion queries, so answers reach into the tools where work actually lives.

## Meetingroom Equipment
- New MTR-certified hardware just landed: Nureva and AudioCodes received Microsoft Teams Rooms certification for a joint Android-based solution designed for medium-sized meeting spaces, combining Nureva's Microphone Mist audio with AudioCodes' PTZ camera and room controller.
- IT teams should note a certification cliff: Microsoft ended certification for Teams Rooms devices running Android 10 and 11 on September 3, 2026, and while they're expected to keep working for a limited time, many first-generation devices aren't future-proof.
- Channel data shows a shifting vendor landscape: Logitech, Cisco, Neat, Yealink, Q-SYS, and Shure are the vendors gaining channel momentum, while HP Poly, Crestron, Barco, Jabra, and EPOS are losing ground.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- Slack has been reclassified in a key analyst category: the addition of new capabilities such as meeting-triggered actions and background tasks moved Slack into the Enterprise AI Assistants category, dominated by Zoom, Webex, Teams, and Google Workspace.
- Multi-platform enterprise reality is now the norm rather than the exception: the five dominant enterprise messaging platforms in 2026 are Slack, Microsoft Teams, Zoom Team Chat, Cisco Webex, and Google Chat, each with a distinct primary use case and ecosystem.
- Zoom is pushing further into cross-platform meeting notes: Zoom's AI Companion can now take notes across third-party meeting platforms like Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and WebEx, not just Zoom itself.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- Review and pilot the new external meeting-bot blocking policy in the Teams Admin Center ahead of its October GA rollout to avoid disrupting legitimate AI notetaker workflows.
- Audit any Teams Rooms still running Android 10/11 — certification has already lapsed, so plan hardware refreshes proactively.
- Track the Work IQ preview (rolling out from Sept 30) for potential Copilot/Teams grounding improvements relevant to sales and business workflows.
