# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Thursday, 22 October 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Microsoft published plans for **Cross-Platform Meeting Connect**, aiming to give Teams Rooms hardware a smoother experience when joining Zoom-hosted meetings — including planned HDMI sharing so people can connect a laptop and share content directly into the meeting, plus synced room/touch console controls prioritizing mute, unmute, and end-call.
- User-reported issues with Teams and Microsoft 365 spiked on October 20, with a monitoring snapshot showing 83 Teams reports against a baseline of 13, and 73 Microsoft 365 reports against a baseline of 22 — no single outage was confirmed, and admins were advised to check tenant Service Health.
- Admin reminder: the October 16 Copilot SKU renaming (existing add-on licenses) is now live, with a further pricing/catalog milestone due November 1 (see Action Items).

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- Per Microsoft's October Frontier updates, **@Copilot in Teams** and a **Teams meeting summary without a retained transcript** are rolling out, alongside a redesigned Notebooks experience and new "Today" home surface — Today in Home and @Copilot in Teams are both noted as private preview.
- Licensing shift: usage-based billing will be enabled by default for new Microsoft 365 Copilot Business subscriptions purchased through CSP beginning December 1, 2026, with a default spending limit of 4,000 Copilot Credits per user per month, adjustable by admins.
- Copilot Studio and Copilot Cowork are gaining **natural-language app building**, letting users describe the business solution they need, create a working app through a conversational experience, and refine it as requirements evolve.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- CSP partners must act on the Copilot naming change: beginning October 16, 2026, existing Microsoft 365 Copilot subscriptions that require a qualifying base subscription now include "(add-on)" in their names
