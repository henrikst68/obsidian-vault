# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Friday, 30 October 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Microsoft confirmed Teams will begin using AI-powered phishing detection to Teams messages starting in early November 2026, with the rollout part of a targeted release phase expected to end by mid-November, reaching general availability worldwide between early and mid-December.
- The new capability will use conversation context (without retaining message content) to identify phishing and suspicious behavior in higher-risk messaging scenarios, such as external first-contact chats; IT admins won't need to take any action to enable this new security protection feature.
- Teams is also tightening its AI-notetaker policy, with reporting noting Microsoft Teams will now **automatically block external bots** as part of a broader AI notetaker crackdown.
- Microsoft had to restore service on 26 October after an issue affecting some users in the United States, tracked under incident TM1526111, with automatic scaling efforts returning the service to healthy levels.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- Microsoft's internal "Inside Track" blog published two new Copilot adoption pieces on 29 October — one on deploying AI with employee councils, bringing employee voices into AI decision-making as Teams experiment, ideas are generated across the organization and momentum builds fast, and another sharing internal best practices for writing better Copilot prompts.
- Partner Center's October update confirms usage-based billing will now be enabled by default for new Microsoft 365 Copilot Business subscriptions purchased through CSP, beginning December 1, 2026.
- New CRM capabilities are landing directly inside Teams: surfacing customer context, insights, and actions directly in Microsoft Teams so organizations can work across customer data and workflows without switching applications, now in public preview from October 8, 2026.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- Microsoft is running a partner promotion through next year: a 30% off the net partner price of Microsoft 365 E3, with and without Teams, available October 27, 2026, through June 30, 2027.
- The Custom Solutions for Microsoft Teams specialization will be retired in November 2026, a signal that partners should review their specialization pathways ahead of Ignite.
- Cross-platform meeting interoperability continues to develop: Microsoft and Zoom's "Cross-Platform Meeting Connect" builds on Direct Guest Join, letting Teams Rooms join Zoom meetings and Zoom Rooms join Teams meetings, though Microsoft hasn't given a timeline for Teams Rooms while Zoom targets H1 2027.

## Meetingroom Equipment
- UC Today's latest meeting-room market analysis notes Microsoft's Teams Rooms Pro reports now combine reservations with occupancy signals, giving facilities teams a better chance of catching rooms that look busy on the calendar but aren't, with desk analytics arriving in November, although participating desks need connected peripherals and Shared Space licenses.
- Barco continues to invest in Teams-certified hardware, having launched another Microsoft Teams-certified ClickShare Hub Pro bundle, pairing its system with Huddly cameras and Shure audio last month.
- Vendors are increasingly framing demand around intelligence rather than just hardware refreshes, with Barco's Jan van Houtte noting "Our customers expect meetings and devices to be intelligent".
