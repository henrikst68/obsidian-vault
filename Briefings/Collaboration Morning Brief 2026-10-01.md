# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Thursday, 1 October 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Teams Rooms on Windows build 5.7.112.0 (released 9/28) moves **all app updates exclusively to the Pro Management Portal** (no longer via Microsoft Store) and adds **native local PTZ camera controls** from the in-room console.
- Inventory, updates, health and settings for Teams Rooms on Android, Teams phones, panels and SIP phones are moving to the Teams Rooms Pro Management Portal, with the phased deprecation of device management in the Teams Admin Center beginning the week of 1 September and expected to complete by end of September in all clouds — meaning by today, TAC stops being where you manage Android devices.
- Teams is rolling out an admin-controlled meeting policy that automatically blocks identified external bots, such as third-party AI note-takers, from joining meetings — off by default and requiring admin activation.
- A new capability allowing users to temporarily pause all notifications for a selected period is rolling out from early October, aimed at reducing interruption overload.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- OpenAI's Sept 29 DevDay announcements bring workplace-focused ChatGPT updates: Business and Enterprise workspaces can now build teams with shared, scheduled tasks and call @ChatGPT inside Slack and Microsoft Teams — a direct competitive overlap with Copilot's positioning inside Teams.
- The Copilot app is being renamed, with Microsoft redirecting remaining users from m365.cloud.microsoft to copilot.cloud.microsoft in early October; organisations whose proxy, firewall or Conditional Access policy blocks the new host may find affected users unable to use the Copilot web app.
- The Learning Coach agent in Microsoft 365 Copilot is being retired in the first week of October 2026, and users should transition to the replacement Learning Agent before the retirement date.
- Cost management in Agent 365 is expanding beyond Cowork and Work IQ APIs to include Code and Copilot Managed Runtime, with support for agents built in Microsoft Copilot Studio planned for October.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- Microsoft's Agent 365 partner roster continues to grow — Miro's visual collaboration boards are being brought into Microsoft Teams via Agent 365, enabling teams to create, update, and collaborate — alongside Box, Canva, Achievers and Cornerstone integrations surfacing directly in Teams/Outlook.
- Teams Rooms on Android now supports two-way Direct Guest Join interoperability with Google Meet, letting users join across platforms with one click via calendar or meeting ID.
- Panel-device settings now sync bidirectionally with the Pro Management Portal across AudioCodes, Cisco Room Navigator, Jabra Scheduler, Logitech Tap Scheduler, Neat Pad and Poly TC10, and new desk-hub devices like the Logi Dock Flex now support auto-reservation with guest booking from the touch screen.

## 🏢 Meeting Room Equipment
- Hardware admin consolidation continues: Teams Rooms app distribution is shifting entirely to the Pro Management Portal, reinforcing Microsoft's push toward unified device lifecycle management across Poly, Yealink, Logitech, Jabra, Crestron and Neat estates.
- Microsoft's Teams certification for first-gen HP Poly Studio X30/X50/X70/G7500/TC8 and original Neat Bar/Board ended 3 September 2026, though Microsoft commits to supporting the latest Teams app on certified hardware for two years after certification ends — a relevant lifecycle note for anyone budgeting room refreshes.
- Vendor comparisons (Poly vs Yealink) highlight diverging AI strategies: Poly leads on premium AI audio with NoiseBlockAI and Acoustic Fence 2.0, while Yealink leads on AI room video with SmartVision 80's quad-lens system and a 34-TOPS NPU.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- Salesforce is pushing deeper into agentic AI: Salesforce is rolling out Hyperagent capabilities inside Slack, enabling teams to deploy autonomous AI agents that can source leads, generate reports, update CRM records, and execute multi-step workflows without leaving the chat interface.
- Google Workspace's September 18 recap introduced flow automation in Workspace Studio with custom starters, steps, third-party integrations and webhooks, alongside a new beta allowing PowerPoint files to be imported and converted to client-side encrypted Google Slides for Microsoft Office interoperability.
- Zoom is tightening its scheduling tools: Zoom Scheduler Basic is being removed from Zoom Workplace Basic and Zoom Workplace Pro plans, effective October 23 — with Zoomtopia 2026 (Oct 20–22) set to unveil the next wave of Zoom AI features.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
1. **Today's deadline**: Confirm migration of Teams Rooms on Android, panels and phones out of the Teams Admin Center and into the Pro Management Portal — TAC device management ends around now.
2. **Network/firewall check**: Ensure `copilot.cloud.microsoft` is fully allow-listed ahead of the early-October redirect to avoid Copilot web app access issues.
3. **Watch the OpenAI vs. Microsoft dynamic**: OpenAI's new @ChatGPT integration inside Teams and Slack (announced at DevDay) could reshape how users choose their in-app AI assistant — worth monitoring adoption signals internally.
