# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Wednesday, 19 August 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Microsoft is mid-rollout on a redesigned in-meeting experience: redesigned meeting controls began rolling out in late July 2026, with redesigned sharing controls following in August 2026, ahead of a worldwide GA in late September/October.
- New chat organization is landing this month: Teams is introducing two new system chat sections, Muted chats and Meeting chats, letting users group muted conversations and meeting chats into dedicated, toggleable sections, with rollout completing by end of August.
- Calling gets more flexible: Teams Phone user multi-line now lets admins assign up to 10 phone numbers to a single user, with full support across Teams desktop, mobile, and Teams devices, plus a matching multi-line mobile calling experience.
- Admin/security tooling is expanding — user-reported security signals are now surfaced in Teams admin center, letting admins view and download signals from messages users flag as a security concern to fine-tune policies.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- August's Copilot wave adds model choice and new formats: eight new features were introduced in August 2026, including integration of advanced AI models like GPT-5.6 and Claude that dynamically adapt to tasks, and Copilot now supports additional file types, including markdown (.md), text (.txt), and RTF files.
- Copilot Notebooks go mainstream: notebooks, previously limited to select users, are now accessible across all Microsoft 365 licenses, including commercial and educational plans, integrating with OneNote for brainstorming and collaboration.
- Task management AI widens access: Planner Agent, previously limited to premium plans, is now available in all group-based Planner plans for users with a Microsoft 365 Copilot license.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- Hiring workflows move into Teams: the LinkedIn Hiring Assistant is now integrated in Teams, helping streamline candidate review and hiring manager collaboration.
- Governance is tightening: the August Roadmap adds further depth to the calling and compliance story, with Purview expanding to non-Microsoft apps and Teams Phone governance controls becoming more granular.
- Admin housekeeping: Teams will remove the "Require verification by participants (CAPTCHA)" policy from the admin center starting late August 2026, shifting to a default-on bot detection model requiring organizer approval for external bots.

## Meetingroom Equipment
- Vendor certification cliff approaching: Teams certification for first-gen HP Poly Studio X30, X50, X70, G7500, TC8 and the original Neat Bar/Board ends 3 September 2026, alongside select Logitech, DTEN, Yealink and EPOS models — devices keep working but lose certification guarantees.
- Poly and Yealink are locked in a hardware arms race: Poly added Mission and Focus 6 headsets plus new room compute, while Yealink expanded its Teams room architecture, MP E2 phone roadmap, and YMCS integration with Microsoft Places.
- Software-only meeting rooms are coming: a new Teams "Room Optimization Mode" aims to bring AI-powered active-speaker tracking similar to dedicated Teams Rooms hardware, but with all processing on the laptop's CPU or NPU and no external USB peripherals required, benefiting BYOD huddle spaces.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- Slack's August update is one of its biggest of the year: it delivers 12 new capabilities — including agents in DMs, Slackbot-generated slides and dashboards, Google Docs as a Workflow Builder knowledge source, and adjustable AI content safety filters; note a FedRAMP support-data decision deadline of **today, 19 August**.
- Salesforce is pushing Slack deeper into its ecosystem: Salesforce Summer '26 makes Slack the default for every new customer.
- Google Workspace added admin AI: Admin Assist brings two new Gemini-powered capabilities to the Google Admin Console — Sidepanel and Search Overviews — designed to simplify complex administrative workflows and troubleshooting, rolling out from 18 August.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- **Patch urgently:** August Patch Tuesday included three Critical vulnerabilities affecting Microsoft Teams, with CVSS scores up to 10.0 (elevation of privilege and spoofing flaws) — confirm rollout status across the estate.
- **Plan change management** for the new Teams meeting/sharing controls redesign and the Muted/Meeting chat sections landing by end of August — update helpdesk scripts and user guidance.
- **Audit meeting room fleet** against the 3 September 2026 certification cutoff for legacy Poly/Neat/Logitech/Yealink devices; budget replacements for boardrooms relying on first-gen hardware.
