# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Monday, 26 October 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Microsoft confirmed Teams will roll out **AI-powered, context-aware phishing detection** starting early November 2026, analysing conversation context (not just links/files) to flag suspicious behaviour, with GA expected by mid-December. Microsoft has announced plans to add AI-powered phishing detection to Teams messages starting in early November 2026, with the rollout part of a targeted release phase expected to end by mid-November, and general availability worldwide between early and mid-December.
- Microsoft also confirmed **third-party synthetic audio/video (deepfake) detection** is coming to Teams meetings, though it won't name the vendors who will do the scanning. Microsoft hasn't named any providers, licensing, or what meeting data those vendors get to see, with the feature first appearing on the roadmap as "Third-party synthetic audio and video detection in Microsoft Teams."
- **Teams Rooms on Android** is gaining a private "backstage" presenter chat for organizers in green-room-enabled meetings/events, appearing as an "Event group" or "Planning group" tab, with GA completing by late November. Teams Rooms on Android with a Pro license will gain access to a private presenter chat in green room-enabled meetings/events when joined as organizers, appearing as "Event group" or "Planning group."
