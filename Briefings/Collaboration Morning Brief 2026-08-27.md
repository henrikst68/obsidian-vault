# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Thursday, 27 August 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Microsoft confirmed a Teams outage on the morning of 26 August affecting multiple features; by 7:26 a.m. IST the company said Microsoft Teams is operational after the morning disruption, with only a handful of user-submitted reports over the previous 24 hours and the outage marked resolved, though Microsoft kept monitoring for a possible relapse.
- This wasn't an isolated incident — Microsoft only recently closed a screensharing video-quality degradation, and a separate advisory still warns that some shared-channel "Copy Link" URLs are malformed after an update meant to improve link generation.
- Guidance for admins: organizations that treat Teams as the default emergency path should treat this as a continuity event, not only a productivity glitch, with a full client restart, switch to web/mobile, or fallback conference bridge more useful than chasing a local policy fix during a live incident.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- Microsoft is rolling out a simplified Copilot Notebooks experience, letting users open Copilot Notebooks from the Microsoft 365 app launcher, use the updated navigation pane to browse notebooks and sections, and open and edit notebook items with new streamlined controls.
- A new integration lets Copilot Chat be available for Microsoft 365 Copilot users from the side pane when using Copilot Search, allowing users to engage with Copilot while browsing universal search without switching pages or losing context.
- Copilot Chat now supports quick email lookups: from an email reference in a Microsoft 365 Copilot app, users can click the email link to open it directly in Chat, streamlining context-switching for knowledge workers.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- Google is pushing deeper AI integration into its chat product: starting 26 August, Google Chat becomes the place where users can bring the power of Gemini into their flow of teamwork, with a new "Ask Gemini in Google Chat" feature acting as a unified command line for work, powered by Workspace Intelligence — a direct competitive move against Copilot in Teams.
- Zoom continues expanding automation: its ZoomMate feature added major workflow automation upgrades, including admin controls for approved action nodes, new Zoom Mail, Workspace, Phone, My Notes and webhook capabilities, with account owners able to define which workflow triggers are available to specific user groups.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- Confirm whether your tenant is impacted by the malformed shared-channel "Copy Link" issue following Microsoft's recent link-generation update, and brief teams on fallback options (web/mobile/conference bridge) in case of a repeat Teams outage.
- Pilot the new Copilot Notebooks navigation and the Copilot Chat side-pane-in-Search feature with a small group to assess productivity impact before wider rollout.
- Keep an eye on Google's "Ask Gemini in Google Chat" launch as a competitive signal — worth a quick feature comparison against Copilot in Teams for any hybrid-tool discussions.
