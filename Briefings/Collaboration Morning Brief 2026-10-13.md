# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Tuesday, 13 October 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Legacy Teams-scoped retention policies will stop implicitly covering Copilot interactions; admins need to create a dedicated Copilot-workload retention policy or lose retain/delete coverage, reaching GA this October.
- Microsoft is adding support for third-party deepfake detection and impersonation-protection tools in Teams meetings, with both features set to reach general availability in November after a worldwide rollout.
- Exchange Web Services is retiring, meaning Teams devices below minimum versions will lose calendar sync; Google Workspace-to-Teams calendar sync also becomes unsupported from October.
- From mid-October, Copilot leaves the Teams chat list entirely — the Copilot app in the app bar becomes the only entry point, though conversations and deep links are preserved.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- Teams Phone Agent — an AI receptionist that answers call-queue questions, books appointments and transfers calls with AI notes in 60+ languages — is now generally available, rolling out from 30 September.
- Copilot in SharePoint has reached general availability, letting every user create/organize content, ask questions grounded in SharePoint data, and build reusable skills.
- Starting 1 December 2026, usage-based billing will be enabled by default for new Microsoft 365 Copilot Business licenses purchased via CSP, with pay-as-you-go covering Copilot Cowork, Work IQ APIs and GitHub Copilot Harness.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- Microsoft has welcomed Logitech to the Microsoft Device Ecosystem Platform (MDEP), with Rally Bar 2, Tap IP 2 and Tap Scheduler 2 rolling out globally as the first Logitech devices built on the platform.
- Teams Rooms on Android gained a modernised Gallery view, an interpreter agent, two-way Direct Guest Join with Google Meet, town hall group chat, and SIP/H.323 calling via Pexip.
- Dynamics 365 Sales added 30 new pre-built CRM skills for Copilot Cowork, now generally available, bringing sales/service/marketing context into AI workflows.

## Meetingroom Equipment
- Logitech unveiled what it calls the largest enterprise product launch in its history, led by Rally Bar 2 (certified for Teams, Zoom Rooms and Google Meet) and built on MDEP.
- Rally Bar 2, Tap IP 2 and Tap Scheduler 2 are launching globally now, while Rally System 2 Pro and RoomMate 2 Pro are planned for Summer 2027.
- Cisco now claims to be the fastest-growing Microsoft Teams Rooms ecosystem partner, with Zoom Rooms integration going GA this month and native Google Meet support arriving via RoomOS 27 in November.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- Cisco unveiled Claude Managed Agents inside Webex at WebexOne 2026 on 7 October, alongside RoomOS 27 and OpenAI's "dot" agents, turning Webex into a multi-vendor AI battleground.
- Google Cloud introduced a unified "Gemini agent" for work on 8 October, with persistent "coworker agents" that get their own Workspace identity, email, calendar and storage.
- Brandeis University has moved its phones, meetings and IT contact centre onto Zoom, putting an AI virtual agent on its help desk as a notable enterprise migration case study.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- Review tenant retention policies now — the Copilot-workload retention gap (GA this month) could create compliance exposure if not addressed before legacy Teams policies stop covering Copilot interactions.
- Evaluate Logitech's new MDEP-based Rally Bar 2/Tap IP 2/Tap Scheduler 2 lineup against upcoming room refresh budgets, and note Rally System 2 Pro slips to Summer 2027.
- Track the November GA of Teams deepfake/impersonation detection — decide whether to pursue a certified third-party provider (e.g., Resemble AI) ahead of rollout.
