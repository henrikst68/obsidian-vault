# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Tuesday, 3 November 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Microsoft Teams is introducing AI-powered, context-aware phishing detection to identify phishing, social engineering, impersonation attempts, and deceptive intent, analyzing conversation context rather than relying on malicious links or files; targeted rollout starts this week with no IT admin action required to enable the protection.
- Per the weekly roadmap roundup, the "Collect information with list form in Workflows" feature has officially launched, while desk utilization analytics for Teams Pro Management portal and a redesigned meeting sharing panel are now in development for release this month.
- Microsoft is defending Teams' resource footprint: the Teams engineering team says "delivering a consistent, high-quality experience across every platform remains a key focus," with Efficiency mode intended for PCs with 8GB of RAM — a trade-off reviewers argue degrades video quality on lower-spec machines.
- GitHub notifications and Copilot are now merged into a single "GitHub" app inside Teams; the legacy GitHub/Copilot preview app will be deprecated on November 16, 2026, making migration an urgent task for admins.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- A new Message Center post (Nov 2) confirms Microsoft Copilot app updates bring a new Home integrating Chat, Cowork, Projects, and Workspaces (replacing Notebooks), with standard rollout beginning late October 2026.
- Usage-based billing models including GPT-6 Astra and Claude Fable 5.1 are becoming available in Copilot Chat and Copilot in Word, Excel and PowerPoint starting November 2026, alongside Enhanced Copilot Memory personalizing responses using Microsoft 365 data, completing by end of November.
- From 4 November 2026, standard users can create AI agent blueprints unless an admin turns this off — worth a policy check before go-live.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- Dataverse for Teams environments can no longer be created starting November 15, 2026, pushing admins toward full Dataverse migration.
- Teams Phone Agent is now generally available, and intelligent call delegation (an AI agent that screens incoming calls, provides context, and books follow-ups via Bookings) is dated for GA in November 2026, requiring Teams Phone plus a Copilot licence.
- Viva Engage is rolling out a new centralized Campaigns hub in its mobile app and Teams mobile, with worldwide GA expected to complete by early December 2026.

## 🏢 Meeting Room Equipment
- A refreshed Message Center notice confirms breakout room support is coming to Teams Rooms on Android, with GA now pushed to begin early December 2026 (previously mid-November).
- Logitech's new MDEP-based Teams Rooms lineup — Rally Bar 2, Tap IP 2, Tap Scheduler 2, Rally System 2 Pro, and RoomMate 2 Pro — brings brighter anti-glare touch controllers and updated chipsets, deepening the Microsoft Device Ecosystem Platform partnership.
- On the rival side, Cisco's RoomOS 27, shipping in November, will let Cisco devices run Webex, Microsoft Teams, Zoom and Google Meet natively on the same hardware — relevant for any mixed-estate room standardisation plans.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- At WebexOne 2026, Cisco confirmed Anthropic's Claude is coming into Webex, OpenAI's "dots" will connect via MCP, and Cisco room devices will run Google Meet natively alongside Teams, Zoom and Webex, signalling a more open, multi-platform meeting room strategy.
- Zoom continues to court mixed-tool environments: its November update adds AI Companion note-taking that works across third-party meeting platforms like Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and WebEx, plus a guided migration wizard to help admins move off Slack.
- Salesforce's Dreamforce momentum continues into Slack: Slackforce (Salesforce context inside Slack) reached GA in October, with Agentforce Coworker GA now live inside Salesforce itself, deepening Slack's role as an agentic CRM front-end — a direct competitive push against Teams/Copilot integration depth.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- **Review GitHub app migration**: notify Teams users on the legacy GitHub/Copilot app to switch before the **November 16** retirement.
- **Check Copilot Agent Builder/blueprint policy**: standard users gain self-service AI agent blueprint creation from **November 4** unless disabled by admins.
- **Audit Dataverse for Teams usage**: no new environments can be created after **November 15** — flag any teams still relying on this for low-code apps.
