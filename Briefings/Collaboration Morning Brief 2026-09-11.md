# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Friday, 11 September 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Teams' Copilot experience is being simplified with a chat-centered design, improved navigation, updated chat layout, Work IQ controls, and a new Tasks view in full-screen mode, rolling out this month with no admin action required beyond updating training materials.
- Android 10/11 certification for first-generation Teams Rooms devices (early HP Poly, Logitech and Yealink models) ended on 3 September, leaving just one year of Microsoft support with no path to upgrade the Android version.
- Communicator, a new Teams app for creating and managing targeted organizational announcements with acknowledgment tracking, has entered Public Preview running from mid-August 2026 to mid-January 2027.
- Microsoft is retiring calendar syncing between Teams and Google Workspace in October 2026, so organizations should notify users and prepare migration steps now.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- Copilot will soon support creating and querying Planner tasks directly, letting users manage task work across Copilot and Planner more seamlessly, rolling out this September.
- This month, reusable Copilot skills in SharePoint/OneDrive can follow users across apps, with Copilot able to measure and improve those skills via evaluations, plus new guidance for building SharePoint-safe AI agent experiences.
- New Copilot and Agent 365 Dashboards now support exporting de-identified, row-level usage metrics, giving admins deeper insight into organizational Copilot/agent usage while stripping personal identifiers — rolling out in September.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- Local PTZ (pan/tilt/zoom) camera control is coming to Teams Rooms on Windows consoles, entering public preview in early September with GA expected late September to mid-October (GCC mid-October to early November).
- Entra passwordless resource accounts for Rooms (Windows and Android), panels and phones are rolling out from August, with a migration wizard and progress dashboard in the Pro Management Portal across worldwide, GCC and GCC High environments.
- Google Calendar is rolling out improvements that make it easier to join third-party video meetings — including Microsoft Teams, Zoom, and Cisco Webex — when collaborating across different calendar and email clients, with full rollout starting 10 September.

## Meetingroom Equipment
- Teams Rooms on Android in the Pro portal is getting richer health signals — storage, meeting app, calendar sync, secondary display and network health — with automatic alerts and ticket workflows, plus richer peripheral health for BYOD spaces, targeted for GA in October 2026.
- Microsoft has extended the certification end date for Android 12-based Teams Rooms devices from October 6, 2026, to August 15, 2027, giving buyers more runway before the next hardware refresh cycle.
- Cisco's public beta of "Cisco Devices for Zoom Rooms" started in June 2026, with general availability expected this September — a notable cross-platform hardware play as Cisco devices now also support Zoom natively.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- Slack shipped a major monthly update centered on AI collaboration, bringing code channels, new Agent and Slackbot workspaces, deeper research, and tighter connections to Salesforce, Seismic, Lists, and Workflow Builder.
- A new "Salesforce approvals in Slack" feature routes approvals straight into Slack via Salesforce flow orchestration so approvers see the full picture and decide faster.
- The five dominant enterprise messaging platforms in 2026 remain Slack, Microsoft Teams, Zoom Team Chat, Cisco Webex, and Google Chat, each anchored to a distinct ecosystem (Teams dominant in M365-standardized enterprises, Slack in engineering/ChatOps).

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- **Security priority:** Microsoft published CVE-2026-65812 on September 8 — an "Important"-rated Teams for Android flaw that could expose credentials over a network under specific conditions — ensure Android Teams clients are patched.
- **Hardware audit:** With Android 10/11 device certification lapsed, inventory any legacy Teams Rooms hardware for refresh planning before support fully ends.
- **Social engineering watch:** Multiple recent campaigns (SynkLoader, Spring Ring, STAC4749) are impersonating IT support staff through Microsoft Teams messages to distribute malware and steal credentials — worth a reminder note to staff about verifying "IT helpdesk" Teams calls/chats.
