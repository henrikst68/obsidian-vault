# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Friday, 2 October 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Microsoft's official Teams blog confirmed a major cadence shift: What's New in Teams is shifting from a monthly to a quarterly rhythm, with the next edition due in December.
- Teams Phone Agent, an AI-powered calling receptionist that helps organizations serve their customers and answers incoming calls to help customers with common requests, reached general availability on September 30.
- The latest release notes (published Oct 1) add several quality-of-life updates: agents and apps now surface directly in Teams search results, and admins can enable automatic recording and transcription for individual Teams call queues with control over access to recordings.
- CSR platform Benevity launched a new Teams app this week, with the company noting the new Microsoft Teams app launches today, with more collaboration platform integrations and deskless QR code access coming soon.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- Microsoft delayed the shift to usage-based billing for new Microsoft 365 Copilot Business licenses: previously communicated as November 2, 2026, usage-based billing will now be enabled by default for new Microsoft 365 Copilot Business subscriptions purchased through CSP, beginning December 1, 2026.
- Agent 365 cost management is expanding, with cost management in Agent 365 expanding beyond Cowork and Work IQ APIs to include Code and Copilot Managed Runtime, with support for agents built in Microsoft Copilot Studio planned for October.
- Work IQ continues to deepen enterprise grounding, as Work IQ now grounds Microsoft Copilot and agents in core business data from Microsoft Dynamics 365 and Microsoft Power Platform.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- Exchange Online's EWS deprecation officially began October 1, with Microsoft's Exchange Team confirming EWS deprecation in Exchange Online is starting today, a change that will ripple into hybrid and cross-org sharing scenarios across the M365 stack.
- Logitech's Zone Vibe Pro for Business headset achieved Teams certification this week, featuring AI-powered voice isolation that uses machine learning to capture your voice clearly, adaptive hybrid ANC to minimize distractions, and custom 40 mm drivers for vivid, true-to-life audio.
- Benevity's new Teams integration (see above) signals continued third-party ISV momentum building directly into the Teams app store.

## Meetingroom Equipment
- Logitech Zone Vibe Pro for Business is the standout new certified device this cycle — positioned for open-office and wide deployment scenarios per this week's Teams blog update.
- Nothing significant on new Teams Rooms hardware (Poly, Yealink, Crestron, MAXHUB) in the last 48 hours — expect the next device wave with the quarterly Teams update in December.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- Salesforce pushed further into agentic AI, with Slack now hosting Hyperagent capabilities inside Slack, enabling teams to deploy autonomous AI agents that can source leads, generate reports, update CRM records, and execute multi-step workflows without leaving the chat interface. Commentary noted Microsoft has been pursuing a similar strategy with Copilot agents inside Teams, and Google is building agent capabilities into Workspace.
- The EU's internally-developed "sovereign" Teams alternative, dubbed Element Pro, is drawing sharp internal criticism — described by the Commission as a "sovereign back-up to Teams," the body's main communications tool developed by US software giant Microsoft — with testers reportedly unimpressed by its value proposition.
- Google Workspace continues incremental security/admin hardening, recently adding the ability to assign administrator roles to users, groups, or service accounts for a defined period of time, with access automatically revoked once the expiration time is reached.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- Flag the Teams "What's New" cadence change internally — plan comms/training cycles around quarterly (not monthly) releases going forward.
- Review Partner Center / procurement plans ahead of the December 1 default switch to usage-based Copilot Business billing to avoid surprise consumption costs.
- Watch the EU "Element Pro" story — early signal of sovereign-cloud pressure that could affect public-sector Teams deployments in Europe; worth monitoring for policy/competitive implications.
