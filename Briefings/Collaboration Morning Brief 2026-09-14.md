# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Monday, 14 September 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Microsoft has begun the **phased deprecation of Teams Android device management in the Teams Admin Center (TAC)**, with the move to the Teams Rooms Pro Management Portal expected to be complete "by end of September in all clouds" — after 30 September, TAC will no longer manage Android rooms, phones, panels, or SIP devices. The phased deprecation of device management in the Teams Admin Center began the week of 1 September, and Microsoft "expects to complete by end of September in all clouds"
- Teams' Copilot experience is getting a redesign this month: a chat-centered design, improved navigation, updated chat layout, Work IQ controls, and a new Tasks view in full-screen mode, available September 2026, requiring no user action but administrators should update training and support materials accordingly
- A new admin policy rolling out lets organizations automatically block identified external meeting bots from joining meetings, off by default and requiring admin activation and evaluation before deployment
- Microsoft continues to hold UC leadership status: Microsoft has been recognized as a Leader in the 2026 Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ for Unified Communications as a Service—its eighth consecutive year with this recognition

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- Copilot Cowork got a UI refresh this month: the left navigation now reads "Automations" instead of "Scheduled," the model picker moved into the prompting box with separate GPT and Claude sections, and a new reasoning-effort slider lets users dial between a faster/cheaper response or a slower, "smarter" pass
- Teams meeting recaps now support translation: multilingual recaps let you pick a language to translate the meeting recap into, actively rolling out through early September
- Copilot in SharePoint's September update focuses on portability and governance: reusable skills can follow you across SharePoint and OneDrive, Copilot can measure and improve those skills with evaluations, and new guidance helps AI agents build live, SharePoint-safe experiences

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- Google is closing a cross-platform gap: Google Calendar is being updated to make it easier to join third-party video meetings, including Microsoft Teams, Zoom, and Cisco Webex, when collaborating across different calendar and email clients, rolling out to Rapid Release domains through mid-September.
- LinkedIn's recruiting tools are now native in Teams: the LinkedIn Hiring Assistant is now integrated in Teams, helping streamline candidate review and hiring manager collaboration
- Microsoft Places is adding location awareness: workplace check-in via Wi-Fi for Microsoft Places and Teams is designed to help employees coordinate in-person work by keeping their workplace location up to date when they're in the office

## Meetingroom Equipment
- HP/Poly is pushing deeper into managed room compute: HP's 2026 Poly push includes Studio Room Compute, VideoOS 5.0 and a forthcoming 5.1 release, Poly Studio V12, Poly Lens, WXP, Focus 6, Mission headsets, and its existing CCX Teams phones, while Yealink expanded its Teams room architecture, MP E2 phone roadmap, and YMCS integration with Microsoft Places
- Teams Rooms hardware roadmap continues to converge Windows/Android: native PTZ camera control rollout begins this month, and Teams Rooms on Android now supports coordinated meetings, enabling paired Android devices and touch boards to work together in the same hybrid meeting, creating more immersive and flexible meeting spaces
- Cisco is opening its room hardware to rival platforms: the public beta of Cisco Devices for Zoom Rooms started in June 2026, with general availability expected September 2026, running as a certified Cisco Device for Zoom Rooms on Cisco hardware

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- Salesforce is escalating its Slack-first strategy against Teams/Copilot: reports indicate Salesforce Summer '26 makes Slack the default for every new customer, continuing the vendor's push to make Slack the enterprise's "agentic OS" front end for Agentforce.
- Analyst comparisons this cycle rank Microsoft Teams as "Best for Microsoft shops: deep Microsoft 365 integration, strong enterprise security" against Zoom, Google Meet, and Webex — with Teams dominating M365-standardized enterprises while Webex remains default for Cisco-stack and government organizations.
- No major Teams service disruptions in the last 24 hours: there have been no user-submitted reports of outages in the past 24 hours, and the last officially acknowledged outage was on September 4, 2026 — worth noting after a rocky August/early-September stretch of multi-service M365 outages.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- **Deadline this month:** Confirm your Teams Rooms Android/panel/SIP device fleet has moved off the Teams Admin Center to the Pro Management Portal before the 30 September cutoff — this is not optional and could break device management if missed.
- **Prep support teams** for the new Teams Copilot chat-centered UI (Work IQ, Tasks view) rolling out this month — update internal training docs ahead of user questions.
- **Watch competitively:** Salesforce's "Slack as agentic OS" push and Cisco opening its room hardware to Zoom Rooms both signal intensifying cross-platform competition — worth monitoring for procurement/vendor-lock-in implications.
