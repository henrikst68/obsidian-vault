# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Monday, 28 September 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Admins can now manage Teams Core agents, such as Facilitator, from a dedicated experience in the Teams admin center, controlling agent availability and settings for the organization, specific users, or groups, with these agents no longer tied to broader Microsoft app settings.
- Teams has rolled out breakout rooms for meetings with up to 1,000 attendees and the ability for users to reserve desks on Teams desk hub devices.
- Microsoft has quietly cancelled two roadmap items: proactive push notifications in the Copilot mobile app and Interactive Agents for Teams Meetings and Calls, both now marked cancelled on the roadmap.
- Teams Rooms devices are being rebranded, with Android device management leaving the Teams Admin Center for the Pro Management Portal by 30 September, alongside 11 other Teams updates including call queue auto-recording with AI recaps — a deadline just days away.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- Facilitator has become proactive: it now requires Microsoft 365 Copilot Premium, must be switched on manually per meeting, and marks a genuine change in kind — every other mainstream Copilot surface in Teams reacts to a prompt, but Facilitator now decides on its own when to speak. Note that its answers appear in meeting chat for everyone present, including external and cross-tenant attendees who may not hold a Copilot licence — worth a governance review.
- Microsoft has reshaped its Copilot app around a new work experience with Home, Chat, Cowork, Code and Autopilot, plus Office in Copilot for Word, Excel and PowerPoint, expanding enterprise grounding, plugins and AI spend controls.
- OpenAI's newest model has joined the Copilot stack: GPT-6 Astra joined the frontier models available in Copilot Cowork and Copilot Studio, rolling out from early September.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- Third-party agent governance is tightening: Teams now offers unified app and agent installation management from either the Microsoft 365 admin center or Teams admin center, with changes automatically synchronized across Teams, Outlook, and Microsoft 365 Copilot.
- On SharePoint/Power Platform: the reimagined SharePoint page authoring canvas has reached General Availability, while Azure ACS (SharePoint App-Only) authentication retires on November 27, 2026, meaning SPFx solutions and third-party integrations using ACS app principals will stop working.
- Teams Live Chat is being sunset: Microsoft is retiring Teams Live Chat in October 2026, just months after expanding it, pointing customers to Copilot Studio instead.

## Meetingroom Equipment
- A hardware refresh wave is underway: Android 10 and 11 Teams devices lost certification on 3 September, leaving one year of Microsoft support — affecting first-generation HP Poly, Logitech and Yealink models with no path to upgrade the Android version.
- New room controls are landing: local PTZ camera control on Rooms consoles — pan, tilt and zoom from the Teams Rooms on Windows console — is in public preview from early September, with GA late September to mid-October.
- Cisco is pushing cross-platform room agents: the Agentic Director and Workspace Advisor agents in RoomOS 26 are already supported for Webex and Microsoft Teams Rooms customers, and Cisco is separately seeking certification for Zoom Rooms on its hardware.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- Slack's biggest governance month in a while: the September update introduces Slack Code, a dedicated space for working with coding agents on a single project from planning through shipping, plus a major admin governance overhaul.
- Slackbot is expanding into deeper work: "Big Mode" gives Slackbot a full-screen, dedicated AI workspace inside Slack built for deeper, longer-form work like research, writing, analysis and file generation, live now.
- Cisco's collaboration business is on an upswing, having recently posted its best quarter in seven years for Webex, devices and contact center — with WebexOne in Austin (Oct 5–8) up next.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- **Deadline this week:** Teams Rooms on Android device management moves from Teams Admin Center to the Pro Management Portal by 30 September — confirm your admin team is ready.
- **Policy decision needed:** Facilitator's new proactive search surfaces answers to all meeting attendees, including unlicensed external/cross-tenant guests — decide whether to restrict this via meeting policy.
- **Plan for October:** Teams Live Chat is being retired in October in favor of Copilot Studio — flag this to any teams still using it for customer-facing chat.
