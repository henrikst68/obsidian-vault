# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Monday, 24 August 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Microsoft has been recognized as a Leader in the 2026 Gartner® Magic Quadrant™ for Unified Communications as a Service—its eighth consecutive year with this recognition, reinforcing Teams' UCaaS leadership position.
- A new admin control is rolling out: Microsoft Teams will require owner approval for users joining private teams via join codes starting late August 2026, while users can still generate and enter codes, but owners must approve requests; public teams remain unaffected and no admin action is needed, though owners should be informed.
- Admins are being pushed to act on device security: Teams is introducing a new migration experience in the Rooms Pro Management portal to move shared device accounts to passwordless Microsoft Entra resource accounts, improving security via device-bound credentials, with admin-initiated migration starting early August 2026.
- Accessibility/compliance tweak: the default profanity filter for live captions is being changed from On to Off across Teams meetings, calls, events, and Rooms devices to align with EU requirements, affecting only users who haven't set a personal preference.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- Copilot Chat is getting more model choice: Microsoft is rolling out GPT-5 in Copilot Chat and adding support for Anthropic models to give users more model choices, with a phased release reaching Copilot license holders first; Mike Tholfsen highlighted these updates include integration of advanced AI models like GPT 5.6 and Claude that dynamically adapt to tasks.
- Watch for a live bug: an open Microsoft support note confirms after Classic Outlook for Windows updates to a recent build, Copilot entry points disappear from the client altogether — the button, icon, and navigation pane entry, and Microsoft's workaround is to use new Outlook or Outlook on the web, with the issue still marked STATUS: INVESTIGATING as of 21 August 2026.
- Consumer-facing cleanup: Deep Research is being retired in the Copilot app for consumers starting August 18, 2026, alongside Podcasts, Group chat, and other features retiring as Microsoft consolidates the app.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- Recruiting workflow gets closer to chat: the LinkedIn Hiring Assistant is now integrated in Teams, helping streamline candidate review and hiring manager collaboration.
- Admins get better call diagnostics: the Teams Admin Center now includes a Call Flow Visualizer for Auto Attendants and Teams Phone agents, offering an interactive, tree-like view of call routing.
- Copilot connectors are faster: connectors now run content crawl and identity crawl in parallel instead of sequentially, reducing total processing time and making content available to users faster.

## Meetingroom Equipment
- Microsoft's certified device list for MTR peripherals was refreshed with a table updated August 2026, and Rooms devices generally continue migrating to Entra passwordless resource accounts for security.
- Recent MTR ecosystem hardware highlighted by Microsoft's July roundup includes the MAXHUB Panel SP10, an 11-inch room scheduling solution with native Teams integration, real-time room availability, one-tap ad hoc booking, and a 3-year warranty, and the AudioCodes C456HD, a native Teams desk phone with eight beamforming microphones and AI noise cancellation.
- Vendor comparisons remain active in the buyer's-guide space, with Yealink, Logitech, Poly and Neat video bars all continuing to compete on AI framing, mic reach and Teams/Zoom certification for hybrid rooms.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- Biggest recent competitive move: Slack Code gives teams a dedicated channel where AI agents write, review and ship software in the open, with Claude, ChatGPT, Devin and Copilot as founding partners, free on every plan, launched by Salesforce on August 20, 2026, letting the whole team see coding agents working at the conversational level.
- Slack's wider August admin release is significant for IT: it delivers 12 new capabilities — including agents in DMs, Slackbot-generated slides and dashboards, Google Docs as a Workflow Builder knowledge source, and adjustable AI content safety filters, plus a FedRAMP support-data boundary change requiring Primary Owners to submit a request before August 19, 2026.
- Analysts continue to frame 2026 as a multi-platform reality: the five dominant enterprise messaging platforms are Slack, Microsoft Teams, Zoom Team Chat, Cisco Webex, and Google Chat, each with a distinct primary use case, driving demand for cross-platform bridging tools in mixed-vendor enterprises.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- Flag the **Classic Outlook Copilot disappearance bug** to IT support — recommend affected users switch to new Outlook or Outlook on the web until Microsoft resolves it.
- Brief Teams **private-channel owners** ahead of the late-August join-code approval change so they're ready to review access requests.
- If evaluating dev workflows, keep an eye on **Slack Code** as a potential agentic-coding competitive signal
