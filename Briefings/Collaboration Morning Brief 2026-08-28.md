# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Friday, 28 August 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Teams suffered a brief but disruptive outage on 26 August, with users in Japan and other regions unable to join meetings or share screens; Microsoft said it halted the maintenance activity causing the issue and service returned to standard availability, though it stopped short of calling it fully "service restored." StatusGator logged further short warning periods on both August 26 (42 minutes) and August 27 (30 minutes), suggesting lingering instability this week.
- A known irritant persists alongside the outage: a separate advisory still warns that some shared-channel "Copy Link" URLs are malformed after an update meant to improve link generation.
- On the recognition front, Microsoft has been recognized as a Leader in the 2026 Gartner Magic Quadrant for Unified Communications as a Service — its eighth consecutive year with this recognition.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- Microsoft is streamlining Copilot Notebooks navigation and has made Copilot Chat available from the side pane when using Copilot Search in the Microsoft 365 Copilot app, letting users engage with Copilot while browsing search results without losing context.
- Copilot Notebooks have broadened significantly this month — previously limited to select users, they are now accessible across all Microsoft 365 licenses, including commercial and educational plans, integrating with OneNote for brainstorming and collaboration.
- Copilot's model lineup keeps expanding, with GPT‑5.6 and Claude models now boosting productivity across Word, Excel, and PowerPoint.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- Microsoft's Agent 365 framework is pulling more third-party tools directly into Teams: the integration brings Miro boards and collaboration tools into Microsoft Teams, enabling teams to create, update, and collaborate on visual content without leaving Microsoft 365.
- Lucid is also now embedded via Agent 365, letting teams create and update Lucid diagrams and flowcharts directly from Teams, and collaborate on process maps and visual plans without leaving Microsoft 365.
- All such agent activity is governed centrally — organizations can track all Lucid agent activity through the Microsoft 365 admin center observability dashboard.

## Meetingroom Equipment
- No new hardware launches broke in the last 48 hours, but the Poly-vs-Yealink Teams Rooms rivalry remains the dominant storyline for 2026, with Poly adding Mission and Focus 6 headsets plus new room compute, while Yealink expanded its Teams room architecture, MP E2 phone roadmap, and YMCS integration with Microsoft Places.
- For IT buyers standardizing rooms, licensing remains a key decision point: Teams Rooms Basic is free for up to 25 rooms per tenant, while Teams Rooms Pro runs $40/room/month and is required for Pro Portal management and Copilot features.
- Certified vendor roster is unchanged: certified hardware vendors include Poly, Yealink, Logitech, Jabra, Crestron, and Neat.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- Salesforce's Slack Code continues gaining ecosystem traction: Darwinbox announced its AI-native HCM platform, Darwinbox Cortex, is now available on the Slack Marketplace, letting customers retrieve workforce data, raise requests, complete approvals and get answers without leaving the thread (announced 26 August).
- This builds on Slack Code itself, launched a week earlier, which allows cross-functional teams to work directly with leading AI coding agents—including Anthropic's Claude Code, Cognition's Devin, GitHub Copilot, OpenAI's ChatGPT, and Vercel agents—in a shared, central space.
- No comparable breaking news from Zoom, Webex, or Google Workspace surfaced in the last two business days — worth watching given the pace of Slack's agentic push.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- Flag the repeated short Teams service warnings (24, 26, 27 August) to IT ops — worth a proactive comms note to end users if instability continues into next week.
- If not already enabled, pilot Copilot Notebooks now that they're available across all M365 license tiers — useful for team brainstorming workflows.
- Track Salesforce's Slack Code / agentic coding momentum as a competitive signal — Microsoft may respond with deeper GitHub Copilot-Teams integration.
