# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Friday, 18 September 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- **Active service degradation (MO1472904):** Microsoft confirmed a fresh service degradation affecting its Microsoft 365 suite, with users worldwide reporting an inability to access core applications since the evening of September 16, 2026, tracked internally as MO1472904 and beginning at 7:17 PM GMT+5:30. Affected users are encountering 502 and 503 HTTP error codes, a pattern typically associated with backend server overload or upstream infrastructure failures rather than authentication issues.
- **Device rebrand — "intelligent spaces":** Microsoft is now referring to Rooms, desks, panels and phones collectively as "intelligent spaces," alongside a rollout of passwordless Entra resource accounts for Windows and Android Rooms devices, panels and phones.
- **Android Teams device certification deadline looming:** Android 10 and 11 Teams devices lost certification on 3 September, leaving one year of Microsoft support — first-generation HP Poly, Logitech and Yealink models with no path to upgrade — and Teams Admin Center management for these stops by 30 September.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- **Facilitator agent goes proactive:** The capability requires Microsoft 365 Copilot Premium, must be switched on manually per meeting, and is designed to fire less than once per meeting — but marks a genuine shift, since every other mainstream Copilot surface in Teams reacts to a prompt while Facilitator now decides on its own when to speak. Once enabled, its answers appear in the meeting chat for everyone present, including external and cross-tenant attendees who may not hold a Copilot licence, making this a governance decision as much as a feature toggle.
- **SharePoint Copilot skills go portable:** This month, reusable skills can follow users across SharePoint and OneDrive, Copilot can measure and improve those skills with evaluations, and new guidance helps AI agents build live, SharePoint-safe experiences.
- **Record Patch Tuesday touches Copilot Studio:** September's Microsoft security update affects Copilot Studio among many other products, driving the total CVE count to 997, with 114 rated Critical.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- **Outside agents entering Teams meetings:** GitHub's changelog describes handing an agent a task mid-standup to investigate a problem in the background while the meeting continues, and Atlassian's Rovo agent works similarly for Jira and Confluence, pulling ticket context into a Teams chat — pointing to Teams becoming a surface where external agents pick up work directly from conversation.
- **Agent 365 partner roster expands:** Agent 365 integrations now bring Miro boards and Lucid's visual intelligence directly into Microsoft Teams for diagramming and visual collaboration without leaving Microsoft 365.
- **Roadmap additions this week:** Planner is introducing built-in Planner Agent chat with natural language Q&A, smart task discovery, and in-plan task management, alongside Advanced Collaboration Analytics for GCC, giving Teams admins new reporting capability.

## Meetingroom Equipment
- **Poly vs Yealink hardware race intensifies:** Poly added Mission and Focus 6 headsets plus new room compute, while Yealink expanded its Teams room architecture, MP E2 phone roadmap, and YMCS integration with Microsoft Places.
- **Android certification cliff:** As above, first-gen Poly, Logitech and Yealink Android Rooms devices lose Teams Admin Center manageability by 30 September — a near-term budget/refresh planning item for room fleets.
- **Firmware/bug-fix cadence continues:** Recent Teams device app updates for Poly, Yealink and Audiocodes fixed common area phone calling config issues, app crashes on incoming calls, and call failures to queues/auto attendants.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- **Zoom ships chat and AI dashboard upgrades:** Hosts can now pin an in-meeting chat message as a persistent banner visible to all participants including latecomers, though this depends on a backend update scheduled for September 28, 2026. Zoom also expanded its AI Dashboard with broader AI feature metrics, redesigned KPIs, and an enhanced leaderboard for account admins.
- **Webex adds meeting whiteboard controls:** When someone starts sharing a whiteboard in a meeting, participants now follow the initiator's viewport as they move or zoom around the canvas before editing begins, alongside Day 0 support for the new Apple operating system from September 2026.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
1. **Track MO1472904** in the M365 admin center — the Sept 16 502/503 degradation may still be resolving; confirm tenant impact before end of week.
2. **Flag the Android Rooms certification deadline (30 Sept)** to facilities/IT — first-gen Poly, Logitech and Yealink Android room devices lose TAC manageability imminently.
3. **Decide governance policy on Facilitator's proactive mode** before enabling Copilot Premium's auto-speaking feature, given it surfaces to external/cross-tenant attendees without a Copilot licence.
