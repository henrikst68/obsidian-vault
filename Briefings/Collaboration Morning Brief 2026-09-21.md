# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Monday, 21 September 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Microsoft is rolling out an org-wide **Meetings & Calls view** in the Teams admin center for live monitoring and troubleshooting, complete with Copilot for Admins support, rolling out worldwide and GCC between 30 September and 15 October with no admin action needed.
- A new **abuse-reporting capability** lets users flag phishing, impersonation and scam behaviour directly inside group calls and meetings, with admins reviewing reports in the Teams admin center and Defender; targeted release is late September with GA mid-October on desktop and web first.
- Microsoft Teams Rooms, desks, panels and phones are being rebranded collectively as **"intelligent spaces"**, alongside a rollout of passwordless Entra resource accounts for Windows and Android Rooms devices, panels and phones.
- Admins are gaining discovery and enablement controls for **third-party agents** (e.g., GitHub Copilot) directly from the Teams admin center, with recommendations and required-configuration visibility ahead of a planned October GA.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- **Facilitator**, the meeting agent, has shifted from reactive to proactive: every other mainstream Copilot surface in Teams reacts to a prompt, but Facilitator now decides on its own when to speak. It requires Copilot Premium, must be manually enabled per meeting, and is designed to fire less than once per meeting to avoid becoming a distraction.
- Governance note: once enabled, Facilitator's answers appear in the meeting chat for everyone present, including external and cross-tenant attendees who may not hold a Copilot licence — worth a policy review.
- Third-party agents are getting deeper hooks into Teams meetings: GitHub's Copilot can be handed a task mid-standup to investigate in the background while the meeting continues, and Atlassian's Rovo agent pulls Jira/Confluence ticket context into a Teams chat.
- Microsoft quietly shelved plans to let **Copilot Studio agents join meetings directly** after over a year on the roadmap — a reminder not everything ships.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- Copilot Notebooks has expanded referencing to **Power BI reports, CSV/TSV files, and JPG/PNG images**, letting Copilot ground summaries and briefs in richer organisational data.
- Microsoft Purview DLP and auto-labeling are extending beyond Microsoft services to cover non-Microsoft connected apps including Google Workspace, Box, Dropbox, Salesforce, ServiceNow, AWS, and Cisco Webex, centralising cross-platform data-exposure governance.
- Google Calendar has made it easier to join third-party meetings across ecosystems: improvements let users join third-party video meetings, including Microsoft Teams, Zoom, and Cisco Webex, when collaborating across different calendar and email clients.

## Meetingroom Equipment
- The **Android 10/11 device generation** (first-gen HP Poly Studio X30/X50/X70/G7500/TC8, original Neat Bar/Board, and select Logitech, DTEN, Yealink and EPOS models) lost Teams certification on 3 September 2026; rooms don't switch off — Teams keeps working, backed by two more years of Microsoft support — but new certification guarantees stop.
- Certified MTR hardware remains concentrated among Logitech, Poly (HP), Yealink, Neat, Crestron, Cisco, Lenovo and Jabra, with Sennheiser and Shure supplying certified ceiling-microphone arrays for larger rooms.
- Cisco is extending its "agentic workplace" beyond Webex hardware: the public beta of Cisco Devices for Zoom Rooms started in June 2026, with general availability expected September 2026, and its Agentic Director/Workspace Advisor agents already support Webex and Microsoft Teams Rooms customers.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- Salesforce unveiled **AIforce**, grouping Claudeforce, Slackforce and Agentforce Coworker under one umbrella so people and agents can work with Salesforce data from interfaces including Claude, Slack and Salesforce's own Agentforce products; Slackforce specifically lets users create or update CRM records and trigger workflows from Slack rather than opening a separate CRM view.
- Cisco and Zoom laid out competing AI platform strategies at Goldman Sachs Communacopia, per UC Today's coverage of the two companies' announcements this week.
- Zoom continues its "Workplace" refresh push, with a refreshed simplified app look, a streamlined in-meeting toolbar, and a refreshed Zoom Chat design rolling out across desktop, mobile and web.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- **Audit MTR fleets** for first-gen Poly/Neat/Logitech/Yealink Android 10/11 devices that lost certification on 3 September — plan refresh cycles even though support continues for two more years.
- **Review Facilitator governance**: decide organisational policy on proactive AI speaking in meetings with external/cross-tenant attendees before enabling it broadly.
- **Watch for recurring M365 service instability** — Teams/Exchange/SharePoint experienced multiple outages through August–September (including a major multi-service incident around 16 September); keep an eye on Microsoft 365 status page during critical periods.
