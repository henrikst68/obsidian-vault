# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Monday, 7 September 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Links to SharePoint pages or news shared in Teams chats and channels will now open inside the SharePoint app in Teams rather than the mobile browser, when available.
- The Teams admin center is launching a single pane for monitoring and troubleshooting meetings and calls organization-wide, letting admins view in-progress and completed meetings, run diagnostics, and use Copilot for analysis.
- Heads-up for device owners: Microsoft's Teams certification for first-gen HP Poly Studio X30/X50/X70/G7500/TC8 and original Neat Bar/Board — plus select Logitech, DTEN, Yealink and EPOS models — expired 3 September 2026, though rooms keep working with two more years of Microsoft support, only new certification guarantees stop.
- Microsoft Entra Resource Accounts for Teams Devices now delivers a secure, passwordless identity model for Teams Rooms, Teams Panels, Common Area Phones, and other shared Teams devices.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- This month's Copilot in SharePoint update lets reusable skills follow users across SharePoint and OneDrive, adds evaluation tools to measure and improve those skills, and provides new guidance for AI agents building live, SharePoint-safe experiences.
- Copilot will soon support creating and querying Planner tasks directly, letting users manage task work across Copilot and Planner more seamlessly, rolling out in September.
- Admins gain new data export capabilities from the Copilot and Agent 365 Dashboards, supporting de-identified, row-level usage metrics for deeper organizational insight — also rolling out in September.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- Microsoft is rolling out a next-generation Microsoft 365 file/folder sharing experience built around a single "hero link" that controls file access consistently whether shared via copy, email, or browser URL.
- Legacy Viva Connections instances are being deprecated as their administration consolidates into SharePoint Home site management, with existing Viva Connections experiences upgraded to full Home sites.
- Google Calendar is adding improvements to make it easier to join third-party video meetings, including Microsoft Teams, Zoom, and Cisco Webex, across different calendar and email clients, rolling out from 10 September.

## Meetingroom Equipment
- Poly added Mission and Focus 6 headsets plus new room compute this year, while Yealink expanded its Teams room architecture, MP E2 phone roadmap, and YMCS integration with Microsoft Places.
- Cisco's "Devices for Zoom Rooms" reaches general availability in September 2026, with Zoom now also certified as a Cisco Device for Zoom Rooms running on Cisco hardware — a sign of deepening cross-platform hardware plays.
- Unaffected by the certification expiry are Logitech's Rally Bar, Rally Bar Mini, Tap IP and RoomMate, the DTEN Mate Gen2 console, and Yealink's RoomPanel/RoomPanel E2, which run to 15 August 2028.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- Slack shipped a major monthly update centered on AI collaboration, adding code channels, new Agent and Slackbot workspaces, deeper research tools, and tighter connections to Salesforce, Seismic, Lists, and Workflow Builder.
- Zoom continues iterating AI Companion on the web: it now pulls context from Zoom Chat messages up to 90 days old, alongside meetings and docs, so users don't need to scroll back to find prior decisions.
- Across the five dominant enterprise messaging platforms — Slack, Teams, Zoom Team Chat, Webex, and Google Chat — Teams remains dominant in M365-standardized enterprises while Slack leads engineering/ChatOps use cases.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- Audit meeting rooms against the 3 September 2026 Teams certification expiry list (older Poly/Neat/Logitech/DTEN/Yealink models) — no immediate outage risk, but plan hardware refresh cycles.
- Track the new unified Teams admin center meetings/calls troubleshooting pane rollout for potential support-workflow improvements.
- Watch Copilot's expanding Planner integration and SharePoint reusable-skills feature as they roll out this month — worth pilot-testing with a small user group before wider rollout.
