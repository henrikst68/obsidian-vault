# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Wednesday, 5 August 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- **New in-meeting Report button rolling out this month**: Microsoft has confirmed it's rolling out a new feature in Teams that allows users to report a security issue during a meeting so organizations, IT admins, or security teams can immediately intervene, with full availability expected in August 2026. Microsoft says the feature is necessary given rising impersonation, scams, and suspicious behavior in online meetings, as AI tools have lowered the technical barrier for fraud and cybercrime.
- **Redesigned meeting UI incoming**: Microsoft is also rolling out other improvements, including a new meeting design that reduces accidental screen shares and meeting exits.
- **Stricter federated chat controls updated**: Microsoft updated the rollout timeline on August 4 — External Access Restrictions for Chat Participants now available August 14 (previously July 31), with Mutual Federation for Chat Participants pushed to September 30. These PowerShell controls, disabled by default, help admins limit chat participation to approved external users and ensure mutual federation compliance, with automatic removal of non-compliant participants.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- **Facilitator's proactive web-search feature delayed**: The rollout of Teams Facilitator's ability to detect unanswered questions and proactively search the web has slipped — Targeted Release now begins early September 2026 (previously early August) and General Availability shifts to late September/mid-October (previously mid-to-late August).
- **Governance guidance tightening around Facilitator/AI meeting assistance**: Admins are being advised to review Copilot and Facilitator admin settings, consider implications for sensitive meetings since Facilitator's proactive suggestions are visible to participants, and align with AI governance policies before enabling this level of in-meeting AI assistance.
- **Copilot release notes continue weekly cadence**: Recent updates include Copilot now surfacing rich images inline within responses from files and meetings to provide visual context, supporting richer content formats while maintaining data security compliance.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- **Teams device management consolidating**: Management of Android-based Teams Rooms, Teams panels, and Teams phones is moving from the Teams admin center to the Pro Management portal, providing a consistent and secure management experience across devices and manufacturers.
- **Admin trust tooling for apps/agents**: A new Organization Trust Score feature introduces a scalable, automated evaluation process so IT admins no longer have to manually review trust data for Teams apps and agents.
- **Cross-tenant Exchange recall rolling out mid-August**: Microsoft has introduced cross-tenant message recall in Exchange Online, allowing users to recall emails sent to external tenants; it's disabled by default and the rollout begins mid-August 2026.

## Meetingroom Equipment
- **Poly vs Yealink rivalry intensifies in 2026**: Poly added Mission and Focus 6 headsets plus new room compute, while Yealink expanded its Teams room architecture, MP E2 phone roadmap, and YMCS integration with Microsoft Places.
- **Certified MTR hardware landscape**: Leading meeting room vendors in 2026 include Logitech, Poly, Cisco, Yealink, Jabra, Crestron, Neat, HP, and Dell, each serving different room sizes and needs.
- **HP/Poly pushing managed ecosystem**: HP's 2026 Poly push centers on Studio Room Compute, VideoOS 5.0 (with 5.1 coming), Poly Studio V12, Poly Lens, Focus 6, and Mission headsets alongside existing CCX Teams phones.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- **Slack's AI push deepens Salesforce ties**: Slackbot can now listen to meetings on Zoom, Google Meet, or Slack Huddles via desktop audio, summarizing decisions and creating action items — and because it's connected to Salesforce, it can log those items directly into a CRM.
- **Webex leaning on AI-assisted supervision**: New Webex AI Quality Management tooling empowers supervisors with actionable insights and real-time coaching with visibility across both AI and human agents.
- **Zoom Rooms hardware parity with MTR**: Zoom Rooms Appliances are the default in 2026, running the same physical hardware as MTR-on-Android, just booted into Zoom mode, with five OEMs (Logitech, Poly, Neat, Yealink, DTEN) dominating the certified list.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- **Review federated chat policy timeline**: With the External Access Restrictions date now confirmed for August 14, IT/security teams should audit external collaboration configurations before enabling the new PowerShell controls.
- **Prepare for Facilitator governance conversations**: Even with the slip to September, start briefing stakeholders now on proactive AI meeting assistance and how to control/disable it for sensitive meetings.
- **Track the new in-meeting Report button rollout**: Worth testing internally this month as a frontline defense against deepfake/AI-driven meeting intrusions.
