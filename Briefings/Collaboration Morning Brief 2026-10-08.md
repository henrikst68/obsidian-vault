# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Thursday, 8 October 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Microsoft confirmed it is shifting "What's New in Teams" from a monthly to a quarterly publishing rhythm, with the next edition due in December, signalling a slower cadence of consolidated roadmap comms going forward.
- A new **Video Recap** feature turns a meeting you missed into a short, narrated highlight reel, so what a meeting decided outlasts the conversation itself.
- Admins get a new **Security Detection Report**: protection for everyday collaboration brings detections such as impersonation and malicious URLs into one view in the Teams admin center.
- Users should note that **Teams Live Chat is being retired** — the widget must be removed from websites by 5 October, with Microsoft pointing to Dynamics 365 Contact Center or Copilot Studio as replacements; separately, a sporadic sign-in/connectivity disruption was reported on Oct 6, per user outage trackers.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- **Copilot in SharePoint and OneDrive is now GA**: every user can now create and organize content, ask questions grounded in their SharePoint data, enrich libraries with AI-generated metadata, and build reusable skills.
- Admin action needed: by October 25 the new "advanced package uploads" setting will let users automatically upload advanced packages such as a declarative agent with actions or an MCP server — worth reviewing before it auto-enables.
- Billing shift ahead: usage-based billing will now be enabled by default for new Microsoft 365 Copilot Business subscriptions purchased through CSP, beginning December 1, 2026 (pushed back from the original Nov 2 date).
- Copilot Chat is getting a redesign: Copilot-suggested actions and curated work content will sit alongside chat so users can move to the next task without hunting for the right entry point, rolling out in October.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- **Teams Phone Agent** (AI receptionist) is now fully live: it begins rolling out to general availability as of September 30, 2026, scaling to serve customer requests during call-volume spikes and outside business hours, though the supervisor dashboard in the Queues app and custom Copilot Studio voice agents remain in preview.
- **Teams Rooms device management** is consolidating: Microsoft's Device Ecosystem Platform (MDEP) is becoming standard on all new Android devices certified for Teams, bringing Windows-grade management — hardware attestation, secure pairing, zero-touch deployment and remote manageability.
- Agentic admin controls expand: Teams admin center will recommend Teams agents for third-party apps already in use, with GitHub Copilot joining the chat, reaching GA in October 2026.

## 🖥️ Meeting Room Equipment
- Cisco continues to position itself as the fastest-growing MTR hardware ecosystem partner while simultaneously opening its devices to rivals: through a new partnership with Google, Cisco hardware will run fully featured Google Meet alongside Webex, Microsoft Teams and Zoom as part of RoomOS 27, due in November.
- Vendor comparison trend: Poly added Mission and Focus 6 headsets plus new room compute, while Yealink expanded its Teams room architecture, MP E2 phone roadmap, and YMCS integration with Microsoft Places.
- Certification watch: Microsoft's Teams certification for first-gen HP Poly Studio X30, X50, X70, G7500, TC8 and the original Neat Bar and Neat Board ended 3 September 2026 — Henrik's estate may need a hardware refresh review if running older Poly/Neat kit.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- **Cisco/WebexOne 2026**: Cisco unveiled a major agentic push — bringing AI agents into team conversations, connecting office technology with workplace management, and enabling customer-service agents to continue working after an interaction ends, plus Claude-powered collaborative agents in Webex chats, positioned as a rival to Slack's Claude Tag and Zoom's ZoomMate.
- **Google Workspace**: weekly update includes "skills"—reusable prompts that guide Gemini—rolling out to Workspace users, eventually replacing Gems, alongside upgraded AI voiceovers in Google Vids.
- **Zoom vs Webex competition**: UC Today reports Cisco already counts itself the fastest-growing Microsoft Teams Rooms ecosystem partner, with Zoom Rooms integration going live for general availability this month on Cisco hardware — intensifying multi-platform room device strategy across vendors.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
1. **Review the Copilot "advanced package uploads" setting before October 25** — it auto-enables user uploads of declarative agents/MCP servers unless policy is set.
2. **Audit any Poly/Neat/Logitech/Yealink devices that lost Teams certification on 3 September** — no forced cutover yet, but plan refresh budget.
3. **Watch Teams Phone Agent and third-party agent rollout in Teams admin center (GA in October)** — decide an enablement/governance policy before recommendations start surfacing to end users.
