# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Friday, 25 September 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Microsoft is finalising its external-bot crackdown: a new Teams meeting protection policy allows administrators to automatically block all identified external bots from joining Teams meetings. Targeted rollout is completing by late September, with General Availability worldwide beginning in early October 2026 and expected to complete by late October 2026.
- Device admin consolidation is nearing a deadline: management of Android-based Teams Rooms, Teams panels, and Teams phones is moving from the Teams admin center to the Pro Management portal, providing a consistent and secure management experience across devices and manufacturers, with the cutover landing by 30 September.
- Security teams should stay alert — recent campaigns include a previously unknown malware family dubbed SynkLoader being distributed in Microsoft Teams phishing campaigns to steal credentials via a fake lock screen, and threat actors impersonating IT support staff in Teams calls to deploy Chaos ransomware in attacks targeting North American organizations.
- Legacy hardware wind-down: Microsoft ended certification for Teams Rooms devices running Android 10 and 11 on September 3, 2026, affecting many first-generation room devices.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- Copilot in Teams has moved to GA with a cleaner look: Microsoft Teams' Copilot experience is updated with a simplified, chat-centered design available in full-page and side pane views starting September 2026, with easier navigation, improved chat layout, Work IQ controls, and a new Tasks view in full-screen mode.
- Meeting AI is getting more proactive: Facilitator can now act without being prompted — running a Copilot web search mid-meeting when a factual question goes unanswered — and outside agents like GitHub Copilot are getting an official route into Teams through the admin centre.
- Copilot Cowork went GA worldwide with fresh capability: Microsoft Copilot adds Copilot Cowork worldwide in general availability, bringing a full agentic experience for long-running, multi-tool work with new plugins, Edge web browsing, and stronger security, compliance, and cost management controls. Separately, OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra joined the frontier models available in Copilot Cowork and Copilot Studio, rolling out from 4 September.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- Teams devices got a rebrand: Microsoft is now referring to Rooms, desks, panels and phones collectively as "intelligent spaces," alongside a rollout of passwordless Entra resource accounts for Windows and Android Rooms devices, panels and phones.
- Nine new partner plugins landed in Cowork, including Enosix, Harvey, LSEG, Miro, monday.com, Moodys, Morningstar, S&P Global Energy, and TeamsMaestro.
- Google is smoothing cross-platform meeting joins: improvements to Google Calendar make it easier to join third-party video meetings, including Microsoft Teams, Zoom, and Cisco Webex, when collaborating across different calendar and email clients.

## Meetingroom Equipment
- New camera controls are rolling out: local PTZ camera control on Rooms consoles — pan, tilt and zoom from the Teams Rooms on Windows console — enters public preview in early September with GA late September to mid-October.
- The Android 10/11 certification lapse means IT teams managing first-generation HP Poly, Logitech and Yealink models have roughly a year of support left before forced replacement.
- Certified hardware ecosystem continues broadening across Yealink, AudioCodes, Poly, Neat, MAXHUB, and Biamp
