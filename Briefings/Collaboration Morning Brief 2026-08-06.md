# 🗓️ Collaboration Morning Brief — Thursday, 6 August 2026

## 🔵 Microsoft Teams
- Microsoft has confirmed a new **"Report a concern"** feature rolling out through August 2026, letting participants flag suspicious activity mid-meeting so IT/security teams can intervene immediately; the report will also be made available through the Microsoft Defender portal and Teams admin center, where security teams can investigate potential deepfakes or other fraudulent activity.
- The feature is a direct response to rising AI-driven meeting fraud — Microsoft argues the feature is necessary in 2026 due to rising impersonation, scams, and suspicious behavior in online meetings, and warns that AI tools have lowered the technical barrier for fraud and cybercrime, particularly during online meetings.
- Alongside this, Microsoft is rolling out other improvements including a new meeting design that reduces accidental screen shares and meeting exits.
- Admins should note recent Teams admin changes: management of Android-based Teams Rooms, Teams panels, and Teams phones is moving from the Teams admin center to the Pro Management portal, providing a consistent and secure management experience across devices and manufacturers.

## 🤖 Copilot & AI in Teams
- Copilot responses are getting richer: Copilot now displays rich images from files and meetings directly within responses to improve comprehension, surfacing relevant images inline from files and meetings for visual context.
- Governance push continues — Teams' new Organization Trust Score lets IT admins move from manually reviewing trust data for apps and agents to a scalable, automated evaluation process.
- Watermarking for transparency is rolling out: to provide additional transparency about what content has been generated or altered using AI in Microsoft 365, watermarks can now be added to videos and audio content.

## 🔗 Ecosystem & Integrations
- Meeting Recap is being consolidated: Teams has added a Recap app that centralizes past meeting recaps with filters, Audio Recap, and 30 days of history.
- Interpreter improvements landed: Teams improved AI Interpreter's Simultaneous mode with matching audio and live caption languages and new voice options, while admins can now fully disable voice simulation.
- SharePoint/Copilot targeting is sharper: users can now target Copilot Chat prompts to specific SharePoint Lists, reducing irrelevant results, so teams can collaborate more effectively by referencing exact lists in Copilot Chat.

## 🖥️ Meeting Room Equipment
- Hardware rivalry heats up: Poly added Mission and Focus 6 headsets plus new room compute, while Yealink expanded its Teams room architecture, MP E2 phone roadmap, and YMCS integration with Microsoft Places.
- Yealink's room breadth is notable — its portfolio stretches across MeetingBar, MVC S80, MVC S98, SmartVision 80, MeetingBoard Pro, MTower, Teams-certified phones, Teams-certified headsets, and ProAV-style room builds.
- Poly (HP) is consolidating its managed estate around Studio Room Compute, VideoOS 5.0 and the forthcoming 5.1 release, Poly Studio V12, Poly Lens, WXP, Focus 6, Mission headsets, and its existing CCX Teams phones.

## 🌐 Broader Collaboration Landscape
- Google Meet is scaling AI note-taking: over 110 million attendees have used Take Notes For Me in the last month (8.5x YoY growth), and Google is expanding the feature so it can capture summaries and action items for any meeting — even ones hosted on Zoom or Teams.
- Zoom patched a critical flaw: CVE-2026-53412 (CVSS 9.8) affects Zoom Workplace for Windows before version 7.0.0 and Zoom Workplace VDI Client for Windows, alongside three high-severity privilege-escalation bugs — no known active exploitation.
- Slack's latest release deepens Salesforce ties: Slack shipped a productivity-focused release adding Slackbot-powered slide and dashboard creation, mobile Salesforce updates, scheduled status, a merged Omniswitcher, smarter canvases, temporary channel joins, and Workflow Builder scale upgrades.

## ⚡ Action Items & Things to Watch
- Review the new Teams **"Report a concern"** rollout and brief security/IT teams on the Defender portal integration ahead of full August availability.
- Ensure Windows/VDI Zoom clients are patched against CVE-2026-53412 if any part of the org still uses Zoom alongside Teams.
- Track the Teams Rooms device management migration to the Pro Management portal — plan admin workflow changes before Android-based rooms/panels/phones transition completes.
