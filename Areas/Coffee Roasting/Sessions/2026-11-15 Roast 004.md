**Tasting result (day 9, 93 °C):** 1:2 best balance. No distinct notes at any ratio → limit is the roast, not the brew. Possible cause (hypothesis): in 003 and 004 first crack started slowly and was never popcorn-like, and weight loss runs ~2 % above what the colour suggests → low heat momentum / long, "baked" profile → flat cup. Next roast: change heat before first crack (more energy into FC), not only the drop time. Optional brew test: 1:2 at 92 °C.
