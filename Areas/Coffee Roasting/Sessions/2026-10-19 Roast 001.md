## 6. Conclusion and next roast
- **Main problems:** (1) uneven heat across the batch, (2) development far too long (5 min, DTR ~36 %).
- **Basket:** Weber oval mesh basket with internal mixer wings, so tumbling should be adequate.
- **Likely causes of unevenness:** strong radiant heat from the rear infrared burner hitting the beans on the back side of each turn; thin bean layer (250 g) gives little buffering; unripe beans (pale ones).
- **Next roast — change only this:** rear IR burner off, main burners on (convective heat), lid closed, 230–250 °C at basket height. Keep 250 g so the result is comparable.
- **Roast 003:** shorten development to ~2:00 after first crack.
- Before tasting: pick out the very light and the black beans.
