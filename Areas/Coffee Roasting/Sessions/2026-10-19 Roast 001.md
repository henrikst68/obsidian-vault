## 6. Conclusion and next roast
- **Main problems:** (1) uneven heat across the batch, (2) development far too long (5 min, DTR ~36 %).
- **Likely causes of unevenness:** direct flame under the drum, drum too full so beans do not tumble along the full length, mixed bean sizes, unripe beans.
- **Next roast — change only this:** fix the evenness first (indirect heat: burner directly under the drum off or low, outer burners on). Keep batch size the same so the result is comparable.
- **Roast 003:** shorten development to ~2:00 after first crack.
- Before tasting: pick out the very light and the black beans.
