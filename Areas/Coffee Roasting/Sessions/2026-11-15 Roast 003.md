### Dial-in 2026-11-20 (5 days post-roast), SES995 warm (~1 h), 93 °C, Auto Dial-In off, manual mode with bloom
Reference: Black Coffee Supreme ran well at grind 21 the same evening.

| Time | Grind | Dose (g) | Out (g) | Time (s) | Note |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 20:02 | 19 | 20.8 | — | — | Only spurts, massive channeling. Too fine |

| 20:22 | 30 | 21.0 | 56.2 | 25 | 2.25 g/s, 1:2.68. Spurting (channeling), thin. ~80 g beans left |

| 20:27 | 28 | 22.1 | 42.8 | 38 | 1.13 g/s, 1:1.94. Less spurting, quite foamy. Fuller taste but astringent. ~60 g left |

Ratio result: 1:2 is the best of the three.

**Conclusion 2026-11-20 (day 5):** beans still degassing — foam, spurting/channeling and astringency from uneven extraction. Settings close (grind 28–29, 1:2, ~22 g). Spent puck after 20:27: clear shower-screen and screw imprint, very firm surface, some grounds on the basket wall → puck swelled and was pressed hard against the screen during bloom (gas + ~22 g dose, little headspace), adding to uneven flow. If it persists after resting, lower dose ~1–1.5 g via tamp screw. Stopped to save beans. Retest at day 8–10 (2026-11-23 to 25) at grind 29, 1:2; expect a slightly finer grind once gas is lower.

Finding: these beans need a much coarser grind than the Supreme (27+ vs 21), not finer. Fresh CO₂ adds resistance and raises the dose.
